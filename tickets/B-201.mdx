**Brief:** the owner, 2026-11-09 (B-191): "**Sync** `/sync` — the mirror's view: head,
observations, wanted, and Discrepancies (where git and Baton disagreed)."

**Spec:** [BATON_SPEC §4.3](../spec/BATON_SPEC/04-tickets-syncs-board.md#sec-4-3) ([R-BTN-11](../spec/BATON_SPEC/04-tickets-syncs-board.md#sec-4-3):
heads by compare-and-set, the forced resync; [R-BTN-62](../spec/BATON_SPEC/04-tickets-syncs-board.md#sec-4-3):
observations), [§4.7](../spec/BATON_SPEC/04-tickets-syncs-board.md#sec-4-7) ([R-BTN-85](../spec/BATON_SPEC/04-tickets-syncs-board.md#sec-4-7):
`{sub, baton, git, observation}`), [§3.3](../spec/BATON_SPEC/03-repositories-and-forges.md#sec-3-3)
([R-BTN-94](../spec/BATON_SPEC/03-repositories-and-forges.md#sec-3-3): wants and dispatch),
[§16.1](../spec/BATON_SPEC/16-adoption.md#sec-16-1) ([R-BTN-28](../spec/BATON_SPEC/16-adoption.md#sec-16-1):
cutover and adoptions), [§11.4](../spec/BATON_SPEC/11-people.md#sec-11-4) ([R-BTN-155](../spec/BATON_SPEC/11-people.md#sec-11-4):
a forced resync is confirmed, a resync is not), [§14](../spec/BATON_SPEC/14-api.md#sec-14) (`POST
/v1/ui/resync`; `/v1/ui/board/accept` has no row).

**Depends on:** B-192 (sessions on the repository reads; the registry), B-205 (the window and
response conventions).

**Used by:** B-220.

### What to build

The §14 rows first, then the code.

1. **`GET /v1/repositories/{repository}/sync`** (`repo.sync`, read; the repository reads'
   principals and a session): `head: {sha, parent_sha, received_at, ticket_count, observation,
   source}` (B-183's source declaration), `observation: {last, outstanding: {number, age_s} |
   null}` (R-BTN-62), `last_post: {at, sha, accepted, code?}`, `wants: [{role: sync | mirror,
   since, last_dispatch_at, adapter}]` (R-BTN-94), `held: [{sha, parent_sha, ticket_count,
   received_at, auto_accept_at}]` (today's `uiState().held`), `mode: {mode: shadow | cut-over,
   cutover_at}` (R-BTN-28), `adoptions: [{claim, ticket, runner_id, hostname, session_name,
   requested_at}]` — present for an administrator only — and `discrepancies: [{sub, ticket,
   baton, git, observation, at}]` for `?since=` (7 days default, 30 at most), R-BTN-85's stored
   fields, from `history.view`. From `observations.ts`, `history.ts`, `cutover.ts` and the
   held-posts query.
2. **`POST /v1/ui/resync`** (`repo.resync`): the §14 row exists and no code serves it; build it —
   a session of either role on its repositories (B-76's owner-session rule: its repositories
   only), `{repository, force?, sha?}`; without `force` it dispatches the sync (as a webhook
   does) and answers `{ok: true}`; with `force` and `sha` an administrator's session only,
   `202 {confirm_code}` and on confirmation the head reset to `sha` (R-BTN-11). `admin.repo.resync`
   stays the key's route. `ui.view` on `repository/sync`.
3. **`POST /v1/ui/board/accept`** (`repo.accept-post`, Decision 5a): the route exists
   (`baton.ts`); give it its §14 row — a session of either role on its repositories,
   `{repository, sha}`, accepts the held post with that sha, `{ok: true}`, unconfirmed (it
   accepts a post the sync already made; R-BTN-155 does not list it) — and `ui.view` on
   `repository/sync`.

### What not to do

- No screen (B-220); no sync logic beyond what exists: ancestry and acceptance are the
  server's (R-BTN-11).
- No "Accept history" action (R-BTN-85); no board post from a session.

### Trust

- **`repo.sync`** — Baton's rows; `baton` and `git` are text (§12.3); shas and kinds validated
  identifiers; `adoptions` to an administrator only.
- **Resync and Accept** — a session on its repositories, through `authorize`; a resync is a
  dispatch Baton already performs on a webhook, an accept takes a post the sync made.
- **Forced resync** — an administrator's session, confirmed by the minting key (R-BTN-155);
  `sha` validated on the server.

### Invariants

- **Heads advance by compare-and-set** (R-BTN-11); the forced resync is the exception and is
  confirmed. **Every disagreement is logged** (R-BTN-85).
- **R-BTN-155's confirmed list**: forced resync through the code; resync and accept not.
- **An owner's session, its repositories**; **both hosts** (R-BTN-55).

### Tests

Scenarios numbered at merge, on both hosts, with a repository in shadow holding an outstanding
observation, a held post, a refused post (`head-moved`), a want, a pending adoption and two
discrepancies: `repo.sync` returns each section with R-BTN-85's field names, `adoptions` absent
for an owner's session, `?since=` bounding discrepancies; `POST /v1/ui/resync` dispatches for an
owner's session on its repository, refuses it `force` and another repository, and answers `202`
to an administrator's with `force` and `sha`; `board/accept` accepts the held post for an owner's
session.
