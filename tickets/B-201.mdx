**Brief:** the owner, 2026-11-09 (B-191): "**Sync** `/sync` — the mirror's view: head,
observations, wanted, and Discrepancies (where git and Baton disagreed)." The mockup's
Discrepancies table (kind, ticket, Baton had struck through, git showed, age) and its SHADOW
banner with "Cut docs over…" are this screen's rows; its "Accept history" button is not built
(R-BTN-85 adopts git's value at once — B-191's drop list) and its "Confirm adoption" is.

**Spec:** [BATON_SPEC §11.4](../spec/BATON_SPEC/11-people.md#sec-11-4) (the Sync screen, carrying
the retired Discrepancies view; [R-BTN-155](../spec/BATON_SPEC/11-people.md#sec-11-4): a forced
resync, cut over and an adoption confirm are confirmed, a resync is not),
[§4.3](../spec/BATON_SPEC/04-tickets-syncs-board.md#sec-4-3) ([R-BTN-11](../spec/BATON_SPEC/04-tickets-syncs-board.md#sec-4-3):
heads by compare-and-set, the forced resync; [R-BTN-62](../spec/BATON_SPEC/04-tickets-syncs-board.md#sec-4-3):
observations), [§4.7](../spec/BATON_SPEC/04-tickets-syncs-board.md#sec-4-7) ([R-BTN-85](../spec/BATON_SPEC/04-tickets-syncs-board.md#sec-4-7):

### What to build

The §14 rows first, then the code.

1. **`GET /v1/repositories/{repository}/sync`** (`repo.sync`, read; the repository reads'
   principals and a session): `head: {sha, parent_sha, received_at, ticket_count, observation,
   source}` (B-183's source declaration), `observation: {last, outstanding: {number, age_s} |
   null}` (R-BTN-62), `last_post: {at, sha, accepted, code?}`, `wants: [{role: sync | mirror,
   since, last_dispatch_at, adapter}]` (R-BTN-94), `held: [{sha, parent_sha, ticket_count,
   received_at, auto_accept_at}]` (today's `uiState().held`), `mode: {mode: shadow | cut-over,
   cutover_at}` (R-BTN-28), `adoptions: [{claim, ticket, runner_id, hostname, session_name,
   requested_at}]` — present for an administrator only — and `discrepancies: [{sub, ticket,
   baton, git, observation, at}]` for `?since=` (7 days default, 30 at most), R-BTN-85's
   stored fields. From `observations.ts`, `history.ts`, `cutover.ts` and the held-posts query.
2. **`POST /v1/ui/resync`** (`repo.resync`): the §14 row exists and the route does not; build
   it — a session of either role on its repositories, `{repository, force?, sha?}`; without
   `force` it dispatches the sync and answers `{ok: true}`; with `force` and `sha` an
   administrator's session only, `202 {confirm_code}` and on confirmation the head reset to
   `sha` (R-BTN-11). `admin.repo.resync` stays the key's route.
3. **`POST /v1/ui/board/accept`** (`repo.accept-post`, Decision 5a): the route exists; give it
   its §14 row — a session of either role on its repositories, `{repository, sha}`, accepts the
   held post with that sha, `{ok: true}`, unconfirmed (R-BTN-155 does not list it; it accepts a
   post the sync already made) — and its `ui.view` on `repository/sync`.
4. **`/repositories/{r}/sync`** (`src/dashboard/screens/sync/`), in this order: the mode banner
   (the mockup's `SHADOW` line; **Cut over** for an administrator's session by `ui.view`, the
   code dialog); the head line — sha linking the forge commit through `web_base` (R-BTN-92),
   age, ticket count, source kind — with **Resync** (one click) and, for an administrator's
   session, **Force resync onto …** (a `sha` field; the code dialog); the observation counter
   with an outstanding number's age (marked past 10 minutes); the last post and its refusal
   spelt out as text; wants and dispatches; held posts with their auto-accept time and
   **Accept** (one click); pending adoptions with **Confirm adoption** (an administrator's; the
   code dialog, naming the requesting runner); and **Discrepancies** as the mockup's `<table>`
   — kind (`sub`), ticket linking its page's history section, "Baton had" (`baton`, drawn
   struck through as a style on a text node), "Git showed" (`git`), observation, age — with
   `?since=` as URL query.
5. **Counts.** The sidebar's Sync entry shows the window's discrepancy count and marks an
   outstanding observation older than 10 minutes.
6. **Phone.** The banner, head line and Resync fit 375 px; the table collapses to cards.

### What not to do

- No sync logic in the client: ancestry and acceptance are the server's (R-BTN-11).
- No "Accept history" (R-BTN-85); no board post from a session — a resync is a dispatch.
- No duplicate of the forced resync elsewhere: B-214's registry screen names
  `baton admin repo resync` as text and draws no control.

### Trust

- **`repo.sync`** — Baton's rows; `baton` and `git` are text (§12.3) through `Text`; shas and
  kinds validated identifiers; the forge link from `web_base` only; `adoptions` to an
  administrator only.
- **Resync and Accept** — a session on its repositories, through `authorize`; a resync is a
  dispatch Baton already performs on a webhook, an accept takes a post the sync made.
- **Forced resync, cut over, adoption confirm** — an administrator's session, confirmed by the
  minting key (R-BTN-155); `sha` validated on the server.

### Invariants

- **Heads advance by compare-and-set** (R-BTN-11); the forced resync is the exception and is
  confirmed.
- **Every disagreement is logged and shown** (R-BTN-85).
- **R-BTN-155's confirmed list**: forced resync, cut over and the adoption confirm through the
  code; resync and accept not.
- **Text, never markup** (§12.3); **an owner's session, its repositories** (R-BTN-155).

### Tests

Scenarios numbered at merge, on both hosts, with a repository in shadow holding an outstanding
observation, a held post, a refused post (`head-moved`), a want, a pending adoption and two
discrepancies: `repo.sync` returns each section with R-BTN-85's field names, `adoptions` absent
for an owner's session, `?since=` bounding discrepancies; `POST /v1/ui/resync` dispatches for an
owner's session and refuses `force` to it, answers `202` to an administrator's; `board/accept`
accepts the held post for an owner's session. Under `test/ui/`: Resync and Accept send with no
dialog; Force resync, Cut over and Confirm adoption draw only for an administrator and open the
code dialog; the table links each ticket; a hostile `git` value is text.
