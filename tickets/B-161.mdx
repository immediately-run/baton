**Brief:** the owner, 2026-11-01, on the whole-spec review they commissioned ("assess it for …
can any vocabulary, core concepts or actors be simplified … is the user experience intuitive …
are the security guarantees … truly guaranteed and … do they matter"), asked for its findings
to be filed as tickets. Source: `docs/review/spec-simplification-review-2026-11-01.mdx`.
The review's claims are a reviewer's, checked against the spec where they cite it; this ticket
verifies each against the text before changing anything.

### Why

Review finding 5 and section 1.2: R-BTN-116, R-BTN-78 (whose decision also raises
`hitl.decision`, so Needs you lists one question twice), R-BTN-160/161, R-BTN-173 (~150 lines,
two outbox kinds, `baton_held`, a Quint model) and R-BTN-163. Light questions exist to save one
git commit per question.

### Decide first (the owner)

Retiring light questions (R-BTN-173), which the Decisions section chose over a ticket file per question.

### What to build

1. **A spec PR first**, under the babysit skill's *Spec PRs*: two fresh reviewers, the impact list,
   every rule restating the same fact changed with it.
2. Person legs as the one primitive; posted decisions become a ticket source's way to declare
   one; light questions retired with their outbox kinds and model; `baton.*` conditions keep
   the resolve page.
3. Tickets building the retired parts rewritten or superseded with a map.
