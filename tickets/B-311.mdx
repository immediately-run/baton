**Brief:** the owner, 2026-11-21:
- "Baton should handle work performed outside of baton gracefully: If it is possible to determine
  that a ticket has been completed in a separate session, baton should update its board accordingly
  … This should be performed by the sync github action code, not the baton server".
- "baton *must* work well in a setup where only a portion of the organization is using baton".
- "the codegen leg is not the only leg which may be completed outside baton".
- "Baton's whole strategy is explicitly modeling processes that have been traditionally implicit
  via the coarse graph. This only works if transitions between stations that happen outside Baton
  are also modeled. So the reconciler legs need to be part of the course".

After two adversarial reviews, the owner chose:
- hand acts as separate fact legs;
- mentions filtered, rather than an explicit trailer;
- a hand merge closing only where the course allows.

The case that started it: B-166 was delivered on 2026-11-02 by PRs #158, #159, #163 and #164, never
claimed or closed, and cost a runner's implement leg on 2026-11-21.

- The open-world stance: Baton cannot prevent moves outside it, only detect them and place the
  ticket; a factory that needs strictness gates the forge (values #13, the owner, 2026-11-21).

## Plan

### Core (`src/core/`)

1. **`arrives` and `reconciles` in the course file.** Accept both in `validateCourse`
   (`src/core/course.ts`: the `LEG_KEYS` allowlist ≈66, which refuses `arrives` today; the
   comment in the `autonomous` check ≈376).
   - A fact leg (`arrives: {fact, verdict?, next?}`) has exactly one outcome, is never
     `kind: person`, never `start_leg` and never another leg's `next`. Its `next` leaves its
     outcome; on a `pr-verdict` leg it may be `{approve, changes}`.
   - A reconcile leg (`reconciles: {to: {station: next | null}}`) declares no `to`. Its outcomes
     are its `from` and the keys of `reconciles.to`, and it has `max_runs`. Each `next` leaves its
     station (`null` only at an end). At most one leaves a station, and it is never `start_leg` or
     a `next`.
   - R-BTN-39's default next skips fact and reconcile legs.

   Raise the standard courses' `min_runner_version`, since older runners and servers refuse
   either field, and deploy the server before any repository adopts the new course versions.
2. **Applying `std:arrival@1`.** Today `src/core/questions.ts` ≈195 records every type but
   `std:decision@1` as `arrival-unapplied`. Build the apply path R-BTN-39 states:
   - the board came from a sync key or the CI identity (on a machine-key board these attestations
     are dropped and the board accepted), and the policy admits the writer;
   - a fact leg leaves Baton's station;
   - `reached` is its outcome;
   - no claim is active and nothing holds the ticket (otherwise defer).

   Apply once per attestation `ref`, queue `next` (per verdict on a `pr-verdict` leg), and log
   `fact-arrival`. A hand arrival counts as a run of the fact leg itself (R-BTN-63). A sync
   arrival meeting a claim or hold is recorded `arrival-unapplied` and dropped, not deferred: the
   next sync posts it again if its fact still holds. A sync arrival on a fact leg with no runs left
   is kept as `fact` evidence. An arrival refused only because the policy admits
   no `sync` writer is kept as evidence (`fact`, or `merged-early` for `pr-merged`).
3. **`std:evidence@1` and the reconcile leg.**
   - A new numbered migration (append-only) for `ticket_evidence` and `reconcile_displaced` (§13).
   - Keep each admitted piece of evidence once per `ref`. A policy stored before the type existed
     admits `sync` for it (R-BTN-188).
   - In every transaction that changes a ticket's station, queued leg, claim, hold or evidence,
     apply R-BTN-39's rule: queue the reconcile leg leaving the station (runs left; never in place
     of a person, suspended, routed, exhausted or expired leg), and record the displaced leg with
     its station. A reconcile leg already queued at that station keeps the record. Any station
     change the reconcile leg's own finish didn't make clears it. Log `reconcile-queued`.
   - Accept as its finishing position only `unchanged` (the station it left, `next` = the
     displaced leg) or a `reconciles.to` station with its mapped `next`. That position marks the claim's evidence
     weighed and clears the record. A claim ending any other way leaves both.
   - Evidence the rule can't hand on waits, and the ticket read lists it (§14 `evidence`). The
     attestations read lists fact arrivals and evidence.
4. **The claim answer** (§9.1) for a reconcile leg carries `evidence: [{ref, kind}]` and
   `displaced`. The observation (§4.3) serves the sync `runner_pushers` and `verdict_refs`. A
   review leg's finishing position names its verdict comment's URL as `ref` (R-BTN-83); the
   standard review stubs must report it.

### Sync (`src/defaults/`)

5. **Facts.** Extend `PrFact` (`src/defaults/course-pr.ts` ≈15) with the merge commit and the
   title and body, which are read and never posted. Read PRs for every course's live tickets
   (R-BTN-96 as amended). Note that `BATON_PR_AUTHOR` (`cli.ts` ≈1186) narrows the PR list to one
   author, which defeats this; it must not apply to the evidence read.
6. **Arrivals.** In the CLI, for every ticket whatever its station function, post an
   `std:arrival@1` as `sync` for each fact a fact leg leaving Baton's station takes, with the ref
   and the leg's `next`. Extend `StationsResult` and `PostedStation` so a post can carry them (they
   carry no attestations today), and pass `ctx.positions` (`StationCtx` doesn't carry it). A
   machine-key sync posts none.
   - No `pr-opened`, `pr-verdict` or `pr-pushed` while `runner_pushers` is NULL or `[]`.
   - `pr-opened`: a PR whose head carries no claim's trailer, and not authored by a
     `runner_pushers` login.
   - `pr-verdict`: ignore `runner_pushers` logins, the PR's author, the head's pushers and the
     `verdict_refs` Baton served. Honour `arrives.verdict`. No condition on the test gate (open
     world, values #13).
   - `pr-pushed`: after the latest changes verdict (none without one), by no `runner_pushers` login,
     with no claim trailer, changing more than the merged-in base.
   - `pr-merged`: at least one PR of the ticket's, every one merged, none open, none by a `merge`
     leg.
7. **Evidence.**
   - Post `merged-early` for a `pr-merged` fact that no fact leg can take.
   - Post `mention` for pushers' PRs (home and `repos:`) merged between the times Baton accepted
     the last head and handed out this observation, and for the home's first-parent commits
     between those heads, that name the id as a whole word, with every exclusion R-BTN-203 lists.
   - `baton repo sync --backfill-mentions <since>` widens one run's window, so outside work from
     before the feature (B-166's kind) can be found once.

### Courses

8. **Fact legs and reconcile legs** in `create-pr`, `review-pr` (`approved-by-hand`, with
   `verdict: approve`) and `create-spec` (`opened-by-hand`, `pushed-by-hand`, `merged-by-hand`)
   (`src/defaults/course-pr.ts`, `src/defaults/courses.ts` and the course files), as
   `docs/standard-courses.mdx`'s two tables list, with the course versions raised (R-BTN-22).
   - Each reconcile leg's stub reads the answer the page gives:
     - `unchanged` (next = the displaced leg from the claim);
     - `at <station>` (its `reconciles.to` stations, the one it left included, with the mapped
       next), where a merge or verdict it names is checked as the fact leg's would be, and an
       unchecked `at merged` fails;
     - on `create-pr`, `delivered <sha|PR,…>`, checked on the default branch as B-257's
       `delivered` is. Every
     reconcile leg and every cyclic fact leg has `max_runs`, so `baton test-course` passes the
     cycle rule.
   - Its instructions file (`legs/reconcile.md`) says what the agent reads and what it may not
     decide.

### Checks and tests

9. **`baton ticket check`** reports, never as a finding, each ticket that is done on the board but
   whose file is live.
10. **Tests:**
    - S-BTN-309's cases, in one test file naming the row.
    - A `baton test-course` run for each changed course.
    - B-166's shape as a fixture: four merged PRs naming it in titles, none in `prs:`. It gets
      `mention` evidence and its `reconcile` leg is queued at `filed`; `delivered` leads to
      `confirm`, and `unchanged` re-queues `implement`.
    - A `create-pr` ticket at `pr-open` whose PR is merged by hand arrives at `merged` by
      `merged-by-hand` (open world: no gate or review required).
    - The same ticket at `reviewed`, with a person's approval and then a hand merge, ends at
      `merged`.
    - A repository whose course narrows `merged-by-hand` to `reviewed` gets `merged-early`
      evidence for a merge at `pr-open`, and its reconcile leg is queued.

### Trust

New inputs, each read by the sync and judged by Baton:
- **The forge's PR states, merges, verdicts and pushes** move a ticket only through a fact leg its
  own course file places (open world, values #13: Baton records what happened). They need a board
  posted with the sync key or the CI identity, never a runner's machine key, and a policy whose
  `attestations` admit `sync` for `std:arrival@1` (`cooperative` does). Under `protected` the
  refused arrival becomes evidence. No PR, verdict or push counts as a hand act while runner
  identities are unknown, and runners' own acts are excluded once they are known.
- **PR titles and bodies, and commit messages**, are read as text to find an id and never
  posted. A `mention` is evidence for the reconcile leg. The agent may conclude where the evidence
  shows the ticket, `merged` included, but its stub verifies any merge or verdict it names against
  the forge as the fact leg would. A mention alone, with no such merge, can't close the ticket
  except through `delivered` and a person's `confirm`.
- **`std:evidence@1`** is admitted for `sync` under both presets.
- **The reconcile leg's `reconciles.to`** comes from the repository's own course file.

### Invariants

- **The sync finds, the core places (R-BTN-35, R-BTN-39, R-BTN-50).** The sync never sets a
  position. Baton applies arrivals and reconcile legs' conclusions along edges of the ticket's own
  course.
- **A claimed or held ticket is untouched.** Arrivals and queueing wait for the claim or hold to
  end. Archive and `status: done` close as before. Pinned, person and suspended legs are never
  displaced.
- **A partial reading is never posted (R-BTN-96).**
- **Filter fields only (R-BTN-33).** Evidence is a `ref` and a `kind`; no title or message is
  stored.
- **Migrations are append-only.**
