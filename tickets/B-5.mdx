**Brief:** the owner, 2026-10-16 and 2026-10-17, as specified by B-1 (baton#43).
**Spec:** `spec/BATON_SPEC.md` §2m.3 (R-BTN-114, R-BTN-115), §2m.7a's courier, notice and check-runner rows, R-BTN-66 and R-BTN-103 as amended.

### Why

§2m is agreed and specified; this ticket builds its part of it.

### What to build

1. **Check tickets** from the `changes` ticket source, `check.<course>.<number>`, superseded by a push; claimable only by a check-runner key, and only in `session` isolation.
2. **The notice.** Queued only after a sync confirms the end and its entry; conclusion from the owners' `checks` setting; signed (Ed25519, `BATON_SIGNING_KEY`, `kid`); retired keys still listed.
3. **The forge courier.** Its own credential kind, never a machine key, never the pool; `?delivers=forge-check`; `check_id` on `sent`; an unsent notice listed after 15 minutes.
4. **Read-back.** The sync reads the check runs; a run Baton did not issue is logged `check-mismatch`.
5. **Tenancy** *(added 2026-10-18 by BATON_SPEC §3b, R-BTN-133)*: the notice also carries and signs `origin` and `forge_repo_id` (null while the repository has no ids); `GET /v1/keys/signing` returns `{keys: [{kid, alg, public_key, retired_at?}]}`; the forge courier refuses a notice whose `origin` is not its configured origin or whose `forge_repo_id` is not the target repository's id.

6. **Hardening** *(added 2026-10-18 by BATON_SPEC §10.7, R-BTN-150)*: a check ticket's head is the core field `check: {sha, head_repo_id}`, a fork's dropped and every check dropped while the repository has no ids; a claim binds `check_sha` and refuses an `advance` without it (`head-moved`); a concluding notice only for a confirmed `done` of an ordinary check-runner claim, mapped from the end's own `reached`; the verdict leg runs `check_command` first through the provider's `exec` in a fresh sandbox, and a success end carries `exit_status: 0`; an agent reaches only failure or neutral stations; never open-world; `courier add --delivers forge-check` refuses an App installed on others' repositories; S-BTN-118, G-BTN-138.

### Do not

- Let the course file's `checks` set conclusions: they are the owners'.
