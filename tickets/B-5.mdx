After the adversarial review (`spec/BATON_SPEC_HISTORY.md`, "Adversarial review (2026-10-20)"):
R-BTN-114 and §12.1 (adv-3 F5, F6), R-BTN-153 (F4), R-BTN-115 (F7), R-BTN-10 and R-BTN-168's

### Why

R-BTN-114, R-BTN-115 and R-BTN-153 are specified; this ticket builds them.

### What to build

1. **Check tickets** from the `changes` ticket source, `check.<course>.<number>`; a push that
   changes `check.sha` supersedes every claim on the ticket (released `superseded`, R-BTN-168;
   B-65 adds that reason) and restarts it at its start station. Claimable only by a
   check-runner key. Check legs require `runner:session` (R-BTN-107): no `process` or
   `attached` runner takes one (rule 4). Whichever of B-5 and B-70 lands second wires that
   requirement into the check courses.
2. **The notice.** Queued only after a sync confirms the end and its entry; conclusion from the
   owners' `checks` setting; signed (Ed25519, `BATON_SIGNING_KEY`, `kid`); retired keys still
   listed. `forge-check` is a kind in today's `notices` (`src/core/notices.ts`); B-67 later drops
   every other kind.
3. **The forge courier.** Its own credential kind (`courier`), never a machine key;
   `?delivers=forge-check`; `check_id` on `sent`; a notice not sent within 15 minutes raises
   `baton.forge-check-late` (through today's listing if B-57 has not landed).
4. **Read-back.** The sync reads the check runs; a run Baton did not issue is the discrepancy
   `check-mismatch` (R-BTN-85) and raises `baton.check-mismatch`.
5. **Tenancy** *(added 2026-10-18, §2.2 R-BTN-136)*: the notice also carries and signs `origin`
   and `forge_repo_id` (null while the repository has no ids); `GET /v1/keys/signing` returns
   `{keys: [{kid, alg, public_key, retired_at?}]}`; the forge courier refuses a notice whose
   `origin` is not its configured origin or whose `forge_repo_id` is not the target repository's
   id.

6. **Hardening** *(added 2026-10-18, §10 R-BTN-153)*: a check ticket's head is the core field
   `check: {sha, head_repo_id, head_ref}`, and the claim response carries `check_sha` and
   `check_head_ref`, a fork's dropped and every check dropped while the repository has no ids; a
   claim binds `check_sha` and refuses an `advance` without it (`head-moved`); a concluding
   notice only for a confirmed `done` of an ordinary check-runner claim, mapped from the end's
   own `reached`, anything else raising `baton.check-unattested`; the verdict leg runs
   `check_command` first through the session provider's `exec` in a fresh sandbox at
   `check_sha`, after checking `check_sha` is `check.head_ref`'s head (else
   `released:stale-board`, with a sync want); add `exec` to the Claude session provider, without
   which no check passes on any runner, and an `advance` or end reaching a success station
   carries `exit_status: 0`; `process` and `attached` runners take no verdict leg (rule 4's
   `missing-capability` on `runner:session`); an agent reaches only failure or neutral stations;
   never open-world; `courier add --delivers forge-check` refuses an App installed on others'
   repositories or on "All repositories"; S-BTN-123, G-BTN-143.
7. **The adversarial review's fixes** (2026-10-20):
   - **The `check_runners` row is the grant** (R-BTN-114, adv-3 F5). `baton owner check-runner
     add <key> --name <hostname> [--capabilities …] [--max-tier <t>]` stores them in
     `check_runners`. Rule 7 reads that row for a check ticket, rule 4 its `capabilities` and
     rule 5 its `max_tier` (`src/core/gates.ts`). The hostname binds at `add` and is the name the
     key heartbeats under (R-BTN-3). A check-runner holds no other grant, and `not-eligible`
     names the rule `check-runner`.
   - **The command from the branch tip** (R-BTN-153, adv-3 F4). The runner reads
     `check_command` and its `timeout_s` from `course.json` at the head of
     `refs/baton/<repository>/default` it fetched for the claim, never at an older
     `snapshot_sha`, so a post cannot run a check on a weaker historical command.
   - **`superseded_at` on a new head** (R-BTN-114, R-BTN-168, adv-3 F6). A post that changes
     `check.sha` also marks a completed claim on the old sha that is still unconfirmed
     `superseded_at`, so a push right after a check ends never wedges the next one. B-65 builds
     the mark and its effects (never confirmed, concludes nothing, no `baton.check-unattested`,
     no attempt, no longer holding rule 3). Whichever of B-5 and B-65 lands second wires this
     trigger to it, as item 1's `superseded` release already is.
   - **No older `seq` over a newer one** (R-BTN-115, adv-3 F7). Before posting, the forge
     courier reads the sha's check runs of that name and skips its notice when one carries a
     higher `seq`; each run's output holds its notice's `seq` beside the signature. In the
     sync's read-back, a newest run of a name that is not the highest-`seq` notice already marked
     `sent` for that head is `check-mismatch`, and Baton also re-queues that highest notice
     (`src/core/notices.ts`). A mark arriving after the lease lapsed stays accepted.
   - **Fileless check tickets re-verified against Baton's record** (R-BTN-5/R-BTN-34, adv-2
     M3). A `changes` check ticket has no file, so the runner (`src/runner/runner.ts`'s
     re-verification before the leg runs) checks it against the claim response's station and
     queued leg, never against git, and never releases it `stale-board` for a missing file.
     B-64 and B-65 add the built-in legs to the same path; whichever of the three lands first
     writes it.

### Do not

- Accept `checks` in a course file: conclusions are the owners' setting, a course file carrying
  `checks` is refused (R-BTN-69, S-BTN-68), and `baton course test` prints a check course's end
  stations for the owners to map.

### Tests

- S-BTN-68, S-BTN-81, S-BTN-123; G-BTN-93, G-BTN-103, G-BTN-143. Item 7's cases, on both
  hosts:
  - S-BTN-68: a push right after a `done`, before the sync, marks that `done` `superseded_at`
    and leaves the new head claimable; a courier that stalls past its lease and posts an older
    `seq` is skipped, or read-back raises `check-mismatch` and re-queues the newest notice;
  - S-BTN-123: the command read from the branch tip, not from an older `snapshot_sha`;
  - G-BTN-103: a check-runner's claim judged by its `check_runners` row's capabilities and
    `max_tier`, and refused to a key without one.
- Unit: a fileless check ticket re-verified against the claim response, with no git read.
- `npm test`, `npm run typecheck` and `npm run check:spec` green.
