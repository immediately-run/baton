**Brief:** ruling 5 of the spec simplification ("cut them",
[the review](../docs/review/spec-simplification-review-2026-11-19.mdx)) removes the `resource`
prerequisite and block. In the code, `BLOCK_KINDS` in `src/core/blocks.ts` lists `resource`; the
same file keeps `leg_needs` and `resource_fleet` (migration 0016, rebuilt in 0043), writes and
clears `resource` rows in `ticket_blocks`, and carries the "answer lost" record
(`tickets.answer_lost`, `lostAnswer`); `resourceAdvice` in `src/core/claims.ts` reads those rows
to leave a leg out of recommendations and to log `advice-overridden`; `checkBlockedOn` in
`src/core/validate.ts` admits `resource:` on a `blocked` end, which may carry a reading. Tests:
`blocks.test.ts`, `migrations-0043.test.ts`, `cli.test.ts`, `S-BTN-44.blocks`,
`S-BTN-45.block-edges`, `B-241.sync-unchanged`.

## Plan

1. **Spec, same PR, written before the code.** Delete R-BTN-76's *The `resource` prerequisite
   and block* and the `resource` row of §6.1's table, of R-BTN-75's kinds and of the `blocked`
   end's `on` (R-BTN-126). State in R-BTN-76 the one thing that stays: Baton stores and serves
   readings (R-BTN-99), and a course's `rank.mjs` reads them; Baton itself neither blocks nor
   leaves a leg out of recommendations for a reading. Three consequences are decided in that
   text, not in the code:
   - the sync's posted `needs` (the only source built: `src/core/course.ts` does not accept a
     leg's `requires.resources`): removed from the board post, or kept as data served to
     `rank.mjs` and stored nowhere;
   - a leg that meets an exhausted account mid-run (S-BTN-20, S-BTN-91): it ends
     `released:blocked` on a `capability` whose check is bound to the instance (R-BTN-121,
     B-245), or it is a failed attempt; the reading it carried is posted as a reading;
   - `baton.resource-unknown` and the `no-instance` word in `other`: retired with the row.
2. **Scenarios.** S-BTN-44, S-BTN-45 and S-BTN-24 lose their resource cases; S-BTN-20, S-BTN-91
   and G-BTN-111 are rewritten to the choice made above or retired; G-BTN-70 keeps the other
   kinds. S-BTN-224 (a runner's readings of an account) stays. History entry with `Rules
   changed:`, `npm run spec:index`, `npm run spec:impact -- R-BTN-76 R-BTN-75 R-BTN-126 --term
   "resource"`.
3. **Server.** Remove `resource` from `BLOCK_KINDS` and `checkBlockedOn`; the `leg_needs` and
   `resource_fleet` maintenance, `lostAnswer`, `expectedBack` and `answer_lost` handling in
   `blocks.ts`; `resourceAdvice` and its three call sites in `claims.ts` (recommendations, the
   claim's advice list, the `advance` override event); the board post's `needs` in
   `board.ts`. Keep `src/core/resources.ts`'s registry, instances, readings
   and states, and the views that serve them.
4. **Migration.** One new file (number from `node cli/baton.mjs tickets next migration --for
   B-291`): delete `ticket_blocks` rows of kind `resource` and recompute `blocked_since` for the
   tickets that had only those; drop `leg_needs` and `resource_fleet`; drop
   `tickets.answer_lost`. Migration 0016 and 0043 are not edited. B-287's candidate projection
   (PR #297) reads resource availability: whichever lands second removes or rebuilds that
   column in its own migration.
5. **Runner and CLI.** The runner stops sending a `resource` block; `baton leg why` and the
   board's block list lose the kind; `baton admin resource …` and `admin reading …` stay.
6. **Tests.** Remove the resource cases from the six files above. Add a migration test: a
   ticket blocked on a `resource` row alone is unblocked by the migration with no `unblocked`
   event and no dispatch (it was advice), and one blocked on a `ticket` row too stays blocked
   with its original `blocked_since`.

### Trust

No new input. Inputs removed: the `needs` a sync posts with a placed ticket (a sync key's or a
machine key's statement, reach 7) and the `resource:` target of a runner's `blocked` end. A reading keeps its writers and its
checks unchanged: the policy's `readings` knob says whose count (R-BTN-181), and
`src/core/validate.ts` bounds each. What changes is who acts on a reading: Baton no longer does;
a course's `rank.mjs` does, on the runner, where a false reading from a compromised runner could
already only misrank that runner's own choices.

  left and shows none referred to a `resource` row.
