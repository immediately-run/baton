**Spec:** `docs/content/specs/BATON_SPEC.mdx` §13 step 3 (preconditions, drain, R-BTN-28), R-BTN-66/67 (owner bars and required bars), R-BTN-2 (docs@2dbf1424; amended at docs#212 4e5ab93f: the path-rule precondition is withdrawn)
**Depends on:** R3-1216, R3-1219, the amendment's items R3-1231–R3-1240, and the checked model R3-1243 with its trace check R3-1244; and the 2026-10-15 amendments' items R3-1251 and R3-1253–R3-1258 (§2g–§2i; R3-1258's load test is a cutover precondition)

### Requires

- `cloudflare-admin` — exit criterion 1: the Workers Paid plan.
- `human-operator` — exit criteria 3–5: the drain, the dashboard switch and the deployment-log row.

### Why

Cutover is one-way (R-BTN-28) and has three preconditions only the owner can meet: the paid
plan (a quota outage would stop all claiming, R-BTN-18), docs' owner bars and required bars
set in Baton (R-BTN-67), and a drained fleet. *(2026-10-14: the owner withdrew the push rule on
the Baton workflows — whoever can push is a legitimate user, R-BTN-66.)*

### What to build

The owner, in one sitting:

1. Cloudflare: move the account to Workers Paid.
2. Baton: `baton owner settings docs --required-bar '*' --course roadmap-pr --except
   implement,review,adjudicate --tag-prefix family:`, and any provider suspensions in force as
   owner bars on every course (`baton owner bar docs --course '*' …`) — bars are per course
   since docs#212 @1dae3316.
3. Drain: stop every fleet session; confirm the dashboard's Workers view is empty.
4. Dashboard → Cut over (confirm). Legacy items appear in Needs you; confirm each adoption as
   its session returns.
5. Restart the fleet from pulled checkouts.
6. Add the measurement-source row to the deployment's log (§13).

### Architecture — the decisions, named

- Order matters: the paid plan and the bars first, then the drain, then the switch.
- **Do not:** cut over with a worker alive, or before R3-1243's model and R3-1244's trace check are green.

### Tests

None to write.

### Exit criteria

- [ ] the Cloudflare account shows Workers Paid
- [ ] `baton owner settings docs` lists the two required bars
- [ ] **live leg** (`human-operator`): the dashboard shows `mode: cut-over` and every legacy claim either adopted or listed with its `reap_after`
- [ ] the deployment's log has the measurement-source row dated the cutover day
- [ ] `npm run verify` green
