**Brief:** the owner, 2026-10-16 and 2026-10-17, as specified by B-1 (baton#43).
**Spec:** `spec/BATON_SPEC.md` §6.4 (R-BTN-116, R-BTN-78), §5.5 R-BTN-109 (a sync-posted arrival
is checked as a claim's is), §6.6 (rules 1 and `person`), §13.4's `ticket_decisions.asks`.
**Depends on:** B-3 (done: the `std:` library and the entry check).

### Requires

None: a checkout and a terminal.

### Why

A person's typed answer is a leg like any other: the ticket waits in that leg's queue, git
records the answer, and the station it reaches can require it. Baton only has to keep runners
out of the leg and check the answer when a sync shows it.

### What to build

1. **The course file.** `validateCourse` (`src/core/course.ts`) accepts `"kind": "person"` with
   `"asks": <label>` (R-BTN-78's `asks` pattern); `asks` is required on a person's leg and
   refused on any other.
2. **Rule `person`.** `src/core/gates.ts` refuses a claim or `advance` into a person's leg
   (`not-eligible`, rule `person`), and the recommendation query in `src/core/claims.ts` never
   offers one. A ticket whose queued leg is a person's waits in that leg's queue (R-BTN-79), with
   its posted decision's `decision` block (rule 1).
3. **The leg's `asks` wins.** When a snapshot posts a decision on a queued person's leg,
   `ticket_decisions` (`src/core/blocks.ts`) stores the leg's `asks`, whatever the post says, and
   logs `asks-mismatch` (R-BTN-81) when they differ. Decisions on other legs are stored as
   posted.
4. **The answer.** A snapshot whose path is extended by a person's leg is checked as R-BTN-109
   checks a sync-posted arrival: the output is a `std:decision@1` value or refinement, and the
   reached station's `entry` holds. An invalid output is the discrepancy `output-invalid`
   (R-BTN-85), is never resolved as an input, and raises `baton.output-invalid` (through today's
   listing if B-57 has not landed).

### Do not

- Open a decision or queue a notice in Baton: the ticket source posts the decision (R-BTN-116),
  and the `hitl.decision` it raises is B-56's.
- Make a person's leg wait on a delivery: it waits on the answer in git.
- Build completion at a choice station without being queued: that is B-63's (R-BTN-166).

### Tests

- S-BTN-67; G-BTN-90's person clause.
- Unit: the validator on `kind`/`asks`; rule `person` at a claim and an advance; the
  recommendation query skipping a person's leg.
- `npm test`, `npm run typecheck` and `npm run check:spec` green.

### Exit criteria

- [ ] the scenarios above pass on both hosts, with their traces replayed through the model where they touch the core
- [ ] merged under the review gate
