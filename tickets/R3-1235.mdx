### Requires

- none — the hook is pure and runs against fixture inputs and Baton's harness.

### Why

`roadmap-pr` needs provider-aware advice — forecasts, exhaustion, model advice — and
provider suspensions, and the owner decided all of it is course logic, not core
(2026-10-14). Since the stations revision a suspension is an owner bar in Baton, not a file
in git. Tier: judgement — one course, many inputs.

### What to build

1. The sync's course data: providers, prices, matrix and catalog, estimates (no
   suspensions: they are owner bars, R-BTN-67).
2. The sidecar's tags (`family:`, `account:`, `model:`) from the harness configuration and
   reports keyed per UTC hour (`key: h:<hour>`) and per claim.
3. `.baton/courses/roadmap-pr/hooks.js`: forecasts by reservation over active and ended
   claims' reports within `report_horizon_h` (168) and the ledger's month totals; exhaustion
   from any report, worker-level ones included; model advice and `seat_pairs`; the review-queue
   bound from the board summary; refusing a review to a new claim whose reviewers would bill
   a line under a `no-new-work` bar (from `claimed_at` and the bar's `starts_at`), and to a
   worker that cannot bill the seats.
4. The pre-tool hook reads the bars its heartbeat carried, with their times, to deny reviewer
   spawns offline.

### Architecture — the decisions, named

- **The hook is one ES module, bundled by the sync**, under 1 MB, importing the pure
  functions from `scripts/lib/courses/roadmap-pr/` at bundle time.
- **Suspensions are read only from the bars** in the hook's input and the heartbeat; no file
  in git sets or lifts one.
- **Do not**: refuse a claim on a forecast; read another repository's claims (the input has
  none); write a provider name into core.

### Tests

- `scripts/lib/courses/roadmap-pr/hooks.test.mjs`: every gate test of the course spec §7 over
  fixture inputs.
- Integration in Baton's harness with docs' `.baton/` as the fixture course: S-BTN-8, -9, -11,
  -19, -20, -21, -27.
