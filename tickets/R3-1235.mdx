**Spec:** `docs/content/specs/BATON_ROADMAP_PR_COURSE.mdx` §1 (the ranking code's row), §2, §3 (R-BTN-42), §4 (R-BTN-43 as of 2026-10-16), §5 (R-BTN-49), §6, §7 (G-BTN-28–30, -34, -45, -46 as replaced, -48, -52, -65); `docs/content/specs/BATON_SPEC.mdx` §2l R-BTN-98 (`rank.mjs`, `view`, `protocol_files`), R-BTN-99, R-BTN-100 (`bills`), R-BTN-101 (`measure`), §13a S-BTN-19, -20, -21, -27 as amended (docs@646eca6, the merge of docs#220)
**Depends on:** R3-1233 (the course in docs), R3-1269 (the views and `bills`), R3-1272 (the CLI that loads `rank.mjs`)

### Requires

- none — `rank` is pure over its `view` and runs against fixture views; the scenarios run in Baton's harness with docs' `.baton/`.

### Why

`roadmap-pr` needs provider-aware advice — which leg to take on which account, which model to
start a session on — and its suspensions. Until 2026-10-16 that was a hook inside Baton forecasting
by reservation. The owner retired both: the hook because Baton runs no repository code, the
forecast because estimates are unverifiable and gameable. What remains is the course's ranking
code on the worker, judging room from measurements. Tier: judgement — one course, many inputs.

### What to build

1. The sync's course data: providers (accounts, per-model limits, `max_concurrent`, the prefix → accounts map), prices, matrix and catalog, estimates (p75 per leg kind and family). No `factory_share`; no suspensions (owner bars).
2. The sidecar: tags (`family:`, `account:`, `model:`) from the harness configuration; hourly reports; a **`measure`** reading per billed account — the session's cumulative list-price spend per window, at each leg's end and at most hourly while billing, `valid_for` up to the longest window (≤ 31 days); `state` readings on a rate limit and on recovery; `bills` on every claim and `advance` (the claimant's coding account for `implement`, `fix`, `merge`; the seats' accounts, with `n`, for `review`).
3. `.baton/courses/roadmap-pr/rank.mjs`, listed in `protocol_files`: `rank(view)` marks each account short or possibly roomy from its readings, summed `measure`s and billing count against `max_concurrent`; orders candidates; skips a review the worker cannot bill or whose reviewers bill a line under a `no-new-work` bar for a new claim; flags `wip-bound` past 8 tickets beyond `filed`; returns per-candidate `advice` (ranked models, `seat_pairs`) and, for `worker: null`, `launch` (the model to start on).
4. The leg scripts and `baton-seats.mjs` read `seat_pairs` from the `advice` `baton runner start` kept, falling back to the matrix order.
5. The pre-tool hook keeps denying reviewer spawns on suspended lines from the heartbeat's bars, fail closed.

### Architecture — the decisions, named

- **`rank.mjs` imports the pure functions** from `scripts/lib/courses/roadmap-pr/` (created by R3-1233 and this item: `qualifyingPair`, `familyOf`, the room rule) at the commit it is loaded from (R-BTN-98 lets it import any module of the repository there); no bundling step.
- **The room rule is one function** `roomOf(readings, billings, account, legEstimate, now)` in `scripts/lib/courses/roadmap-pr/room.mjs`, used by `rank` and by nothing that posts to Baton.
- **Estimates never leave the worker**: no estimate in `bills`, reports, readings or `rationale`.
- **Do not**: refuse anything from `rank` (skip is advice); read another repository's tickets from the views; sum spend in Baton; recreate `factory_share` or reservations.

### Tests

- `scripts/lib/courses/roadmap-pr/rank.test.mjs`: every course-spec §7 gate test over fixture views built from a real board post of docs' sync (R3-1233's `sync.mjs` run on a docs checkout, saved under `scripts/lib/courses/roadmap-pr/fixtures/`) and readings shaped by the registered schema.
- `scripts/lib/courses/roadmap-pr/room.test.mjs`: exhausted, measured-too-high, at-`max_concurrent`, and roomy cases; a `measure` from another repository counts.
- Integration in Baton's harness with docs' `.baton/` as the fixture course: S-BTN-19, -20, -21, -27 as amended in BATON_SPEC §2l.
