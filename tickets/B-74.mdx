**Brief:** the owner, 2026-10-20: "Within the timeout interval, the parent leg can wait for the
HITL answer to arrive. If the human response arrives during this time then the channel can respond
directly. Otherwise, the parent course's leg will be interrupted and the HITL response must be
written to git." The design was checked against every failure point the owner named (process
death, lost responses, two channels, wait expiry, the outbox writer) by `model/lightQuestion.qnt`
before it was written into the spec.
**Spec:** `spec/BATON_SPEC.md` §7.2 R-BTN-173 (with its failure table); R-BTN-160 (§7.1, `light`
on the raise, the declaration and the report); R-BTN-161 (handler tickets, delivery runners);
R-BTN-162 (the wait, `handled`, the blocked end, `resume`); R-BTN-169 (§7.9, `ask` and `deliver`,
`retract`); R-BTN-171 (§7.8, the outbox's `handler` and `answer` kinds, `skipped`); R-BTN-114
(§10, tickets Baton holds without a file); R-BTN-10 (§9.6, confirmation); §9.4 (the end's
`answers`); §13.4, §13.6, §13.8 (`tickets.baton_held`, `conditions.light`, `promoted_at`,
`recorded`, `condition_answers`, the outbox's `answer` kind, `answer-ignored`); §14 (`POST

**Depends on:**

### Requires

None: a checkout and a terminal.

### Why

Every `hitl` question today costs a ticket file and a push before the leg can wait, and a reply on
a channel cannot count as the answer. A leg that asks many quick questions needs a question that
costs no git write when the person answers while it waits, and loses no answer when it does not.

### What to build

1. **Raising light** (`src/runner/`, `src/core/`):
   - `light` on `ctx.raise`, `baton leg raise --light`, a type's declaration and the raise's
     report; the course validator accepts it on `suspend` types only.
   - For a bound light raise the runner writes no handler file and makes no default-branch
     commit: it pushes the question as a parentless commit holding `question.md` to
     `refs/baton/questions/<local id>/<commit sha>` and reports `question` (a `std:git.file@1`);
     Baton stores the pointer only and files the handler ticket as a Baton-held row
     (`tickets.baton_held`), at the course's start station, under R-BTN-161's id.
   - A Baton-held ticket's children are Baton-held: its leg's `spawned` end carries `{local_id,
     course, input?}` per child, Baton creates them in that transaction, and they are never
     `spawned-unposted` (R-BTN-86). The ask's `open` files its `deliver` child this way.
   - R-BTN-5's re-verification reads Baton's record, not git, for a Baton-held ticket.
   - `answered_by` is a channel's opaque id (`<channel>:<id>`), never a name or address.
   - A Baton-held ticket's legs are claimed and advanced as any leg's; its ends are confirmed at the
     end; an accepted snapshot neither posts nor drops it.
2. **The answer report** (`src/core/`, a registry entry): `POST /v1/runner/claims/{id}/answers`
   from the holder of a delivery leg of the condition's handler, `{condition_id, channel,
   message_id, restart, answered_by}`, validated in `src/core/validate.ts`. In one transaction:
   - a repeat of an accepted `(condition_id, channel, message_id)` answers its first state;
   - an open condition settles (`outcome: answered`), the handler closes `done`, `handled` is
     queued with the restart inline and the holder is rung;
   - a promoted condition with no answer queued queues an outbox `answer` entry;
   - anything else records `ignored` and an `answer-ignored` history row.
3. **Recording and carrying** (`src/runner/`, `src/core/`):
   - The runner pushes each consumed light answer into the leg's evidence or hand-off before any
     end, and sends `answers: [condition_id]`; Baton sets `conditions.recorded`.
   - The claim response's `resume` lists every settled `suspend` condition of the leg not
     `recorded`, in raise order, after any end (a reap included); `ctx.resume` is that list.
4. **Promotion** (`src/core/`): in the transaction of any end of the waiting claim, or its reap,
   that finds its light condition open, queue an outbox `handler` entry (the ask file at the
   station reached, with `baton_position: {station, path}` read by the CLI's station-function
   library as closing markers and the question's text as its body; its `deliver` child staying
   Baton-held), set `promoted_at`, and write a non-provisional `condition` block on the leg
   (R-BTN-162's rule for every `suspend`). Keep the ticket's row and arrival
   instants; clear `baton_held` when the entry lands.
   - A ticket that changes station or closes with a settled, unrecorded light answer of a leg it
     leaves queues a `handler` entry writing the closed ask with its answer.
5. **The outbox's `answer` kind** (the `git` courier, B-64): write `answer` and `answered_by`
   with `baton_write: <id>`; read the file at the fetched head first, and mark the entry
   `skipped` when it already carries an answer; Baton then records that reply `ignored`.
6. **Channels** (`src/defaults/courses/deliver/`): `answers: true` on `push` with a webhook
   channel that keeps replies until acknowledged; the leg acknowledges a reply only after Baton
   answered, re-reads its condition's replies when re-claimed, and `retract` reports unread replies
   before it withdraws. A leg declaring `answers: false` never reports.
7. **Migration** (a new numbered file): `tickets.baton_held`; `conditions.light`,
   `promoted_at`, `recorded`; the `condition_answers` table; `answer` in the outbox's kinds and
   `skipped` in its states; `answer-ignored` in the history kinds.
8. **The model**: `model/lightQuestion.qnt` is the design's check. Keep its rules in step with
   the code, and run its `full` instance and each `no*` instance in the PR (commands in the file's
   header).

### Do not

- Write or push anything to git at the moment a reply settles a condition in the claim.
- Decide promotion on the runner's clock or outside the transaction that ends or reaps the claim.
- Apply a reply to a condition that is not open, or apply two replies to one condition.
- Re-run the leg, or let another claimant take it, while its light question is open.
- Drop a settled answer at a reap: it is carried until an end records it.
- Overwrite an answer already in an ask file.
- Acknowledge a reply to the channel before Baton has answered the report.
- Store the question, the reply's text or anything but restart ids, opaque identities and the
  question's pointer (R-BTN-33).
