**Brief:** the ticket plan for the current-state spec (PR #71, 2026-10-20), §2's B-65. A call
that settles on two approvals of three, an ask answered before its push channel fired, or a
condition an owner resolved by hand all leave work running that nobody needs; this ticket ends
it and undoes what a leg declared undoable.
**Spec:** `spec/BATON_SPEC.md` §7.7 R-BTN-168; §4.6 R-BTN-86 (children, the parent's close
superseding `lifetime: parent` children); §5.2 R-BTN-69 (`kind: compensation`, `compensate`,
`spawns.lifetime`, the validator's compensation rules, built-in legs and rule 9 binding
`baton.cancel`); §5.5 R-BTN-111 (`spawns`); §6.9 (`release` reason `superseded`, no hand-off);
§8.5 R-BTN-119 (stopping at once); §9.4 (`released:superseded`, not a failed attempt); §7.8
R-BTN-171 (`withdraw` entries, their drain by `baton.cancel`); §13.6
`ticket_overrides.override_kind` `cancel`; §13.8 `events.kind` `superseded`; §15 S-BTN-129,
S-BTN-132, G-BTN-153.
**Depends on:** B-64 (the outbox, `withdraw` entries and the `baton.write` drain this reuses),
B-62 (call settlement, the first supersession trigger, and `spawns` on a call leg), R3-1258
(children: `tickets.parent` and the `spawned` record that `lifetime` reads), B-9 (R-BTN-119's
stop, which a `superseded` release uses). The choice trigger needs B-63 and the `changes`
trigger needs B-5: whichever of this ticket and each of those lands second wires that trigger.

### Requires

None: a checkout and a terminal.

### Why

Without supersession a call's surplus children, a lapsed ask's push message and a resolved
condition's handler run to completion and keep runners busy; without compensation a superseded
`deliver` leaves its notification on the person's phone. G-BTN-153 is the guarantee.

### What to build

1. **Course file** (`src/core/course.ts`, `src/core/graph.ts`). Accept `kind: "compensation"`
   (no `from`, no `to`, outside the graph), a leg's `compensate: <leg>` naming one, and
   `spawns.lifetime` `parent|station`. Refuse a compensation leg as any leg's `from`, `to`,
   `next` or completion `next`, and a `compensate` naming a leg that is not one. The kit
   (`src/local/testCourse.ts`) reports the same.
2. **`supersede(ticket, by)` in a new `src/core/cancel.ts`**, called in the deciding transaction
   of each trigger:
   - a call settled without the ticket (B-62's `settleCall` path in `src/core/stations.ts`);
   - a handler's condition closed by other means (an owner's resolve, its cause ending, the
     raising ticket cancelled: B-56/B-58's close path);
   - a `lifetime: parent` child whose parent closed or was cancelled, a `lifetime: station`
     child whose parent left the station it was filed at (the snapshot transaction in
     `src/core/board.ts`);
   - a choice settled against the work (B-63) and a fileless `changes` ticket no longer posted
     (B-5), as above.

   It sends any running claim a `release` with reason `superseded` (the `release` message
   `src/core/claims.ts` already writes for a stale board), logs `superseded {by}`, and then: for a ticket with a file, queues a `withdraw`
   entry (B-64) and holds `baton.cancel` (`next_source: cancel`) if any leg on its path named a
   compensation, else `baton.write` (`next_source: outbox`); for a ticket with no file, holds
   `baton.cancel` if its path has compensations, else closes it `withdrawn` at once. The ticket
   shows `cancelling` (dashboard, `GET …/tickets/{t}`) until it closes. A ticket withdrawn in git
   whose path has compensations stays open, `cancelling`, while `baton.cancel` runs.
3. **The end.** `released:superseded` in the end validator and `src/core/attempts.ts`: not a
   failed attempt, no hand-off expected. The runner (`src/runner/runner.ts`) stops at once and
   calls `close` but not `handoff`.
4. **`baton.cancel` in the runner** (built-in, `runner:baton-legs`, bound by rule 9). Under one
   claim it runs, in reverse path order, the compensation leg of each completed leg that named
   one, each as a leg run needing that leg's capabilities (a missing one releases `blocked` on
   it, R-BTN-126); then drains the ticket's `withdraw` entry as `baton.write` does; then pushes
   and ends at the withdrawn close. Core gives its claim response the compensation list and the
   entry.
5. **Cascade.** Cancelling a ticket supersedes its open `lifetime: parent` children, each in the
   same transaction.
6. **Model.** The `superseded` release and the `cancel` override kind, with a `model:pr` report
   (R-BTN-68).

### Do not

- Kill anything from core: a `superseded` release asks the runner (R-BTN-119).
- Count `released:superseded` toward attempts or `max_blocked`.
- Run compensations from `baton.write`, or drain a compensating ticket's `withdraw` entry
  anywhere but its own `baton.cancel`.
- Supersede children with no `lifetime`: they live on (R-BTN-86).

### Tests

- **S-BTN-132**'s supersession half on both hosts (the third `review-pr` child superseded and
  withdrawn through the outbox), its trace replayed through the model.
- **S-BTN-129**'s compensation case with a fixture course whose leg names a compensation (the
  whole scenario is B-66's, on `ask`/`deliver`).
- Unit: the validator's compensation rules; reverse path order; a fileless ticket with and
  without compensations; the `lifetime: station` trigger on a station change.
