**Brief:** the owner, 2026-11-01, on the whole-spec review they commissioned ("assess it for …
can any vocabulary, core concepts or actors be simplified … is the user experience intuitive …
are the security guarantees … truly guaranteed and … do they matter"), asked for its findings
to be filed as tickets. Source: `docs/review/spec-simplification-review-2026-11-01.mdx`.
The review's claims are a reviewer's, checked against the spec where they cite it; this ticket
verifies each against the text before changing anything.

### Why

Review finding 1, F4 and F7, and journey "Move from `cooperative` to `protected`":
- `merge.checks: required` and "nothing merges until `std:checks@1` is set" (R-BTN-188), but the
  trusted executor that writes `std:checks@1` (R-BTN-181) is never registered, granted or run.
- `tickets.created: proposed` makes every handler ticket, Baton's own `baton.*` notifications
  included, "not claimable until it lands with a `std:approval@1`" (R-BTN-161).
- `std:decision@1` "admits `human` alone" under `protected` (R-BTN-191), while the `ask` course's
  answer is the sync's attestation of the ask file (R-BTN-169): every question expires.
- `adopt` needs a `human` key under `protected` (R-BTN-39): one ceremony per in-flight ticket
  after a course rename.
- A trusted-runner deliverer "marks every git entry `failed` with `refused`" on a repository
  with its own `tickets.mjs` (R-BTN-171).
- `baton request` "opens a PR, not a push" (R-BTN-180).

### Decided (the owner)

**Rulings of 2026-11-01** (the owner, answering each question in the session that continued
immediately-run/baton#150; the restructure, B-166, writes them into the spec):

- **Checks:** "Forge checks suffice": `protected`'s required checks are met by check runs from
  workflows on the protected default branch (R-BTN-183); the policy table's "nothing merges until
  `std:checks@1` is set" is corrected, and the trusted executor becomes optional.
- **Notifications:** "No ticket file": a delivery runner polls Baton's open conditions and
  delivers the resolve link; no handler ticket, outbox entry or withdrawal for Baton's own
  conditions. Handler tickets remain for conditions a leg raises.

### What to build

1. **A spec PR first**, under the babysit skill's *Spec PRs*: two fresh reviewers, the impact list,
   every rule restating the same fact changed with it.
2. Define the trusted executor (likely §10's check-runner posting `std:checks@1` as its
   `service:` key), or make `protected`'s `merge.checks` `optional`.
3. Exempt Baton's own notify handlers from `tickets.created: proposed`, or deliver them
   without a ticket file (see B-160).
4. Give `ask` a `protected` answer path (a `human` `std:decision@1`).
5. A cheaper adopt for a course change Baton can map itself (`renamed_from`).
6. `baton owner policy apply --dry-run`: every guarantee, ticket and leg the preset would
   stop, before applying it.
