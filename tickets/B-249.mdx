**What:** Baton's own downtime must not count against a claim's lease. On 2026-11-15 the Durable Object quota ran out at 14:12Z; vltava2's runner held baton:B-176, finished its fix leg during the outage, and has retried the end every beat since ("the end of baton:B-176 did not reach Baton (internal error)"). When the quota resets at 00:00Z the alarm's reaper runs again and can reap the claim — its last heartbeat ~10 h old — before the runner's next beat delivers the end, discarding a finished leg.

- Baton records when it was last able to serve (e.g. the alarm's last successful tick or last successful request) and, on the first tick after a gap longer than a lease, extends every active lease by the gap (or treats `last_seen_at` as no earlier than the gap's end) before reaping.
- Equivalently acceptable: a grace of one lease after any gap before the reaper may reap.
- The runner's end retry already exists; the fix is server-side.

**Done when:** a test stops the alarm and requests for longer than a lease, resumes, and shows a held claim is not reaped before its runner's next beat delivers the end; and a genuinely dead runner is still reaped one lease after recovery.

### Trust

No new input: the gap is measured from Baton's own clock and its own records.

### Invariants

- R-BTN-19 (fencing) and the reaper's lease rule: a runner that stays silent a full lease after Baton recovers is still reaped; only Baton's own downtime is excused.
