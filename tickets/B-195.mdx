**Brief:** the owner, 2026-11-09 (B-191): "**Conditions** `/conditions` → `/conditions/{id}` —
what waits on a person (the old 'Needs you' as an entity screen); the Resolve page is the detail
view, one restart click." The mockup's Needs you list — a row per item with its kind mark
(◆ Unconfirmed, ? Decision open, ‖ Held, ▲ Nobody can take, ✕ Retries exhausted), the ticket,
one sentence of why, and its one or two actions at the right (Confirm / Release, Answer…,
Release hold / Cancel…, View blocked, Retry once more / Hold) — is the Conditions screen's row.

**Spec:** [BATON_SPEC §7.3](../spec/BATON_SPEC/07-conditions.md#sec-7-3) ([R-BTN-163](../spec/BATON_SPEC/07-conditions.md#sec-7-3):
the debugger, resolving, Baton's own conditions, the Resolve page), [§7.1](../spec/BATON_SPEC/07-conditions.md#sec-7-1)
([R-BTN-160](../spec/BATON_SPEC/07-conditions.md#sec-7-1): a condition's fields, `url`),
[§6.4](../spec/BATON_SPEC/06-prerequisites-blocks-advice.md#sec-6-4) ([R-BTN-116](../spec/BATON_SPEC/06-prerequisites-blocks-advice.md#sec-6-4):
a person's leg and its question), [§12.8](../spec/BATON_SPEC/12-security.md#sec-12-8)
([R-BTN-181](../spec/BATON_SPEC/12-security.md#sec-12-8): `attestation.add`; the policy's writer
list), [§11.4](../spec/BATON_SPEC/11-people.md#sec-11-4) (the Conditions screens as B-191 amends
them; R-BTN-155: resolving and a person leg's answer are not confirmed actions),
[§14](../spec/BATON_SPEC/14-api.md#sec-14) (`GET …/conditions`, `ticket.resolve`,
`POST …/attestations`).

**Depends on:** B-193 (the shell), B-55 (the `conditions` row and `GET …/conditions`: today's
Needs you is `notices.ts` and the `needs_you` object, which B-56 and B-57 fold into conditions;
this screen reads conditions only).

**Used by:** B-203.

### What to build

The §14 row first, then the code.

1. **`GET /v1/repositories/{repository}/conditions/{condition}`** (`condition.show`, read; the
   repository reads' principals and a session): the one condition as the list returns it —
   `condition_id`, `ticket`, `leg`, `type`, `disposition`, `handler`, `restarts` (each with the
   station it resumes at), `default`, `severity`, `url` (only if it passed §12.3), `detail`,
   `raised_at`, `closed_at`, `outcome` — plus `unblocks` (the ticket ids its closing would make
   ready, from `ticket_blocks`) and, for a `hitl` type bound to a person leg, the leg's
   `question` as `GET …/conditions?person=1` returns it. `not-found` for another repository's
   condition is the same answer as for none.
2. **`/repositories/{r}/conditions`**: the open conditions and queued person legs from
   `GET …/conditions?open=1&person=1`, ordered by severity then age (R-BTN-163), each row: the
   kind mark and type (core's `baton.*` types named plainly: unconfirmed completion, held, nobody
   can take, retries exhausted, stalled, contract, course refused, queue over, scope finding,
   sign-in burst), the ticket (linking to its page), the one-sentence `detail` as text, since
   when, the tickets it would unblock, and its controls: each restart as a button (one click,
   `ticket.resolve {restart, condition_id}`); a person leg's options as buttons where the
   policy's `attestations` admits an `owner` writer for `std:decision@1` (the answer is
   `attestation.add`), else the line `baton decision answer <ticket> <option>` as text; the
   question's `url` as a link. `?type=` and `?ticket=` filters as URL query; closed conditions
   behind a toggle (`?open=0`), 100 a page.
3. **The Resolve page**, `/repositories/{r}/conditions/{condition}`: the one condition from
   `condition.show`, its detail pretty-printed as text (a blob as JSON, §12.3), its restarts as
   the page's controls, the ticket's position beside it, and what it unblocks. Signed out, the
   shell's sign-in with a return to this path (R-BTN-163); the legacy `/conditions/{condition}`
   redirects here (B-193). After a resolve, the page polls until `closed_at` is set and says so.
4. **Counts.** The overview strip's "needs you" count is this screen's open count; the
   sidebar's Conditions count is the same number.
5. **Phone.** The list reads at 375 px with the controls under each row; the Resolve page is the
   screen a person opens from a phone notification, so its restart buttons are large and the
   whole page fits without horizontal scroll.

### What not to do

- No second source of Needs you: the screen reads `conditions` only; what B-56 and B-57 have not
  yet moved into conditions is not drawn here (it stays on V1 until they land, and B-203
  removes V1 only once every listing is a condition).
- No answer the policy does not admit: the app draws the options as buttons only when the read
  says an owner writer is admitted; it never posts an attestation to find out.
- No handler or restart logic in the app: the restarts are the condition's; the server
  refuses one it does not offer (`invalid-params`), shown as text.
- A resolve and an answer are one click (R-BTN-155): no confirm dialog on them.

### Trust

- **`condition.show`** — Baton's row; `detail`, `type`, `url` and a question's `asks` and
  options are untrusted text validated on the raise (R-BTN-160, §12.3) and drawn as text; `url`
  is a link only because the server already dropped one that failed the link rule.
- **A restart click** — `ticket.resolve` through `authorize`: an owner's session on its
  repositories, an administrator's anywhere; never a delegated key (R-BTN-186).
- **A person leg's answer** — `attestation.add` as writer `owner` (R-BTN-181), bounded by the
  policy's writer list; the option id is validated on the server.
- **The path's condition id** — sent as-is, validated by §12.3's id rule; a foreign or missing id
  answers `not-found` with no existence oracle.

### Invariants

- **R-BTN-163**: open conditions and queued person legs, by severity then age; resolving in one
  step; the Resolve page returns to itself after sign-in.
- **R-BTN-116**: the answer is a `std:decision@1`; where the policy admits no owner writer the
  screen names the command and posts nothing.
- **R-BTN-155**: resolving and answering are not confirmed actions; nothing here is.
- **Text, never markup** (§12.3).
- **An owner's session, its repositories** (R-BTN-155).

### Tests

Scenarios numbered at merge, on both hosts, with a fixture raising a `hitl` condition with two
restarts, a core `baton.retries`, and a queued person leg under two policies (owner writer
admitted, not admitted):

- `condition.show` returns the condition with `unblocks` and, for the person leg, its
  `question`; another repository's id is `not-found`;
- the list orders by severity then age and filters by `?type=` and `?ticket=`;
- under `test/ui/`: a restart click sends `ticket.resolve` with the condition id and the page
  shows `closed_at` after the poll; the person leg draws option buttons under the admitting
  policy and the `baton decision answer` line under the other; signed out, the Resolve page
  shows sign-in and returns to its path; a hostile `detail` is text.
