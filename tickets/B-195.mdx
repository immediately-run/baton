**Brief:** the owner, 2026-11-09 (B-191): "**Conditions** `/conditions` → `/conditions/{id}` —
what waits on a person (the old 'Needs you' as an entity screen); the Resolve page is the detail
view, one restart click." The mockup's Needs you list (`root.txt`: a row per item with its kind
mark — ◆ Unconfirmed, ? Decision open, ‖ Held, ▲ Nobody can take, ✕ Retries exhausted — the
ticket, one sentence, its actions at the right) is this screen's row.

**Spec:** [BATON_SPEC §7.3](../spec/BATON_SPEC/07-conditions.md#sec-7-3) ([R-BTN-163](../spec/BATON_SPEC/07-conditions.md#sec-7-3):
the debugger, resolving in one step, the Resolve page and its redirect as B-191 amends it),
[§7.1](../spec/BATON_SPEC/07-conditions.md#sec-7-1) ([R-BTN-160](../spec/BATON_SPEC/07-conditions.md#sec-7-1):
a condition's fields), [§6.4](../spec/BATON_SPEC/06-prerequisites-blocks-advice.md#sec-6-4)
([R-BTN-116](../spec/BATON_SPEC/06-prerequisites-blocks-advice.md#sec-6-4): a person's leg, its
question and options), [§12.8](../spec/BATON_SPEC/12-security.md#sec-12-8) ([R-BTN-181](../spec/BATON_SPEC/12-security.md#sec-12-8):
`attestation.add` and the policy's writer list), [§11.4](../spec/BATON_SPEC/11-people.md#sec-11-4)
(the Conditions screens; R-BTN-155: resolving and answering are not confirmed; the `allowed` rule),
[§14](../spec/BATON_SPEC/14-api.md#sec-14) (`GET …/conditions`, `POST /v1/ui/conditions/{condition}/resolve`,
`POST …/attestations`).

**Depends on:** B-207 (controls and dialogs), B-55 (the `conditions` row and `GET …/conditions`),
B-56 (`POST /v1/ui/conditions/{condition}/resolve`, the session's one-step resolve).

**Used by:** B-203.

### What to build

The §14 rows first, then the code.

1. **`GET /v1/repositories/{repository}/conditions/{condition}`** (`condition.show`, read; the
   repository reads' principals and a session): the condition as the list returns it
   (`condition_id`, `ticket`, `leg`, `type`, `disposition`, `handler`, `restarts` each with the
   station it resumes at, `default`, `severity`, `url` where it passed §12.3, `detail`,
   `raised_at`, `closed_at`, `outcome`), plus `unblocks` (ticket ids its closing would make
   ready, from `ticket_blocks`), `allowed` (`["ticket.resolve"]` where the caller may resolve
   it), and for a `hitl` type on a person leg `question: {asks, options: [{id, reached}],
   url?, answerable}` — `answerable` true when `authorize` says this caller may write the
   `std:decision@1` under the policy in force (R-BTN-181, R-BTN-116). `not-found` for another
   repository's id, the same as for none.
2. **The list read** (`GET …/conditions`, B-55's): `?open=1` answers in severity then age order
   (R-BTN-163), and each `questions` row carries `answerable` as above — two §14 row changes.
3. **The redirect** (`src/core/front.ts` and one core lookup): `GET /conditions/{id}` with a
   session whose repositories hold the id answers `302` to
   `/repositories/{r}/conditions/{id}`; otherwise the shell is served, which shows sign-in or
   `not-found` (B-191, R-BTN-163). `resolve_url` in the list read is the tree path.
4. **`/repositories/{r}/conditions`** (`src/dashboard/screens/conditions/`): the rows from
   `GET …/conditions?open=1&person=1`, in the read's order: the kind mark and a plain name for
   core's types (unconfirmed completion, held, nobody can take, retries exhausted, stalled,
   contract, course refused, queue over, sign-in burst; a course's own type by its name), the
   ticket linking its page, `detail` as text, since when, what it unblocks, and the controls:
   each restart a one-click button (`POST /v1/ui/conditions/{condition}/resolve {restart}`,
   placed by `ui.view` on `repository/conditions/{condition}` and drawn in the row too); a
   person leg's options as buttons where `answerable` (each an `attestation.add` of a
   `std:decision@1`), else the line `baton decision answer <ticket> <option>` as text; the
   question's `url` as a link. `?type=` and `?ticket=` as URL query; closed conditions behind a
   toggle (`?open=0`); load-more by the read's cursor.
5. **The Resolve page**, `/repositories/{r}/conditions/{condition}`: from `condition.show` —
   the condition, `detail` through `View` where its type's schema has a view, else as
   pretty-printed text; the restarts as large one-click buttons; the ticket's position beside
   it; what it unblocks. Signed out, the shell's sign-in in place, returning to this path; after
   a resolve, the page polls until `closed_at` is set and says so. The one control not drawn
   is a code dialog: resolving and answering are not confirmed (R-BTN-155).
6. **Counts.** The strip's "needs you" count is this read's open count; the sidebar's the same.
7. **Phone.** The list's controls sit under each row at 375 px; the Resolve page fits without
   horizontal scroll (it is the page a person opens from a notification).

### What not to do

- No second source of Needs you: this screen reads conditions only; what B-56 and B-57 have
  not yet made a condition (B-203's list) is not drawn here.
- No permission logic: options are buttons only when `answerable`; restarts only when
  `allowed`; the app posts nothing to find out.
- No confirm dialog on a resolve or an answer (R-BTN-155).

### Trust

- **`condition.show` and the list** — Baton's rows; `detail`, `type`, `asks`, options and `url`
  are untrusted text validated on the raise (§12.3), drawn as text or through R-BTN-112's
  renderer; `url` is a link only because the server already applied the link rule.
- **`answerable` and `allowed`** — computed by `authorize` with the session as principal; the
  client draws on that word and the server decides again on the call.
- **A restart click** — `ticket.resolve` as a session (B-56's route); never a delegated key.
- **An answer** — `attestation.add` as writer `owner`, bounded by the policy on the server.
- **The redirect** — the core looks the id up within the session's repositories; nothing
  outside them is revealed, and a miss serves the shell.

### Invariants

- **R-BTN-163**: open conditions and queued person legs by severity then age; one-step
  resolving; the Resolve page returns to itself.
- **R-BTN-116**: an answer is a `std:decision@1`; where the session may not write it, the
  command is named and nothing is posted.
- **R-BTN-155**: nothing here is confirmed. **Text, never markup** (§12.3). **An owner's session,
  its repositories** (R-BTN-155).

### Tests

Scenarios numbered at merge, on both hosts, with a fixture `hitl` condition with two restarts, a
core `baton.retries`, and a person leg under two policies (owner writer admitted, not):

- `condition.show` returns `unblocks`, `allowed` and the question with `answerable` true under
  one policy and false under the other; another repository's id is `not-found`;
- `?open=1` orders by severity then age; `GET /conditions/{id}` is `302` for a session holding
  the repository and the shell otherwise;
- under `test/ui/`: a restart click sends the resolve and the page shows `closed_at` after the
  poll; options are buttons only when `answerable`, else the command line; signed out, the
  Resolve page shows sign-in and keeps its path; a hostile `detail` is text.
