**Brief:** the owner, 2026-11-01, on review round two of immediately-run/baton#154, choosing to
split the `merged-by-hand` hardening out of it: "Split the hardening out".

**Spec:** `spec/BATON_SPEC.md` R-BTN-131 (`create-pr`'s `merged-by-hand`, the record courses),
R-BTN-192 (`pr-merged`, `fact-legs`), R-BTN-183 (*Continuity*, *Foreign commits*, *Paths*),
R-BTN-5, R-BTN-74 and §4.3 (`positions`), R-BTN-83 (`route`), R-BTN-96 (plain git), R-BTN-120
(`closed_tickets.final_station`).

### Why

`merged-by-hand` arrives on any merge of the ticket's PR that no `merge` leg reported. Review of
#154's rounds one and two (`spec/BATON_SPEC_HISTORY.md`, 2026-11-01) found, for every course that
uses it:

- **Which merge counts.**
  - A merge made before the last writing leg's push, or before the leg began waiting, can
    satisfy a later wait.
  - A merge racing the last leg's `advance` can strand the ticket closed in git at a non-end
    station.
  - Squash merges contain no ancestor head.
  - A `prs:` field written on the branch can name another PR.
  - Under a protected default branch, nothing checks that the human identity made the merge.
- **The owner's commits and other runners' commits.**
  - R-BTN-183's foreign-commit reset and continuity rules apply to every ticket's branch, so the
    owner's refinements can be reset, and a route has no defined head.
  - A route that records "the branch's head" would make another runner's commit a leg's input.
- **What a hand merge may change.** Nothing checks it, so a document PR can land `.github/` or
  `.baton/` paths on the owner's merge.
- **A ticket its own merge closed.**
  - `positions`, the station function's input and `fact-legs` list live tickets only, so a merge
    that closes the ticket in git can hide its own arrival.
  - `closed_tickets.final_station` is never rewritten afterwards.
- **Plain git.** There is no `pr-merged` reading: whether a `--no-ff` merge or a fast-forward
  counts, how the merged head is found, and who pushed it.
- **The read point.** R-BTN-5 reads at the branch's fetched head, not at the last attested head,
  and the claim response does not carry that head.

### What to build

1. **The spec amendment first**, its own PR with two fresh reviewers and the impact list. It
   decides each point above for every course at once. Starting points from #154's review:
   - **Which merge counts:** containment of the last attested head by the PR's head at merge, a
     squash included, and the human identity's merge under a protected default branch.
   - **Commits:** a route that takes an explicit `--head`, refused unless every commit after the
     last attested head is the human identity's, and continuity stated for every writing claim
     with the head carried in the claim response.
   - **Paths:** a record course's changed paths limited to its ticket, new ticket files and
     declared outputs, checked at the close and at the arrival.
   - **Closed tickets:** a ticket closed in this run and waiting on a fact leg stays visible
     until its arrival, and `final_station` is rewritten when the arrival applies.
   - **Plain git:** a defined `pr-merged` reading, or `adopt` only.
2. **Build it** in the fact reader (B-140), the sync, the CLI's close and `baton owner route`.

### Tests

The scenario rows the amendment writes, covering each point above for `create-pr` and for one
record course.
