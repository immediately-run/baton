**Brief:** the owner, 2026-10-30: "human attestation and instrument readings can also be
generalized into a single thing", and "Why not leave this up to the course to decide what the gate
should be for the leg? It could actually depend on a reading if we want (eg: account funds)."
Recorded in `spec/BATON_SPEC_HISTORY.md` (baton#145).
**Spec:** R-BTN-181 (attestations: shape, credential-derived writers, request authentication,
supersession, the prerequisite, standard types, records not git), R-BTN-76 (readings as attestations about an instance, read per
repository; requirements on them deferred, item 6), R-BTN-75 (block kinds, `binds`), R-BTN-120 (prerequisites), §6.6 rule 1, R-BTN-88
(export), R-BTN-188's `attestations` and `readings` knobs; §13 `attestations`, `ticket_blocks`,
`instance_readings` (deferred, item 6); §14 the attestation routes (the reading routes deferred); S-BTN-181, S-BTN-24, G-BTN-70.
**Depends on:** B-145 (the policy's `attestations` and `readings` entries say whose count).
**Used by:** B-213 (the attestations read and screen list this store).

### Requires

None: a checkout and a terminal.

### Why

Work attestations let a leg require evidence under repository policy. The November 19 ruling
cut the resource prerequisite and block, which [B-291](B-291.mdx) removes with its code, and kept
readings as advice (R-BTN-76); the unbuilt `requires.resources` short form is not part of this work.

### What remains to build

1. **Store and routes.** Use the work-attestation shape, request authentication, nonce
   idempotency, revocation and attested-key supersession in the
   [security proposal](../spec/proposals/security.md) and its
   [tables and routes](../spec/proposals/data-model-and-api.md). Bind by subject, not by use
   count. Do not add record signatures, a second signing key or consumption state. Preserve the
   built person-answer path. Keep the writer classes data, not code: #449's proposals add a
   writer `person` (a person's interactive grant;
   [ticket ownership](../spec/proposals/ticket-ownership.md)), a content hash in
   `std:approval@1`'s value, and `std:notified@1`
   ([personal secrets](../spec/proposals/personal-secrets.md)). They are proposals, not this
   ticket's work, but the store must take them without a migration of its shape.
2. **Standard work records.** Record `std:wrote@1` with the range and runner key, and
   `std:review@1` only for a run that finishes at the review leg's approving outcome. Use the
   subject Baton holds, not a caller-supplied subject. A review names its run id and only
   supersedes a review of the same run (R-BTN-63, R-BTN-183).
3. **Work requirements.** Evaluate `requires.attestations` on a `work` subject only (item 6
   defers `subject: instance` and the `requires.resources` short form) per the security proposal, including
   `where`, `count`, `distinct`, `binds` and `missing`. Course writers may only narrow policy.
   Store unmet requirements as `attestation` rows; rule 1 returns the blocking rows when
   refusing a claim or advance. The validator refuses a trusted leg whose requirement binds as
   advice or takes `missing: allow`. Keep `isEligible` in Baton's shared source in parity with
   rule 1 (R-BTN-37); consumers pin Baton, not the reverse.
4. **Export.** Export work records with owner state and drop them on import (R-BTN-88).
5. **Sync.** Supersede facts by type and subject across credentials authorized to post those
   facts. Reconcile the proposal's machine-key supersession text with R-BTN-203 before changing
   who can post fact arrivals or outside-work evidence. Permission to post a board is not, by
   itself, permission to post those facts.
6. **Readings: deferred (the owner, 2026-11-26).** B-291 owns removal of the existing fleet
   advice prerequisite and block. The `requires.attestations` instance form (`subject: instance`,
   a requirement on readings) is not built here: it stays on the proposal page, with S-BTN-181's
   funds, `count: 2` and latest-per-writer cases, until a course needs it, and is ruled on then.
   Do not add a reading signature, a reading nonce, or a `signed` policy knob. A leg that should
   wait on a reading (a rate limit, host memory) does so through its course's selector
   ([B-351](B-351.mdx)), not through a requirement core evaluates.
