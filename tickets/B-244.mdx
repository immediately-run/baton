**What:** a runner that claims nothing says so, and says why.

Failed-check attribution depends on B-245. A generic missing capability is not evidence of a
failed check: it may never have been declared or may be outside the grant.

- **The runner (`src/runner/runner.ts`, `runOnce`).** Today a `no-eligible-ticket` or `not-eligible` refusal
  returns `null` and the loop idles without a line. Log the refusal once when the runner goes idle
  (`baton: idle — <code>: <message>`), stay quiet while the answer is unchanged, and log again when it changes
  or a claim ends the idle stretch (`baton: idle for <duration>, ended by claim <id>`).
- **The reason (the core, `recommendations` / `next`).** `no-eligible-ticket` gets a summary of why the
  ready set was emptied for this worker, from what the recommendation already computes: per gate, the count
  of the repository's ready legs it removed. Gates: tier cap (the declared ceiling and the grant's
  `max_tier`, named apart), `attempts-exhausted` holds (a `baton:retries` decision open), unconfirmed ends,
  blocks, rule 4 (naming, for this runner, each required capability it stopped declaring after a failing
  check, B-245), rule 8 bars, backoff, `awaiting-sync`, a `resource` block from an `unavailable` reading
  (with its writer and `until`), a set that isn't current. Counts and up to five ticket
  ids per gate, only for tickets the worker's key may see ([BATON_SPEC §6.7](../spec/BATON_SPEC/06-prerequisites-blocks-advice.md#sec-6-7) [R-BTN-99](../spec/BATON_SPEC/06-prerequisites-blocks-advice.md#sec-6-7)). It's sent in the refusal's `detail`, and
  an older runner ignores it.
- **The runner's idle line names it.** For example: `baton: idle — no ready ticket fits this worker: 37 above
  the grant's max_tier judgement, 13 held attempts-exhausted (baton:retries open: B-193, B-176, …), 1 unconfirmed end`.
- **A runner that has been idle past a threshold (default 30 min) while the reason says work is held** raises
  the repository's owner notification once per episode, through the same channel as `unblocked_trigger`. It
  isn't raised again for that repository while the episode lasts. An idle alert is its own dispatch
  with `tickets: []`; no unblock is reported, and the existing workflow input schema is unchanged.

**Why:** on 2026-11-15 both bering runners (workers 42, 43) and vltava2's runner sat idle for about six
hours with healthy heartbeats and 51 ready tickets on the board. Every ticket a `judgement` grant could reach
was on hold with `attempts-exhausted` (13 open `baton:retries` decisions) or held by an unconfirmed end
(B-175, claim 185). The other 37 were critical or untiered, above the grant's `max_tier`. Nothing said so:
the runner logs nothing on `no-eligible-ticket`, the board's `ready` list doesn't apply holds or the grant's
cap, and `baton` has no owner channel (`needs_you.no_owner_channel`: 129 decisions). Finding the cause took
the admin state read, the owner's grants read, and source reading. The V2 UI work (behind B-193) waited the
whole time.

**Done when:** a scenario test with one ready ticket above the grant's tier and one held `attempts-exhausted`
shows the runner logging one idle line naming both gates, no further line on the next idle beats, a new line
when the hold is retried and the claim lands, and the owner notification raised once after the threshold. An
older runner against the new core, and the new runner against an older core (no `detail`), both still work.

### Trust

The reason only counts what the worker's key could already read through the board and its own refusals.
Ticket ids are limited to what R-BTN-99 lets that key see, and no other key's holdings or grants are named.
