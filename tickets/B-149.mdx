**Brief:** the owner, 2026-10-31, in a design session on Fieldhouse's courses, on being shown that
the marker courses cannot run under the strong policy: "This seems like a pretty serious issue.
Please address this now."

**Spec:** `spec/BATON_SPEC.md` §5 R-BTN-131 (*The marker courses*: each leg commits its work to the
default branch); §12.8 R-BTN-188 (`refs.default_branch`, `merge.*`, `tickets.created`,
`leg_state`), R-BTN-180 (*Nothing requires a runner the refs exclude to write the default branch*),
R-BTN-183 (what the merge runner merges), R-BTN-189 (`no-runner-lands`, `independent-review`,
`signed-authorship`), R-BTN-190 (leg state); R-BTN-39, R-BTN-83, R-BTN-10 (positions, holds,
confirmation); R-BTN-116 (person legs); R-BTN-165 (call legs); R-BTN-168 (cancel).

**Depends on:** B-120 (course history in Baton) and B-140 (the merge runner). The person legs the
gates sit on come from B-7, B-41 and B-62 where they are not built yet: item 1 names them in the
course table either way.

### Why

The five marker courses cannot run on a home whose default branch is protected.

- **The code.** Every marker leg's `close` commits the ticket on the default branch and pushes it
  (`src/defaults/ticket-state.ts`, `PUSH_TRIES`), retrying only a refusal because the branch moved.
  Each leg also commits its own work there under its `## <leg>` heading; `build-app` writes
  `product/<ticket>/`; `bug-report`, `draft-ticket` and `spec-to-tickets` file new ticket files.
- **The spec.** Under `merge.trusted`, "no course commits to the default branch" (R-BTN-188,
  R-BTN-180). R-BTN-180 routes what a runner's action *creates* through proposals, but gives no
  route for a marker leg's record, `build-app`'s documents, or the tickets these courses file.
  `create-spec`'s `converse` and `address` no longer write frontmatter (B-120 moves their state to
  course history), so they need nothing here.
- **The ticket that held it.** B-130 item 4: "No course writes main directly …", with the exit
  criterion "the standard courses file tickets by PR; no course commits to a default branch".
  B-130 was superseded on 2026-10-31 without it. This ticket takes item 4 back.
- **Who it hits.** Under the `protected` and `protected-signed` presets every marker leg fails at
  its close once the rulesets `baton repo protect` prints are applied. FLDH-42 applies them to
  `fieldhouse`, `docs` and `baton`; docs runs `bug-report`, `draft-ticket` and `decision`.

### What the owner has decided

- **2026-10-31:** a marker course's PR touching only ticket and output paths merges on the owner's
  `std:approval@1` alone, replacing `merge.reviews`. Where `refs.default_branch` admits `runner`,
  the courses keep writing the default branch with no approval.
- **2026-11-01**, each the recommended option:
  - `decision`'s packet approval carries the answer: one act, not two;
  - a `build-app` plan's children land in the plan's change, under its one approval;
  - no agent-filed change, a status-only one included, lands without the owner's approval.
- **2026-11-01:** part 4 is split out of immediately-run/baton#150 into this ticket's own spec PR,
  after two drafts and two review rounds there.

### What to build

1. **The spec amendment first**, its own PR, with two fresh reviewers and the impact list (the
   babysit skill's *Spec PRs*). The second draft (immediately-run/baton#150 at `449b07f`, its
   history entries kept in `spec/BATON_SPEC_HISTORY.md`) is the starting text. Its shape:
   - **One branch per ticket.** Records and outputs go on `baton/ticket/<id>`, in place of the
     default branch, under the same leg instructions.
   - **Gates.** A course declares them per leg (`lands`). A gated arrival is held
     (`next_source: merge`) until the owner's human approval covers the head.
   - **The merge.** A built-in trusted leg, `baton.ticket-merge`, merges the change. Only courses
     the policy's `merge.ticket_courses` lists may land this way.
   - **The owner's actions.** The approval lists the owner's own commits (`owner_commits`). The
     owner sends a change back with `route` and rejects it with an owner cancel.

   The amendment must also close every finding of the second draft's review round
   (2026-11-01):
   - **(a) Confirmation deadlock.** A gated end is never confirmed under R-BTN-10, since
     confirmation waits for git to show the ticket done, and the merge cannot be claimed while it
     is unconfirmed. Decide whether Baton owns closure on this route (amending R-BTN-35 and
     R-BTN-10) or the merge writes `status: done` as a templated change.
   - **(b) A plain answer at a gated person leg.** It must be refused while the gate is open, and
     `merge` added to R-BTN-39's deferral list.
   - **(c) A push after an approval.** The approval moves the attested head to its own head,
     owner commits included, and a later foreign commit is reset to that head without voiding
     the approval. *Continuity* must then hold for the next writing leg.
   - **(d) `owner_commits` signed by an owner key**, checked by the merge runner, or
     `signed-authorship`'s guarantee text amended to name the exception.
   - **(e) A cancel racing a claimed merge.** Consuming the approval must refuse while the ticket
     is cancelling.
   - **(f) After the merge.** Post-merge filings and outputs (`prevent`'s decision ticket,
     `verify`'s fix tickets and `VERIFY.md`) need a landing path: a second gate on the same branch,
     or a kind whose deliverer copies blobs by id.
   - **(g) Policy changes.** Refuse a policy that strands an open ticket branch (its course
     dropped from `merge.ticket_courses`, no merge runner, no human key), and define a `lands`
     leg on a ticket with no branch.
   - **(h) `independent-review` honesty.** Its *Holds when* must cover `merge.ticket_courses`, or
     `outputs` must be limited to a non-code class.
   - **(i) `build-app`'s children.** Baton derives the `spawned` set from the landed children,
     never from the runner's list.
   - **(j) Name the person legs and their stations** in R-BTN-131's table. Specify route-back as
     a station reset bounded by `max_runs`, `prepare`'s `settled` edge, a validator rule that
     every path to an end crosses a merge gate after the last branch write, and
     `baton.ticket-merge`'s requirements and `close` action as R-BTN-69 specifies `baton.cancel`.
   - **(k) Scenario gaps:**
     - who sees a merge or close by hand;
     - refusal reasons for a missing or non-human approval and for a cancelled ticket;
     - R-BTN-183 *How*'s "returns to the leg the course names" against "stays held";
     - a case for each new refusal;
     - the post-merge record under `leg_state: forge`.
2. **Refuse it before it runs** (with B-145). The course validator accepts `records`
   (`default-branch` | `policy`) and the amendment's gate fields. The policy validator refuses
   `unlandable-course`, naming each course, and the sync's registry post refuses each such course,
   with `baton.course-refused` naming the policy version.
3. **A refused close ends the claim with its own reason.** A push the forge refuses for protection
   (not because the branch moved) is never retried. It ends `released:cannot-finish:policy` naming
   the ref, and raises `baton.unlandable` once per repository. If the policy tightened during the
   claim, the leg is queued again on the new route instead.
4. **The route itself**, as the amendment states it, for the five marker courses.
5. **Migration.** A home whose policy tightens while marker tickets are in flight places each one
   from its frontmatter state once (B-120's `baton owner ticket adopt`). A ticket mid-course
   resumes at its station with no leg re-run.
6. **The docs.** `docs/standard-courses.mdx`: *How each course finds where a ticket is* and
   *`build-app`, step by step* gain the route. `docs/security-model.mdx` lists the marker courses
   among what `no-runner-lands` changes.

### Do not

- Push the default branch from any runner leg where `refs.default_branch` excludes `runner`,
  including a "status-only" frontmatter write.
- Let a ticket merge cover a path outside the ticket folder and the course's outputs, or land a
  head no human approval covers.
- Reach a course's end before its change lands.
- Retry a protection refusal, or report it as a moved branch.
- Copy the marker courses into Fieldhouse or any other home as hardened variants: Baton's standard
  courses must run under Baton's own strongest preset.
