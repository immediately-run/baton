**Brief:** filed from the ticket plan for the current-state spec (PR #71). It follows the schema
review of 2026-10-20 (`spec/BATON_SPEC_HISTORY.md`, "Schema review", items 6, 8, 10, 11 and 12),
which the owner approved. It replaces the per-leg deadline fields `max_ready_s` and
`max_attempts_s`, which were never built.
**Spec:** `spec/BATON_SPEC.md` §7.5 R-BTN-167 (shape, when each clock starts, interrupting and
non-interrupting timers, core's default timer, choice stations); R-BTN-90 (the `leg-timeout`
release and reap, which B-60 built); R-BTN-122 (§7.4, `budget-spent`, the owners' ceiling, the
reset when a leg reaches an outcome); R-BTN-163 (§7.3, `baton.stalled` and its restarts); R-BTN-83
(§5.3, held positions); R-BTN-88 (§4.8, owner state); R-BTN-64 (§5.7, renames carry timers);
R-BTN-69 (§5.2, the validator); §13.6 `ticket_timers` and `ticket_overrides.override_kind`; §15
S-BTN-10, S-BTN-37, S-BTN-87, S-BTN-134, G-BTN-64, G-BTN-152. R-BTN-64's per-leg rewrite comes from
the schema review (`spec/BATON_SPEC_HISTORY.md`, "Schema review (2026-10-20)", item 9).
**Depends on:**
- B-60 (the `start` timer, `deadline_at`, and the `leg-timeout` release and reap);
- B-11 (`leg_attempts.first_attempt_at`, exhaustion and `budget-spent`, the owners' bounds);
- B-38 (held positions with their kind);
- B-56 (`baton.stalled` as a `suspend` core condition).

B-11 and B-38 add `leg_attempts` and the held position's `route_leg`/`route_station`; this ticket
lands after both, so item 8 rewrites every per-leg table in one place and neither of them needs a
rename path of its own.

### Requires

None: every case runs in the integration harness with a fake clock.

### Why

A leg that is ready but nobody takes, or one that keeps failing past a total budget, has no
bound today. The 72-hour `baton.stalled` scan in `src/core/blocks.ts` is one hard-coded timer. This
ticket makes it one row among a leg's timers, which a course can tune.

### What to build

1. **The validator** (`src/core/course.ts`, the kit), lifting B-60's interim refusals and
   refusing, each naming the leg and the field:
   - an `after_s` outside 60 to 2,592,000, or above the owners' ceiling for an interrupting
     `start` or `first_attempt` timer;
   - an interrupting timer with `raise`, or one whose `leg` does not leave the same station;
   - an interrupting timer with `leg` on a leg leaving a choice station. Whichever of B-61 and
     B-63 lands second adds this check;
   - a non-interrupting timer with `leg`, or one without a `raise` that is a `notify` type or
     `baton.stalled`;
   - a second `baton.stalled` timer on a leg;
   - `max_ready_s` and `max_attempts_s`. Nothing ever posted them, so they get no window.
2. **Migration** (a new numbered file):
   - `ticket_timers` exactly as §13.6;
   - `timer` in `ticket_stations.next_source`;
   - B-38's `override_kind` gains `timer` if B-38 did not add it.
3. **Arming** (a new `src/core/timers.ts`, called from the transactions that change each `from`):
   - `arrival`: when the ticket's station changes, never when Baton moves it.
   - `ready`: when the leg becomes ready and unclaimed.
   - `first_attempt`: at `leg_attempts.first_attempt_at`, re-armed when B-11 resets it.
   - `start` stays B-60's `deadline_at`.

   Each timer fires once per `from_at` (`fired_at`). The alarm scans `due_at`, as R-BTN-90's
   alarm does. Renames carry the rows (R-BTN-64), and the owner-state export carries them
   (R-BTN-88).
4. **Interrupting timers:**
   - The alarm releases a running claim on the leg with `leg-timeout`, through B-60's path.
   - With `leg`, Baton queues that leg (`next_source: timer`), held as B-38's override until the
     station changes. Its own run writes the position to git. In this case the release is not a
     failed attempt, which also applies to B-60's `start` timer.
   - Without `leg`, the firing is a failed attempt.
   - A `first_attempt` timer without `leg` spends the budget: no further attempt starts, and the
     ticket goes to B-11's exhaustion with `budget-spent`.
5. **Non-interrupting timers:**
   - Baton raises the timer's `raise` on the ticket, once per timer per arrival, and the leg goes
     on.
   - A course's `notify` type is raised by core with no claim.
   - `{from: "ready", raise: "baton.ready-late"}` covers a leg that is ready but untaken.
6. **Core's default timer:**
   - Every leg carries `{after_s: 259200, from: "arrival", interrupting: false, raise:
     "baton.stalled"}`.
   - It counts only while the sync posts the leg `waiting`, not while Baton holds it waiting or a
     `time` wait holds it.
   - A leg's own `baton.stalled` timer replaces the default on that leg. Nothing removes the
     default.
   - It replaces `src/core/blocks.ts`'s 72-hour scan and its constant.
   - B-56's `retry` restart re-arms it. `baton.stalled` still closes by itself when the leg is
     posted `ready`.
7. **The model** (R-BTN-68): if `model/baton.qnt` cites R-BTN-167 or the stall rule it replaces,
   change it in this PR with its `npm run model:pr` report.
8. **Every per-leg record follows a rename** (R-BTN-64, schema review item 9). Today
   `applyRegistry` in `src/core/baton.ts` calls `renameActive` in `src/core/claims.ts`, which
   rewrites only active claims' `station` and `leg`. Beside it, in the same transaction, add one
   rewrite that maps every old station or leg name (the graph's `legRenames` and
   `stationRenames`) to its new one in:
   - `leg_attempts` (attempts, `first_attempt_at`, `retry_after`, B-11);
   - `ticket_timers` (armed timers, item 3);
   - `ticket_blocks` (a `retry` backoff and every other per-leg block);
   - `leg_needs` and `ticket_next_legs`;
   - `ticket_bars`. These are the posted bars; owner bars still do not follow renames
     (R-BTN-142);
   - `ticket_overrides.route_leg` and `route_station` (a held position of any kind, B-38).

   Runs keep counting toward `max_runs` under the resolved name, and attempts, backoffs, armed
   timers, blocks and held positions carry over unchanged.

### Do not

- Restart an `arrival` instant on a Baton move (R-BTN-88).
- Fire a timer twice for one `from` instant.
- Let a timer raise `baton.stalled` unless it is the leg's one override.
- Count a `time` wait or a Baton-held wait toward the default timer.

### Tests

- On both hosts:
  - `test/integration/S-BTN-87.timers-attempts.test.ts`:
    - an interrupting `first_attempt` timer that a backoff would pass, held `budget-spent`;
    - a `ready` timer raising `baton.ready-late` once;
    - a looped leg's second pass budgeted from its own first attempt.
  - `S-BTN-134.timers.test.ts`:
    - an interrupting arrival timer with `leg`: `leg-timeout`, and the leg queued;
    - `warning.slow` raised once per arrival;
    - a 1-day `baton.stalled` override raising after 1 day, not 3;
    - every refused form in item 1, each named, with the previous file kept.

    The scenario's other refusals belong to other tickets: `error_types` is B-55's, `isolation`
    is B-70's and `max_duration` is B-67's. A leg's `call`, `checks` and `retry.non_retryable`
    are refused as unknown keys and need only an assertion.
  - `S-BTN-10.no-review-capacity.test.ts`: `baton.stalled` at 72 hours from the default timer.
  - `S-BTN-37.course-renames.test.ts`: a rename of a leg with an attempt, a backoff, an armed
    timer and a held position, each carried over under the new name (item 8).
- Unit: the `arrival` instant kept across a Baton move and restarted on a station change; the
  default not counting a `time` wait; item 8's rewrite of each table under a leg rename and a
  station rename.
