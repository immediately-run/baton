**Brief:** the coverage audit of the tickets against the spec (2026-10-20) found the adversarial
review's fixes to fences, claims and cutover adoption (spec/BATON_SPEC_HISTORY.md, "Adversarial
review (2026-10-20)", adv-3 F1, F2, F10–F14; adv-4 M5) ticketed nowhere, and B-60's text still
clearing a `leg-timeout` fence on the runner's silence. This ticket builds them.
**Spec:** `spec/BATON_SPEC.md` §9.5 R-BTN-19 (`fence_deadline`, `baton.fence-pending`,
`session`-mode reaps) and reaping; §7.5 R-BTN-90 (the `leg-timeout` reap clears at the fence's
deadline); §8.1 R-BTN-9 (the lowest declared timeout) and R-BTN-14 (a re-key never clears a
fence); §9.2 R-BTN-6, R-BTN-26 (what the old incarnation computes); §9.8 R-BTN-24 (the resume
record) and R-BTN-25 (continuing a branch); §9.1 (idempotency per runner, the public nonce);
§8.4 R-BTN-144 (the nonce is not masked); §8.3 R-BTN-107 (`session` mode); §16.1 R-BTN-28
(adoption requests, legacy runners); §13.3 `runners`, §13.5 `claims` (`fence_deadline`,
`request_id` unique per runner) and `adoption_requests`; §14 `POST /v1/runner/adopt`; §15 S-BTN-4,
S-BTN-16, G-BTN-2, G-BTN-14, G-BTN-19.
**Depends on:** B-60 (the `leg-timeout` reap, whose clearing this ticket replaces with
`fence_deadline`); B-47 (`session`-mode legs, whose reaps become fence-pending); B-57
(`baton.<sub>` conditions).

### Requires

None: every case runs in the integration harness with a fake clock and a fake provider.

### Why

The adversarial review found that a fence could stay pending forever: after a force-reap, a
sidecar that lost its state file re-keys, the new incarnation's heartbeats keep `last_seen_at`
fresh, the old secret can no longer report `fenced`, and the ticket is never claimable again with
nothing on the dashboard saying why. A `session`-mode child has no sidecar or hooks, so a silence
reap left it pushing unfenced. A heartbeat that lowers the lease timeout and loses its response
let Baton reap on a shorter lease than the sidecar fenced on. Resume records missed late closes
and grew without bound, a prior claimant could steer its successor onto another ticket's branch,
and another runner's colliding `request_id` was handed someone else's claim. At cutover, the first
runner to name a legacy session adopted its claim with no one checking.

### What to build

1. **Migration** (one new numbered file in `src/migrations/`): `claims.fence_deadline`; the
   unique index on `claims.request_id` replaced by one on `(runner_id, request_id)`;
   `adoption_requests (claim_id, runner_id)` with `requested_at` and `confirmed_at`, replacing the
   current adoption rows; `(hostname, session_name)` uniqueness applying to every runner but a
   `legacy` one.
2. **Fences that clear** (`src/core/claims.ts`, the alarm):
   - Every reap of a live runner's claim — the force-reap, the unacknowledged-release reap and
     B-60's `leg-timeout` reap — sets `fence_pending`, `fence_incarnation` and `fence_deadline` (the
     reap instant plus the runner's `lease_timeout_ms`) and sets no `reaped_at`.
   - The fence clears when that incarnation's sidecar reports `fenced: {claim_id, terminated:
     true}` (`fence-cleared`), or when the alarm reaches `fence_deadline`, whatever the runner did
     meanwhile — a re-key, a new incarnation's heartbeats, a heartbeat that cannot terminate
     (`fence-cleared {deadline: true}`). A re-key never clears it.
   - A fence still pending 30 minutes after the reap raises `baton.fence-pending` on the ticket,
     naming the runner and the deadline; clearing closes it.
   - `fence_deadline` is an alarm instant (§13's list) and shows on the ticket's row.
3. **`session`-mode reaps** (`src/core/claims.ts`, `src/runner/runner.ts`): every reap of a claim
   running a `session`-mode leg, a silence reap included, is fence-pending. The runner reports
   `fenced` for it only once the provider confirms the child session interrupted or archived;
   otherwise the fence clears at `fence_deadline`, and the child's later markers are noticed as
   `foreign-write` (B-78).
4. **The lowest timeout** (`src/runner/state.ts`, `src/runner/runner.ts`): the state file keeps the
   lowest `lease_timeout_s` in force at `last_ok` and declared since, and every fence check
   (R-BTN-6, R-BTN-26) computes on it.
5. **Resume records** (`src/runner/runner.ts`, `src/core/workerApi.ts`, `src/core/claims.ts`):
   - The runner drains its spool before it calls `end`.
   - A close accepted in the 10-minute grace that carries `resume` rewrites the claim's
     `resume_json`.
   - The claim response's `previous_attempts` holds the newest 10, newest first, each with its
     resume record, legs run and hand-off URL, plus `previous_attempts_total`.
6. **Continuing a branch** (`src/runner/runner.ts`): a branch the resume record names is continued
   only when it matches the course's current or previous `branch_pattern` for this ticket; any
   other is ignored and logged. A tainted branch is never continued.
7. **Idempotency per runner** (`src/core/claims.ts`): a repeated `request_id` returns the original
   claim only to the runner that sent it; another runner's same `request_id` is a new request.
8. **The public nonce** (R-BTN-144, §9.1): the state view (`IR_BATON_STATE`) carries the claim
   nonce, and the masking set leaves it out. B-47's state view and B-46's masking set follow this.
9. **Adoption** (`src/core/cutover.ts`, the routes in `src/core/baton.ts`, `src/ui/app.js`):
   - `POST /v1/runner/adopt {ticket_id}` from a runner whose `session_name` equals the legacy
     runner's records an `adoption_requests` row and answers `{pending: true}` until confirmed.
   - `POST /v1/ui/adoptions/{claim}/confirm {runner_id}` confirms one request by an administrator,
     through the dashboard's confirmation (R-BTN-155); the dashboard lists each requester's
     hostname and session name. The confirmed runner's next `adopt` gets the claim; every other
     requester is refused `forbidden`.
   - The cutover import makes one legacy runner per ticket, sharing a `session_name` where one
     session held several git claims; lease expiry skips `legacy` runners, whose claims end only
     at `reap_after`.
10. **The model** (R-BTN-68): in `model/baton.qnt`, `fence_deadline` clearing every fence whatever
    the runner does, `session`-mode reaps fenced, and adoption only after a confirmed request; the
    property that every fence clears by its deadline. Attach the `npm run model:pr` report.

### Do not

- Let anything a runner does after the reap — a re-key, a heartbeat, a new incarnation — extend
  or clear a fence before its deadline but the old incarnation's own `fenced` report (G-BTN-14).
- Set `reaped_at` on a fence reap; the runner must still be able to heartbeat and report.
- Report `fenced` for a `session`-mode leg on the runner's word alone.
- Hand a claim to a runner on its `session_name` alone.
- Treat the nonce as a secret anywhere.
- Edit an old migration.
