**Brief:** the owner, 2026-10-17: "I would like the Baton CLI to have a Git-like command, subcommand hierarchy where, for example, all the commands that pertain to a given leg of a ticket would have the prefix baton leg … baton leg log would give you the logs produced by the leg of a given ticket." Asked two questions, the owner chose to save each leg run's output now, and to rename the old commands outright.
**Spec:** `spec/BATON_SPEC.md`, new §2m.6d: R-BTN-127 (the command tree) and R-BTN-128 (a leg run's output saved to git, and `baton leg log`). It also renames the old commands in place throughout the spec, with dated notes.

### Why

The CLI mixes role namespaces (`baton admin …`, `baton owner …`) with top-level verbs (`baton why`, `baton replay`, `baton test-course`). And a finished leg has no log anywhere.

### What to do

1. Add §2m.6d:
   - R-BTN-127: the noun and verb tree, how legs are named, the channel verbs working only inside a leg, the old names refused with their replacements, help, and `--json`;
   - R-BTN-128: the archive in the transcript repos, its bounds and masking, what is not saved, `baton leg log`, and the CLI's stream upgrade.
2. Rename the old commands throughout the spec, add dated notes to the rules they belong to, add the wire rows, the §4 table and the settings row, add S-BTN-97 to S-BTN-99 and G-BTN-117 to G-BTN-119, and add a Decisions entry and a CHANGELOG row.
3. Review it under the review gate.
