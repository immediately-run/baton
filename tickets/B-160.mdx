**Brief:** the owner, 2026-11-01, on the whole-spec review they commissioned ("assess it for …
can any vocabulary, core concepts or actors be simplified … is the user experience intuitive …
are the security guarantees … truly guaranteed and … do they matter"), asked for its findings
to be filed as tickets. Source: `docs/review/spec-simplification-review-2026-11-01.mdx`.
The review's claims are a reviewer's, checked against the spec where they cite it; this ticket
verifies each against the text before changing anything.

### Why

Review finding 4 and F9: each mechanism is reasonable alone, together they are "the largest
single source of actors and knobs after §12.8 itself". The review also finds Baton's own
notification pipeline (section 1.3: handler entry, courier, ticket file, sync, `announce`,
`withdraw`) could be a delivery runner polling `GET …/conditions`.

### Decide first (the owner)

"Baton's server is trusted; its compromise is recovered, not resisted" (the review's suggestion), or reach 0 becomes a named guarantee. B-152 (just confirmed) depends on this.

### What to build

1. **A spec PR first**, under the babysit skill's *Spec PRs*: two fresh reviewers, the impact list,
   every rule restating the same fact changed with it.
2. One sentence in §12.8 and R-BTN-189 stating the decision.
3. If out of scope: remove signed outbox entries and `GET /v1/keys/signing`, the dispatch
   courier (with B-159), signed policy reads and policy-hash pins, and `base_sha` roll-back
   checks, with the tickets that build them rewritten.
4. If in scope: a guarantee row for it in R-BTN-189, each mechanism citing it.
