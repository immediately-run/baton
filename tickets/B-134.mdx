**Brief:** the owner, 2026-10-27, OWASP agentic mapping, hole 7 (ASI10 rogue agents): nothing
checks that a leg's actions stay within its ticket. A leg on a `fieldhouse` ticket can push to any
repository the worker can write, open PRs elsewhere, or reach any allowlisted host, and Baton sees
only its heartbeats.

**Spec:** R-BTN-106 (the runner checks every move), R-BTN-133 (a leg's checkout), R-BTN-140
(`code_repos`), R-BTN-81 and R-BTN-85 (history and discrepancies); `docs/security-model.mdx`
(OWASP mapping, hole 7). Per-claim tokens (the broker ticket filed with this one) prevent the
forge half; this ticket detects the rest and covers the time before them.
**Depends on:** none.

### What to build

1. **The leg's scope** is its ticket's home and `repos:` within `code_repos`.
2. **The runner audits what the leg did**, at the leg's end, from evidence it does not get from
   the agent: the refs pushed (the runner's git wrapper or the forge's push deliveries for the
   worker identity in the leg's window), PRs opened or commented on, and the VM's egress log where
   Sand Pit exports one. Each action outside the scope is reported with the claim
   (`scope-exceeded`), raised under Needs you, and, with the owners' `scope_policy: hold`, holds
   the ticket.
3. **Baton cross-checks** what the forge reports for the worker identity against the claims open
   at the time: a push by the worker to a repository no active claim covers is `unclaimed-write`.

### Tests

- A scenario (the next free S-BTN number): a leg on a `fieldhouse` ticket that pushes a branch to
  `docs` is reported `scope-exceeded`; with `scope_policy: hold` the ticket is held; a worker push
  when no claim is open is `unclaimed-write`.

### Exit criteria

- [ ] the audit runs at every leg end; the scenario passes on both hosts
