**Spec:** R-BTN-19 (*fence before re-issue*), §9.5 (*Reaping*), R-BTN-9 (the lease timeout's
clamp), R-BTN-26 (the sidecar's own fence), R-BTN-178 (seats held until `fence-cleared`), §12.2
(destructive actions), G-BTN-14, S-BTN-4, S-BTN-21, S-BTN-50. Source:
`docs/review/security-review-2026-11-02.mdx`, finding S3, checked against the text in PR #165.

### Why

A claim reaped while its runner was alive — a force-reap, an unacknowledged-release reap, a
`leg-timeout` reap — is fence-pending until that incarnation reports `fenced` or until
`fence_deadline`, the reap plus the runner's declared `lease_timeout_ms`, which R-BTN-9 clamps to
at most 86,400 s. `baton.fence-pending` at 30 minutes is a notice, not a control; rule 3 refuses
every claim meanwhile; adopt and force-reap act only on active claims; the registry has no
fence action. So an administrator who force-reaps a hung laptop runner, and knows the machine is
off, waits up to a day for the ticket, and its capacity seats stay held as long (R-BTN-178). A
hostile runner turns its own reap into a day's hold on the ticket.

### Decided (the owner)

2026-11-02, choosing among this ticket's options: "I accept your recommendations" — option (a),
an administrator's fence clear; the deadline stays. The spec states it (R-BTN-19 *An
administrator's clear*, §9.5, R-BTN-155's confirmed list, §12.2, §14, G-BTN-14; S-BTN-4), PR
`claude/baton-spec-review-2fwiga`.

### What to build

1. The registry action `claim.fence-clear` (administrator only, destructive, never delegated) and
   its route `POST /v1/ui/claims/{id}/fence-clear`, confirmed like force-reap: it clears the
   fence in one transaction, frees the claim's seats (R-BTN-178) and logs
   `fence-cleared {admin: <fingerprint>}`; a claim whose fence is not pending is `conflict`.
2. The dashboard control, drawn only for administrators, on the claim wherever the dashboard
   shows it and on its `baton.fence-pending` condition in Needs you, with the dialog R-BTN-19
   describes (last-seen time; what the clear gives up, the `session`-mode case included); the
   clear resolves that condition. It is its own action, never a condition restart.
3. A freeze does not refuse the clear, so a seal waiting on a fence-pending claim (S-BTN-50)
   proceeds after it.
