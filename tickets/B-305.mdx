**Brief:** the owner, 2026-11-20, asked "Where should the information of who should be able to
eg: approve a particular leg of a course be persisted?" and took the recommendation: the course
names a label, the repository's policy says who holds it. This is §4 of [the users and groups proposal](../spec/proposals/users-and-groups.md).

**Spec:** the proposal, §4, and its scenarios in §7. [BATON_SPEC §6.4](../spec/BATON_SPEC/06-prerequisites-blocks-advice.md#sec-6-4) ([R-BTN-116](../spec/BATON_SPEC/06-prerequisites-blocks-advice.md#sec-6-4)), [§12.8](../spec/BATON_SPEC/12-security.md#sec-12-8)
([R-BTN-181](../spec/BATON_SPEC/12-security.md#sec-12-8), [R-BTN-191](../spec/BATON_SPEC/12-security.md#sec-12-8), [R-BTN-188](../spec/BATON_SPEC/12-security.md#sec-12-8), [R-BTN-184](../spec/BATON_SPEC/12-security.md#sec-12-8), [R-BTN-185](../spec/BATON_SPEC/12-security.md#sec-12-8), [R-BTN-186](../spec/BATON_SPEC/12-security.md#sec-12-8)), [§5.3](../spec/BATON_SPEC/05-courses.md#sec-5-3) ([R-BTN-39](../spec/BATON_SPEC/01-what-baton-is.md#sec-1)), [§5.2](../spec/BATON_SPEC/05-courses.md#sec-5-2)

**Depends on:** B-304.

**Used by:** B-306 (an entry's recipients are its label's holders).

### Decision needed

- **Who answers where no label is named.** As built, `POST …/attestations` takes a
  `std:decision@1` from an owner key of the repository **or any human-only key of the tenant**
  (`src/core/baton.ts`, the attestations route; R-BTN-181). So a human who owns only repository
  A may answer a person leg on B. The proposal leaves this as built and lists it as open.
  **(a)** leave it, and document that a repository narrows by naming its answerers *(default)*;
  **(b)** with no entry, a human-only key must also be an owner of the repository or a
  break-glass key. (b) changes built behaviour for every repository on upgrade.

### What to build

1. **The knob** `answerers` in the policy document: `{<label>: [<entry>, …]}`, entries `owner`,
   `human` and B-304's four forms; validated, logged in a change's `changed[]`, kept by a
   preset's apply, written by no preset.
2. **The answer.** In `src/core/questions.ts` and the attestations route: after R-BTN-181's
   check as built, where the leg's label has an entry the credential must match it on the
   repository through B-304's match, owner or not; else `forbidden`. `answered_by` carries the
   key and, when attached, the user.
3. **A deferred answer** (R-BTN-39) is judged again when it applies; one whose credential no
   longer matches is `arrival-unapplied`.
4. **An answer in git** (the sync's, R-BTN-191): counts for a label with no entry or whose
   entry holds `owner`; otherwise `arrival-unapplied` and `baton.answer-unadmitted`.
5. **Refusals:** a policy giving a label an entry without `owner` while `std:decision@1`
   admits only `sync`; a policy change that sets the knob without naming every label a current
   course asks; a snapshot posting a course whose person leg asks an unnamed label once the
   knob is present (the previous course version stays in force); an entry no live key matches.
6. **The dashboard's Needs-you entry** offers its answer control only to a session whose key
   matches the label.

### Trust

- The `answerers` knob: an `approvers.policy` approver's request, as every knob.
- The `asks` label: the course file, which is git's (R-BTN-66), so anyone who can land a course
  can change a leg's label, drop the leg or add an `expires`. The proposal's *What the entry
  promises* says so, and the ticket must not present the knob as a boundary against a pusher:
  it is one only together with `paths.control`, and against an owner only where
  `approvers.policy` is no wider than the label.
- An answer: the credential that posted it, as built. A sync's answer names no credential and
  is never attributed to a person.

### Invariants

- BATON_SPEC [R-BTN-181](../spec/BATON_SPEC/12-security.md#sec-12-8): whose attestations count is the policy's to say, never the course's.
  The label's holders come from the policy alone.
- BATON_SPEC [R-BTN-116](../spec/BATON_SPEC/06-prerequisites-blocks-advice.md#sec-6-4): a leg waits only on an effect, and an answer is applied as an arrival
  under [R-BTN-39](../spec/BATON_SPEC/01-what-baton-is.md#sec-1)'s rules. This ticket narrows who may write the answer and changes nothing
  about how it applies.
- BATON_SPEC [R-BTN-186](../spec/BATON_SPEC/12-security.md#sec-12-8): a delegated key never answers a person's leg.
- BATON_SPEC [R-BTN-188](../spec/BATON_SPEC/12-security.md#sec-12-8): a stored policy version with no `answerers` narrows nothing, so no
  in-flight person leg changes hands at upgrade.
- BATON_SPEC [R-BTN-64](../spec/BATON_SPEC/05-courses.md#sec-5-7): an in-flight ticket survives a course change; a refused snapshot leaves
  the previous course version in force.

### Tests

The scenario *a person leg is answered by its label's holders*, both hosts, and *applying a
preset keeps who was named* (for `answerers`). Unit tests for each refusal of step 5.

### Exit criteria

- With `answerers: {release: [group:g]}`, an owner outside `g` is refused and an accepted
  member answers; the history's `question-answered` names the user.
- A member who answered while a claim was active and was then removed does not have the answer
  applied.
- A repository that never sets the knob answers person legs exactly as today.
- BATON_SPEC holds the amended [R-BTN-116](../spec/BATON_SPEC/06-prerequisites-blocks-advice.md#sec-6-4), [R-BTN-181](../spec/BATON_SPEC/12-security.md#sec-12-8) and [R-BTN-191](../spec/BATON_SPEC/12-security.md#sec-12-8).
