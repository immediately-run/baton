**Brief:** the owner, 2026-11-09 (B-191): "**Courses** `/courses` → `/courses/{c}` — station/leg
graph with queue depths, per-ticket paths, stats." The mockup's Courses page — per course a
header line (repository, course and version, runners at n, p50 over 7 days), the graph as
stations (circles, "235 resting") joined by legs ("implement, 1 on leg"; a leg marked ▲ when
blocked), and a table per leg (from → to, output, requires, timeout, retries, wait, run, fails
with a slow wait highlighted) — is the course page. The design brief's question 6 names what the
view answers.

**Spec:** [BATON_SPEC §11.4](../spec/BATON_SPEC/11-people.md#sec-11-4) (the Courses screens,
carrying the retired Courses view's content), [§5.2](../spec/BATON_SPEC/05-courses.md#sec-5-2)
(the course file; [R-BTN-69](../spec/BATON_SPEC/05-courses.md#sec-5-2): a refused file keeps the
previous in force), [§5.3](../spec/BATON_SPEC/05-courses.md#sec-5-3) ([R-BTN-83](../spec/BATON_SPEC/05-courses.md#sec-5-3):
a position and the queued leg's source), [§5.4](../spec/BATON_SPEC/05-courses.md#sec-5-4)
([R-BTN-79](../spec/BATON_SPEC/05-courses.md#sec-5-4): legs are queues, `max_queue`, intake),
[§6.2](../spec/BATON_SPEC/06-prerequisites-blocks-advice.md#sec-6-2) ([R-BTN-126](../spec/BATON_SPEC/06-prerequisites-blocks-advice.md#sec-6-2):
a prerequisite failing during a leg blocks the edge), [§7.4](../spec/BATON_SPEC/07-conditions.md#sec-7-4)
(retries and backoff), [§9.3](../spec/BATON_SPEC/09-claims.md#sec-9-3) (hand-offs),
[§8.5](../spec/BATON_SPEC/08-runners.md#sec-8-5) ([R-BTN-128](../spec/BATON_SPEC/08-runners.md#sec-8-5):
a leg's log opens from its edge, as text), [§14](../spec/BATON_SPEC/14-api.md#sec-14) (`courses`,
`stats`, `legs/{leg}/runs`, `blocked`). Context: `docs/context/design-brief.mdx` question 6.

**Depends on:** B-193 (the shell).

**Used by:** B-203.

### What to build

The §14 row first, then the code.

1. **`GET /v1/repositories/{repository}/courses/{course}`** (`course.show`, read; the repository
   reads' principals and a session): the course file as last accepted (its stations, legs with
   `from`, `to`, `kind`, `requires`, `max_runs`, `timeout`, `outputs` and `conditions` names,
   `max_queue`), its `version` and the refused newer version if one is listed under conditions
   (R-BTN-69); per leg its **queue** (`depth`, `max_queue`, `intake` open or closed, R-BTN-79),
   its **state** across the tickets on it — counts of ready, running, blocked (by block kind,
   and whether found before a start or during a run, R-BTN-126), backing off — and the leg's
   runs of the window (`?since=`, default 24 hours) as `legs/{leg}/runs` returns them; per
   station the tickets resting there; **tickets on the course** — each ticket's station, path
   (stations and legs run, with run ids), the live claim's legs since the claim, what a
   suspended one waits for (the station and the condition or prerequisite), its newest hand-off
   (the resume record's pointer, as text), its blocks, its queued leg and the leg's `source`
   (course, route, decision; R-BTN-83), parent and children ids; **off-graph** transitions logged
   in the window (§5's `off-graph`); and the course's **stats** as `GET …/stats` gives them,
   over the same window. Built from `courses.ts`, `stations.ts`, `queues.ts`, `blocks.ts`,
   `history.ts` and `stats.ts`, aggregates and ids only.
2. **`/repositories/{r}/courses`**: one card per course from `GET …/courses` and
   `course.show`'s header fields: course id and version, the mockup's header line, the graph
   drawn small (stations and legs with their depth and state), and the per-leg table (from → to,
   requires as chips, timeout, retries, wait, run, fails — the stats' medians and rates); a card
   opens the course page. A refused course file is a notice on its card, naming the version
   kept.
3. **`/repositories/{r}/courses/{course}`**: the graph drawn large as inline SVG from the course
   file — stations as nodes with their resting count, legs as edges with their state as shape
   and label first (▲ blocked, ● running, ○ ready, ↻ backing off) and colour second, never a
   station for a blocked leg (R-BTN-126); an edge's depth against `max_queue`, over as a marked
   edge and the intake state in text; clicking or focusing an edge opens its panel: the leg's
   fields, its runs of the window (run, runner, started, ended, end, session link under
   R-BTN-113, transcripts, `log_dir` as text with the `baton leg log <ticket> <leg>` line beside
   it, R-BTN-128), its blocked tickets with block kind and when found; clicking a station opens
   its tickets. Below: the tickets on the course as a `<table>` (ticket linking to its page,
   station, path drawn as the stations and legs run, the claim's legs since, waits-for,
   hand-off, blocks, queued leg and source, parent and children), the off-graph transitions,
   and the stats tables (per station and leg wait and run times, ends by reason and detail,
   suspensions, advice overridden) over `?since=` as a URL query. The accessible name and role
   on each `<g>` (the brief's accessibility rule), and the tables carry everything the drawing
   does.
4. **Course-agnostic.** Station, leg, tag and output names are data from the course file; the
   screen holds none of `roadmap-pr`'s or `create-pr`'s names (the brief).
5. **Phone.** The course page reads at 375 px: the graph scrolls horizontally inside its box,
   the tables stack.

### What not to do

- No control here: a course is changed by a commit (§5.7); a ticket's controls are on its page
  (B-194). The screen is read-only.
- No log bytes: a run's log is its directory, as text (R-BTN-128).
- No course validation in the app: a refused file is what the read says it is (R-BTN-69).
- No stats computed in the browser: wait, run and fail figures are `stats`' aggregates.

### Trust

- **`course.show`** — Baton's rows and the course file as last accepted (an opaque blob, §12.3:
  JSON drawn as text and as the graph's node and edge labels, every name a text node);
  hand-off pointers, transcripts and log directories as text; session links only where
  R-BTN-113 passed them.
- **`?since=`** — validated on the server.
- Nothing is written.

### Invariants

- **A blocked leg is a marked edge, never a station** (R-BTN-126).
- **Queue depth is advice** (R-BTN-79): over `max_queue` is a mark and a text, never a refusal
  drawn as one.
- **Positions are Baton's** (R-BTN-39): the paths shown are the course history.
- **Course-agnostic** (the brief); **text, never markup** (§12.3); **log directories as text**
  (R-BTN-128).
- **An owner's session, its repositories** (R-BTN-155).

### Tests

Scenarios numbered at merge, on both hosts, with a fixture course of four stations and three legs,
tickets resting at each, one leg's edge blocked by a prerequisite found during a run, one over
`max_queue`, one off-graph transition and a refused newer course file:

- `course.show` returns the graph, each leg's queue and state counts, the tickets with paths
  and sources, the off-graph row, the refused version and the stats; `?since=` bounds the runs;
- under `test/ui/`: the SVG draws the blocked edge marked and no blocked station; the
  over-`max_queue` edge is marked with its intake text; the edge panel lists runs with
  `log_dir` as text; the tables hold the same tickets and runs as the drawing; station names
  from the fixture appear nowhere in the screen's source.
