**Brief:** the owner, 2026-11-09 (B-191): "**Courses** `/courses` → `/courses/{c}` — station/leg
graph with queue depths, per-ticket paths, stats."

**Spec:** [BATON_SPEC §5.2](../spec/BATON_SPEC/05-courses.md#sec-5-2) (the course file;
[R-BTN-69](../spec/BATON_SPEC/05-courses.md#sec-5-2): a refused file keeps the previous),
[§5.3](../spec/BATON_SPEC/05-courses.md#sec-5-3) ([R-BTN-83](../spec/BATON_SPEC/05-courses.md#sec-5-3):
a position and the queued leg's source), [§5.4](../spec/BATON_SPEC/05-courses.md#sec-5-4)
([R-BTN-79](../spec/BATON_SPEC/05-courses.md#sec-5-4): queues, `max_queue`, intake),
[§6.2](../spec/BATON_SPEC/06-prerequisites-blocks-advice.md#sec-6-2) ([R-BTN-126](../spec/BATON_SPEC/06-prerequisites-blocks-advice.md#sec-6-2):
a prerequisite failing during a leg blocks its edge; [R-BTN-75](../spec/BATON_SPEC/06-prerequisites-blocks-advice.md#sec-6-2)),
[§7.4](../spec/BATON_SPEC/07-conditions.md#sec-7-4) (backoff), [§9.3](../spec/BATON_SPEC/09-claims.md#sec-9-3)
(hand-offs), [§9.8](../spec/BATON_SPEC/09-claims.md#sec-9-8) ([R-BTN-24](../spec/BATON_SPEC/09-claims.md#sec-9-8):
the resume record), [§8.5](../spec/BATON_SPEC/08-runners.md#sec-8-5) ([R-BTN-128](../spec/BATON_SPEC/08-runners.md#sec-8-5)),
[§14](../spec/BATON_SPEC/14-api.md#sec-14) (`courses` — B-192 builds it; `stats` with its
`off-graph`; `legs/{leg}/runs`).

**Depends on:** B-192 (sessions on the reads), B-205 (cursor and window conventions), B-23
(`legs/{leg}/runs`, whose rows the read embeds). `GET …/courses` itself is B-217's.

**Used by:** B-212, B-223.

### What to build

The §14 row first, then the code, in `src/core/courseView.ts`, reading `courses.ts`,
`stations.ts`, `queues.ts`, `blocks.ts`, `history.ts`, `attempts.ts` and `stats.ts` — aggregates
and ids only.

1. **`GET /v1/repositories/{repository}/courses/{course}`** (`course.show`, read; the repository
   reads' principals and a session), `?since=` (24 hours default, 7 days most) and `?cursor=`
   for the tickets:
   - `course: {id, version, file}` — the file as last accepted, an opaque blob (stations, legs
     with `from`, `to`, `kind`, `requires`, `max_runs`, `timeout`, `outputs`, `conditions`,
     `max_queue`); `refused: {version, reason} | null` (R-BTN-69); `runners: {declaring, alive}`
     — the runners whose heartbeat names this course for the repository, and how many are alive
     (the mockup's "workers at 7");
   - `legs: [{leg, queue: {depth, max_queue, intake: open | closed}, state: {ready, running,
     blocked: [{kind, found: before | during, count}], backing_off}, runs: [...]}]` — `runs`
     as `legs/{leg}/runs` returns them, within the window (R-BTN-128: `log_dir` as text);
   - `stations: [{station, resting, arrived_today}]` — `arrived_today` the tickets that reached
     the station in the last 24 hours (the mockup's "18 today" on the end station);
   - `tickets: [...]`, `next_cursor` — 100 a page, ordered by station in the file's order then
     Baton's order, each `{ticket_id, title, station, path: [{leg, reached, run_id}], claim_legs
     (the live claim's legs since it started), waits_for (a suspended ticket's station and
     condition or prerequisite), handoff (the newest resume pointer as text, R-BTN-24), blocks,
     next_leg, source: course | route | decision, parent, children}`;
   - `off_graph` and `stats` for the window, as `GET …/stats?since=` gives them (wait and run
     times per station and leg, ends by reason and detail, suspensions, advice overridden).
   The graph's counts (`legs[].state`, `stations[].resting`) are aggregates over every ticket,
   not over the page. `?summary=1` answers `course`, `refused`, `runners`, `legs` without their
   `runs`, `stations` and `stats` only — no tickets, no runs — for the list screen (B-212), which
   polls one per course. A course not in the repository's registry is `not-found`.

### What not to do

- No stats recomputed: `stats` and `off_graph` come from `stats.ts`.
- No course validation here: `refused` is what `courses.ts` recorded.
- No body, no log bytes; no screen (B-212).

### Trust

- **`course.show`** — Baton's rows and the course file as an opaque blob (§12.3); titles,
  hand-off pointers, transcripts and log directories as text; nothing written.
- **`?since=`, `?cursor=`** — validated (`invalid-params`); the cursor by B-205's rule.

### Invariants

- **Positions are Baton's** (R-BTN-39); **blocks are stored** (R-BTN-75); **queue depth is advice**
  (R-BTN-79).
- **Filter fields only** (R-BTN-33); **an owner's session, its repositories** (R-BTN-155);
  **both hosts** (R-BTN-55).

### Tests

Scenarios numbered at merge, on both hosts, with a fixture course of four stations and three
legs, tickets at each station, one edge blocked by a prerequisite found during a run, one leg over
`max_queue` with intake closed, one off-graph transition and a refused newer file:

- `course.show` returns the file and `refused`, `runners`, each leg's queue and state counts, the
  stations' resting and `arrived_today` counts, the first page of tickets with paths and sources
  and a `next_cursor`, the window's runs with `log_dir`, `off_graph` and `stats`; the counts equal
  the whole, not the page; `?since=` bounds the runs; `?summary=1` carries no tickets or runs;
  an unknown course is `not-found`.
