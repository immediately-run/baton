**Brief:** the owner, 2026-11-09 (B-191): "**Governance** — `/policy`, `/settings`, `/bars`,
`/consents`, `/attestations` — the knobs, floors, bars and people's signed statements."
Attestations are B-213's; this ticket is the four read-only screens.

the policy and its log; [R-BTN-189](../spec/BATON_SPEC/12-security.md#sec-12-8): guarantees and

**Depends on:** B-208 (the shell), B-199 (the grants read's `consents`; the session callers on
`policy` and `policy/log` are B-192's).

**Used by:** B-203.

### What to build

1. **`/repositories/{r}/policy`** (`src/dashboard/screens/governance/`): from `GET …/policy`
   — version, hash, the preset if one was applied, `check_app_id`, the document pretty-printed
   as text (§12.3: a blob), the `guarantees` as a list with a mark per guarantee met and the
   `floors` among them marked as floors (R-BTN-189); below, `…/policy/log` as a `<table>` —
   version, hash, previous hash, changed by, approver, preset, at, changed keys — newest first,
   with "earlier" by `?after=`. `baton owner policy set <repository> <file>` and `baton owner
   policy apply <repository> <preset>` as text.
2. **`/repositories/{r}/settings`**: the grants read's `settings` — every §13.1 owner setting
   (`code_repos`, `retry`, `transcript_repos`, `api_keys`, `scope_policy` and the rest the data
   model lists), each as its value as text with the one-line meaning the data model gives it —
   and the repository's `tiers` and `weight` from `repo.list` (B-204 carries them so an owner's
   session needs no administrator read). `baton owner settings set <repository> […]` as text.
3. **`/repositories/{r}/bars`**: the owner bars in force and scheduled (course, tag, legs,
   applies, starts, ends, release holders, reason, set by — `mcp:<id>` drawn with the key's
   name from `mcp_keys`) and the required bars (course, except legs, tag prefix) from the
   grants read's `bars` and `settings`; `baton owner bar add …`, `baton owner bar lift
   <repository> <id>` and `baton owner bar follow <repository> <course>` as text — a lift is an
   approver's by key (R-BTN-186), not a session's.
4. **`/repositories/{r}/consents`**: from the grants read's `consents` — each home listing this
   repository, consented or not, by whom and when, withdrawn when; `baton owner consent add
   <repository> <home>` and `… withdraw …` as text.
5. **Phone.** Tables collapse to cards at 375 px; the policy document scrolls inside its box.

### What not to do

- No write: policy, settings, bars and consents are keys' and approvers' (R-BTN-186,
  R-BTN-188); the screens name the commands.
- No guarantee or floor computed in the app: `guarantees` and `floors` are the read's.
- No attestation here (B-213).

### Trust

- **The reads** — Baton's rows and the policy document as an opaque blob (§12.3), drawn
  through `Text` and as pretty-printed JSON; `set_by`, reasons and preset names as text; an
  owner's session on its repositories. Nothing is written.

### Invariants

- **The policy is held in Baton, never in git** (R-BTN-188), shown from the read.
- **Floors are Baton's refusals** (R-BTN-189): marked, never enforced here.
- **Read-only** (§11.4); **text, never markup** (§12.3); **an owner's session, its repositories**
  (R-BTN-155).

### Tests

Under `test/ui/` with fixtures (a `protected` policy with two versions, a scheduled bar set over
MCP, a required bar, one consent): the policy screen marks the floors and lists the log; the
settings screen shows `tiers` and `weight` from `repo.list`; the bars screen draws `mcp:<id>` with
its name; the consents screen lists the home; every command line matches R-BTN-127's tree (the
same test as B-199's); no control is drawn; a hostile reason is text.

- [ ] The four screens built, read-only, each naming its verified command.

      `npm run check:tickets` are green.
