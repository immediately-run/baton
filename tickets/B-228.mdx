**Brief:** the runner's log at the claim of B-150 named the home `@ 30f959c`; the board's head
was `6ee04a5`. The agent's sandbox, cloned at the home's HEAD, ran the old `npm test`.

**Spec:** [BATON_SPEC §4 R-BTN-34](../spec/BATON_SPEC/04-tickets-syncs-board.md) ("before
preparing any tree at the sha"), [R-BTN-107](../spec/BATON_SPEC/08-runners.md) (an executor's
checkout is the home's).

**Depends on:** none.

**Used by:** none.

### What to build

`advanceHome(home, sha, defaultBranch)` (`src/runner/home.ts`), called by the runner right after
`ensureSha` and the course load: at the snapshot, nothing; on the default branch, no tracked
change, HEAD an ancestor of the snapshot: `git merge --ff-only`; anything else — another branch,
a detached HEAD, tracked edits, a HEAD ahead or diverged — is left exactly as it is and logged.
A failure of the fast-forward is logged, never a release.

### Trust

No new input: the sha is the claim's, already checked to be on the default branch (R-BTN-143).

### Invariants

- **The home is the runner's alone** (one runner per checkout): only a fast-forward, never a
  reset; a home someone left edited or on a branch is not touched.
