**Brief:** the owner, 2026-10-31, on making the sync's repository facts attestations, answers
included and courier deliveries with them: "yes, please!" It completes the owner's 2026-10-30
unification ("human attestation and instrument readings can also be generalized into a single
thing, which differs only by the participant signing the attestation").

**Spec:** `spec/BATON_SPEC.md` R-BTN-191 (new), R-BTN-181 (writers `sync` and `courier`; the
sync's attestations carried by its post's authentication), §4.4 (`attestations`,
`attestations_truncated`; bars, decisions and `not_before` with the ticket), R-BTN-39, R-BTN-70,
R-BTN-82, R-BTN-46, R-BTN-10, R-BTN-116, R-BTN-171, R-BTN-187, R-BTN-188 (`inputs.answers`
retired), R-BTN-190, S-BTN-187, S-BTN-195.

**Depends on:** B-120 (positions are Baton's, which the readiness and arrival attestations
assume). B-145 builds the policy's `attestations` entries this reads.

### What to build

1. **Writers.** `sync` for the board post's credential (sync key, CI identity, machine key) and
   `courier` for a courier key, in the attestation writer enum and the policy's `attestations`
   entries.
2. **The board post.** `attestations: [{type, ticket, ref?, value}]` validated per type and
   stored in the post's transaction, superseding the previous run's of the same type and
   subject; refused with the post. `stations` and `markers` are retired from the post; bars,
   decisions and `not_before` move into `tickets[]`.
3. **Readers.** Readiness (R-BTN-70), fact arrivals and implied stations (R-BTN-39), outputs
   (R-BTN-82), markers (R-BTN-46) and evidence (R-BTN-10) read from the newest accepted run's
   attestations; a person leg settles from a `std:decision@1` by an admitted writer (R-BTN-116).
4. **Couriers.** `POST /v1/courier/entries/{repository}/{id}` with `delivered` records a
   `std:effect@1` by `courier`; confirmation stays the sync's later attestation of the effect.
5. **The CLI's sync** emits the attestations from each station function's result.
6. **The model:** one inbound path, and no judgement over two runs' attestations.
