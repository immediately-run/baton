**Brief:** the owner, 2026-10-17: a prerequisite may fail during a leg, as well as before it starts. That is a coloured edge in the course graph, not a new station. B-20 specified it (baton#44).
**Spec:** `spec/BATON_SPEC.md` §2m.6c (R-BTN-126), with its dated notes on R-BTN-75, R-BTN-76, R-BTN-77, R-BTN-78, R-BTN-101, R-BTN-106, R-BTN-120, R-BTN-122, §6.10, §6.12, §6.13 and §14.

### Why

R-BTN-126 is specified. This ticket builds it.

### What to build

1. **The end.** `POST /v1/runner/end` takes `released:blocked` with `prerequisite`, plus `reading` and `commit` where the kind needs them. Build the validation for each kind; an invalid prerequisite is recorded as a counted `cannot-finish:transient` in the refusing call. Readings are refused (`reading-refused`) or found invalid exactly as R-BTN-126 splits them.
2. **Resource.** Store the reading, add the `end`-sourced need, and write the ticket's row against R-BTN-76's counts, all in one transaction. Stamp `found_at`, and drop the need and its row at the leg's next end that is not blocked on it, or after 7 days.
3. **Capability.** Remove the capability from the runner's declared set in the end's transaction, updating `fleet_capabilities`.
4. **Ticket and decision.** Write provisional rows that refuse a claim into the leg (rule 1) and stay outside `blocked_since`. Settle them at the first snapshot containing the end's commit, and drop them with `prerequisite-unconfirmed` after an hour.
5. **Bounds.** Add `max_blocked` to the owners' `retry` ceiling. Count blocked ends from `claims.blocked_kind` and `claims.blocked_ref`, apply the `blocked-exhausted` hold with `baton:retries`, and list a worker's blocked ends under **Needs you**.
6. **The runner.** `LegError` options gain `prerequisite`, `reading?` and `commit?`. Add `baton leg release --blocked --kind … --ref …` and the runner's own checks. A block is never retried in the claim.
7. **The legacy mapping.** Map `released:provider-exhausted` exactly as R-BTN-126 states, never shortening a reading already in force.
8. **Views.** The dashboard's **Courses** view and `baton why` draw each leg as an edge with a state, in colour and a text label, with `found`.
9. **Migration.** Add `ticket_blocks.provisional` and `found`, `leg_needs.source`, `added_at` and `found_at`, `claims.blocked_kind` and `blocked_ref` with their index, and the `blocked-exhausted` hold reason.

### Do not

- Add a station for a blocked leg anywhere.
- Retry an error that names a prerequisite.
