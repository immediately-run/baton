**Brief:** the owner, 2026-10-20: a leg that needs a person raises a condition and goes on with
the handler's restart (`spec/BATON_SPEC_HISTORY.md`, "Decisions in full"). Filed from the ticket
plan for the current-state spec (PR #71).
**Spec:** `spec/BATON_SPEC.md` §7.2 R-BTN-162; R-BTN-163 (§7.3, an unhandled `suspend` with the
debugger as handler, resolving inline); R-BTN-161 (`std:restart@1`); R-BTN-122 (§7.4, `max_wait_s`
and `max_blocked`); R-BTN-167 (§7.5, the wait counts toward the `start` timer); R-BTN-126 and
R-BTN-120 (§6.2, §6.1, the blocked end and the prerequisite); R-BTN-44 (§9.3, the hand-off);

### Requires

None: a checkout and a terminal.

### Why

B-55 refuses every `suspend` raise with `suspend-unavailable`. This ticket builds the wait, so a
leg can ask a question in the middle of its work and act on the answer, in the same session when
the answer comes quickly and in a later claim when it does not.

### What to build

1. **Lift B-55's refusal.** A `suspend` raise is accepted. A second one raised while one is
   awaited is refused on the channel (one at a time). `notify` raises in the meantime are not
   waits.
2. **Waiting in the claim** (`src/runner/runner.ts`):
   - After the handler is pushed (bound) or the condition is reported (unbound), the runner keeps
     the claim and heartbeats as usual.
   - It records a `condition-wait` activity naming the handler. **Running** and `baton leg why`
     show it.
   - The wait is the raise's `wait_s`, else the declaration's, else 120 seconds for `hitl` and 0
     for any other type.
   - It is capped at the owners' `max_wait_s`: 900 by default, at most 3,600. B-11 makes this a
     setting; until then use the constant.
   - The wait counts toward B-60's `start` deadline, and nothing pauses that deadline.
3. **Settling within the wait** (`src/core/`):
   - The handler can close in three transactions: a snapshot that shows it closed, the end of its
     last claim, or `owner resolve`. Each queues a `handled` message for the raiser's holder and
     rings it (R-BTN-91).
   - The heartbeat answers `handled: [{condition_id, handler, outcome, output?}]`, where `output`
     is a pointer.
   - The runner reads the value from git and validates it as a `std:restart@1` whose `restart` is
     one the raise offered, or null. `ctx.raise` returns `{restart, reason}`.
   - If validation fails, the leg ends `released:cannot-finish:contract-input`.
   - A debugger resolve is handed over inline as `{restart, reason: "resolved", answered_by}`.
4. **Past the wait:**
   - `ctx.raise` returns `{suspended: true}`, and the script returns.
   - The runner pushes, calls `handoff`, and ends `released:blocked` with the prerequisite
     `{kind: "condition", ref: <condition_id>}`.
   - Baton writes a `ticket_blocks` row of kind `condition`. It is provisional on the handler's
     commit, and not provisional when the debugger is the handler.
   - It writes no row when the handler has already closed, so the next claim resumes at once.
   - The end is not a failed attempt and counts toward `max_blocked`.
   - The prerequisite is met when the handler closes `done` or `withdrawn`, or when the condition
     is resolved.
5. **Resuming:**
   - On `unblocked`, any runner eligible for the leg may claim it.
   - The claim response carries `resume: {condition_id, type, handler, outcome:
     done|withdrawn|resolved, output?, handoff}`, as pointers.
   - The runner validates it as in item 3, and the script reads `ctx.resume` first.
   - A `withdrawn` handler resumes with `{restart: <default>, reason: "withdrawn"}`, or with
     `restart: null` when the raise named no default.
6. **The CLI** goes on B-47's channel:
   - `baton leg raise --wait` blocks for the wait and prints the restart or `suspend`.
   - `baton leg await <handler> --timeout <s>` can be called again until the wait is spent.
7. **Migration** (a new numbered file):
   - `condition` in `ticket_blocks.kind`;
   - `handled` in the messages' kind;
   - `condition-wait` in the activity kinds.
8. **The model** (R-BTN-68): add the `condition` block and its resume to `model/baton.qnt`, with
   the `npm run model:pr` report in this PR.

### Do not

- Count a `condition` block as a failed attempt, or leave it out of `max_blocked`.
- Wait past `min(wait_s, max_wait_s)`.
- Accept a restart the raise did not offer.
- Pause or extend the `start` deadline for the wait.
- Lose an answer that arrives during the end. A handler closed before the end arrives writes no
  row (G-BTN-147).

- On both hosts, `test/integration/S-BTN-127.answered-within-wait.test.ts` and
  `S-BTN-128.answered-after-wait.test.ts`, using a fixture handler course under `test/fixtures/`:
  - an answer within the wait, and an answer outside the restarts (`contract-input`);
  - no answer, then a resume by another runner;
  - a withdrawn handler resuming with the default;
  - an answer 1 second after the wait, with no block row and an immediate resume.

  `handled` message rung once.
