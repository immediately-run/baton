**Spec:** R-BTN-114 (*Conclusions are the owners'*), R-BTN-153 (*Mapping the status*), R-BTN-115
(the `forge-check` entry), §10's trust statement ("a check is as trustworthy as … the check
command"), S-BTN-68, G-BTN-93. Source: PR #165's review round one, on the 2026-11-02 security
review's S2 (`docs/review/security-review-2026-11-02.mdx`).

### Why

GitHub's required status checks are satisfied by a `success`, `skipped` or `neutral` conclusion.
R-BTN-153 sends "a timeout or an `exec` error" to the station mapped to `neutral`, and lets an
agent's `baton-leg-result` or channel `done` reach "only `failure` or `neutral` stations". The
second was meant to keep an agent from printing its way to a pass; on GitHub `neutral` is one.
A change whose tests hang until `timeout_s` therefore lands past a required check. PR #165 made
a change toward `neutral` a loosening of `checks` and added the `failure` fallback where no
`neutral` is mapped, which narrows the hole but does not close it: the standard map still names
`neutral`.

### Decided (the owner)

2026-11-02, choosing among this ticket's options: "I accept your recommendations" — option (a).
`neutral` was never built, so no transition is needed. The spec states it (R-BTN-114, R-BTN-153,
R-BTN-115's delivery, G-BTN-93; S-BTN-68), PR `claude/baton-spec-review-2fwiga`.

### What to build

1. `PUT /v1/owner/{repository}/settings` validates each `checks` map: exactly one `success`,
   exactly one `failure`, at most one `timed_out`, and nothing else (`neutral` refused
   `invalid-params`); a station newly mapped to `success`, or a removed or renamed entry, needs an
   `approvers.policy` approver (R-BTN-186). (B-169's item 2 is the same validation: build it once,
   in whichever lands first.)
2. The check runner maps a timeout or `exec` error to the `timed_out` station, else `failure`;
   Baton refuses an agent's `baton-leg-result` or channel `done` toward any station but a
   `failure` or `timed_out` one (R-BTN-153).
3. The `forge-check` deliverer posts `timed_out` as GitHub's check-run conclusion of that name
   (checks are GitHub only, R-BTN-115).
