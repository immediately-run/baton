**Brief:** [SELF-HOSTING](https://immediately.run/present/github/immediately-run/baton/main/bundle/docs/plans/self-hosting.mdx). The owner, 2026-10-16: tickets, history and
review records move to the baton repository, with no docs access needed to understand Baton.
**Spec:** BATON_SPEC §2a (R-BTN-47), R-BTN-33.

### Why

After R3-1266 the baton repository can hold tickets, but Baton's 30-odd live items, its
archive, its project page, its plans and the review records of its PRs are still in docs. A
reader of the public repository cannot see what is planned, what was done, or why.

### What to do

1. **Partition.** List every `project: baton` item, live and archived.
   - **Moved (owner, 2026-10-16): all of them**, docs' adoption items included. No item
     depends on one outside the project, and no other item depends on one of them, so no
     dependency crosses the line; an adoption item's work still happens in docs.
   - Items in flight in docs (R3-1244, R3-1245) are copied as they stand and brought up to date
     when their sessions finish them there.
2. **Convert.** Each moved item becomes `tickets/R3-NNNN.mdx` (finished ones go under
   `tickets/archive/`), in docs' roadmap format (owner, 2026-10-16):
   - its id, status and dependencies are kept;
   - wiki links become relative links;
   - its **Spec:** line cites `spec/BATON_SPEC.md` (R3-1252);
   - links to docs-only pages become plain text, or are removed when they carry nothing a
     reader needs.
3. **Move the rest.**
   - The `baton` project page becomes `tickets/project.mdx`, beside the tickets it lists.
   - The self-hosting plan moves to `docs/plans/`; the deployment runbook goes with R3-1268,
     and `claim-events.mdx`, which docs' mirror writes, stays in docs.
   - Baton PRs' rows in the review-gate records (`content/plans/review-gate/pr-findings.mdx`,
     `token-usage.mdx`) move to `docs/review/`.
   - Until Baton claims this repository's tickets (R3-1266), `scripts/ticket.mjs` claims them
     with git as the lock (owner, 2026-10-16).
4. **Scrub.** Before anything is pushed, remove or generalise:
   - other projects' names and plans;
   - private repositories, such as the transcript repositories;
   - account names, email addresses and people's names other than the owner's;
   - costs and billing, hostnames that are not Baton's, and anything a docs page marks private.

   The owner reviews the scrub diff. Publishing cannot be undone, so nothing is pushed before
   that review.
5. **Retire in docs, in the same sitting.**
   - Each moved item becomes a one-paragraph stub pointing at its file in the baton repository,
     with `status: superseded` so docs' board drops it.
   - The archive keeps stubs, so wiki links and `check:ids` stay valid.
   - Docs' board and the baton board must never both offer one ticket. Verify this on the
     dashboard after the docs sync.
6. **Changelog.** `CHANGELOG.md` cites baton commits and PRs for ticket changes from here on.
   Earlier rows' docs commit ids stay as history, marked as references to the private
   repository.

### Do not

- Renumber an `R3-NNNN`, or rewrite an item's substance while moving it.
- Move docs' own items, or anything the scrub cannot make public; such an item stays in docs
  and its baton counterpart says only what is public.

### Tests

- In baton: every `R3-`/`B-` id cited in `tickets/`, `spec/`, `docs/` and `CHANGELOG.md`
  resolves to a ticket file; the link check passes; a grep for `immediately-run/docs`,
  `content/` and the scrub list's terms returns nothing outside a marked history note.
- In docs: `npm run verify` is green with the stubs; docs' board has no moved ticket.

### Exit criteria

- [ ] the partition list confirmed by the owner, and the scrub diff reviewed
- [ ] the moved tickets, archive, project page, plans and review records merged in baton; its
      id and link checks green
- [ ] the docs stubs merged; `npm run verify` green; the dashboard shows each ticket on one board
