**Brief:** the ticket plan for the current-state spec (PR #71, 2026-10-20), §2's B-64, rescoped by
the 2026-10-30 amendment (one outbox, couriers; `spec/BATON_SPEC_HISTORY.md`). Baton holds no git
or forge credential, so the handler tickets of its own conditions (R-BTN-163), the withdrawals of
superseded tickets (R-BTN-168), promoted light questions' files (R-BTN-173) and check runs
(R-BTN-115) reach the outside only through a courier.
**Spec:** `spec/BATON_SPEC.md` §7.8 R-BTN-171 (the outbox and couriers); §10 R-BTN-115 (the
`forge-check` kind); §7.3 R-BTN-163 ("Binding core's conditions": the handler id `<local>.c<n>` or
`baton.<sub>.<condition_id>`, `baton.outbox-late` never bound); §4.2 R-BTN-74 (`file(ticket)` and
`withdraw(ticket, reason)`); §5.3 R-BTN-83 (the `outbox` held position, which queues no leg);
§12.1 (the courier principal, one role per key); §13.1 `couriers.delivers`; §13.6
`ticket_overrides` (`override_kind` `outbox`, `hold_reason` `outbox-unlanded`, `outbox-refused`,
`outbox-collision`); §13.8 the `outbox` table, `outbox_due`, `outbox_alarm`, `outbox_pending`;
§14 `/v1/owner/{repository}/couriers` and `/v1/courier/entries/*`; §15 S-BTN-136, S-BTN-81,
G-BTN-156. After the adversarial review (`spec/BATON_SPEC_HISTORY.md`, "Adversarial review
(2026-10-20)"): §4.4's top-level `bindings`, R-BTN-163's "Binding core's conditions" and §13's
`condition_bindings` (adv-2 M12); R-BTN-171's `outbox-collision` (adv-2 m2); S-BTN-131.
**Depends on:** B-58 (bindings, handler ticket frontmatter, the source's `file` export,
`std:condition@1`), B-56 (`baton.<sub>` and `hitl.decision` conditions, which are what a
`handler` entry files), B-38 (route overrides stored with their `next_source` kind; this ticket
adds `outbox`), B-42 (unchanged by the amendment; kept for the override store it shares).

### Requires

None: a checkout and a terminal. The forge-refused push runs against the integration harness's
fake forge.

### Why

Bound core conditions need a handler ticket in git, supersession (B-65) needs withdrawals in git,
and checks need runs on the forge, but Baton must never hold the credential for any of them. Until
this lands, every core condition reaches only the debugger, nothing Baton decides is written back,
and check runs travel through a second queue (`notices`, R3-1256) that does the same job.

### What to build

1. **Schema.** A new numbered migration in `src/migrations/` adds the `outbox` table as §13.8
   gives it (`(repository_id, id)`, `id` increasing per repository; `ticket_id`, `kind`
   `handler|withdraw|answer|forge-check`, `condition_id`, `fields` JSON, `kid`, `signature`,
   `state` `queued|claimed|delivered|skipped|confirmed|dropped`, `credential_id`, `attempts`,
   `not_before`, `queued_at`, `delivered_ref`, `confirmed_at`, `snapshots_since_delivered`), the
   `outbox_due`, `outbox_alarm` and `outbox_pending` indexes, `couriers.delivers`, and `outbox`
   to `ticket_overrides.override_kind`'s CHECK and `outbox-unlanded`, `outbox-refused`,
   `outbox-collision` to `hold_reason`'s. It copies every live `forge-check` `notices` row into `outbox` as a
   `forge-check` entry and drops `notices` (migrations are append-only: a new file, never an edit
   of `0017_notices.sql`).
2. **Core module `src/core/outbox.ts`**, replacing `src/core/notices.ts`. `queue(kind, ticket,
   fields, condition?)` in the caller's transaction, signing the entry with the tenant's
   `SigningKey` (R-BTN-55); `fields` passes a new identifiers-only validator in
   `src/core/validate.ts` (no free text, §12.3; a `forge-check`'s `summary` is rendered `plain`,
   R-BTN-112). Each queue, delivery and confirmation logs an `outbox {id, kind, state}` event in
   the ticket's history (`src/core/history.ts`).
   - **`handler` entries.** When B-56/B-58 raise a bound `baton.*` or `hitl.decision`
     condition, queue a `handler` entry and, in the same transaction, a provisional ticket row
     at the handler course's start station waiting on `outbox` (`next_source: outbox`, held as
     an override in B-38's store; no leg is queued). The id is `<local>.c<n>`, or
     `baton.<sub>.<condition_id>` for a repository-level condition; a runner's or the tenant's
     condition is never bound (item 7). The condition counts as handled from this moment. A
     binding of `baton.outbox-late` is ignored.
   - **`withdraw` entries** are queued by B-65; this ticket gives them their kind, ordering and
     delivery, and tests them with a fixture supersession.
   - **`forge-check` entries** are queued where `notices` were (B-5's check tickets), one copy
     per forge courier of the repository.
   - **Dropping.** A `handler` entry still `queued` when its condition closes, and never claimed,
     is `dropped`, its provisional row deleted, and no withdrawal follows; a claimed or delivered
     one is dropped and followed by a `withdraw` entry, the claimed one's row kept until its mark
     or its lease lapse.
   - **Confirming.** In the snapshot transaction (`src/core/board.ts`), a git entry whose
     `delivered_ref` the accepted snapshot contains, and which the snapshot shows, turns
     `confirmed`; the posted ticket then rules and the override ends. After 3 accepted snapshots
     without it, the ticket is held `outbox-unlanded`, except a proposed entry (under
     `tickets.created: proposed`), which is confirmed only when the proposal merge lands it
     (B-140), is late only if undelivered after 24 hours, and is never held `outbox-unlanded`
     (R-BTN-180). A `forge-check` entry is confirmed by the
     sync's read-back (R-BTN-115).
3. **The courier routes** (`src/core/workerApi.ts` or a new `src/core/courierApi.ts`, with
   `src/core/auth.ts` for the `courier` principal): `POST /v1/courier/entries/claim` leases up to
   50 entries of the kinds the courier delivers on its repositories for 10 minutes, a ticket's git
   entries together in `id` order and none while an earlier one is `claimed` or `delivered`;
   `POST /v1/courier/entries/{repository}/{id}` takes `delivered {ref}`, `skipped`, or `failed
   {transient | refused | collision}` from the claimer only. `transient` backs off 1, 5, 30
   minutes, then hourly; `refused` and `collision` hold the ticket and raise `baton.outbox-late`
   naming the courier. `POST /v1/owner/{repository}/couriers` takes `delivers: "git" |
   "forge-check"` and refuses a key that holds another role (`conflict`).
   - **Who may deliver.** The repository policy's `outbox` section (R-BTN-188; B-145 builds the
     store and who may change it): per class, `deliverers` (`courier`, `runner`,
     `trusted-runner`), `trust` (`any` | `trusted`) and `min_release`, read through
     `src/core/policy.ts`, or the `cooperative` preset's `{deliverers: [courier], trust: any}`
     until B-145 lands. The claim route admits a caller only as the policy of each repository
     allows (`forbidden` naming the unmet knob). A policy admitting runners adds the shared
     `forge-check` copy.
4. **The courier (`src/courier/`, `baton courier run`).** A loop with its own SSH key, outside
   any runner: claim, verify each entry's signature against the pinned `GET /v1/keys/signing`
   keys, deliver, mark. The claim carries `{limit?, release}`, refused below the policy's
   `min_release` (a declaration, not a control). For `git`: fetch the head it writes to (the
   default branch, or under `tickets.created: proposed` the branch `baton/proposed/<ticket>`),
   skip an entry whose file already carries `baton_write: <id>` (or, for `withdraw`, already says
   withdrawn or is absent) and mark it `delivered` with the head's commit; else render it, commit,
   push. A dedicated courier renders through the source's `file(ticket)` (`handler`, body generated
   from `fields`), `withdraw(ticket, reason)` or `answer`; **a trusted-runner deliverer** never
   loads the source's exports and writes only from the CLI's own markdown-source templates, marking
   every git entry `failed refused` on a repository whose ticket source is ejected
   (`.baton/tickets.mjs`; R-BTN-171, R-BTN-182); on a rejected push fetch, check and write again at most 3 times, then
   mark `failed transient`; a push the forge refuses marks `failed refused`; a foreign file at a
   `handler`'s path marks `failed collision`. For `forge-check`: the existing courier's delivery
   (R-BTN-115), moved onto the new routes, with `<repository>:<id>` as `external_id`.
5. **The `withdraw` export.** `src/defaults/tickets-markdown.ts` (and the generated
   `.baton/tickets.mjs` wrapper in `src/defaults/cli.ts`) gains `withdraw(ticket, reason)`,
   setting `status: withdrawn`, `withdrawn_reason` and `baton_write: <id>`.
6. **Alarm, export and model.** The alarm (`src/core/baton.ts`) raises `baton.outbox-late` for a
   git entry not `confirmed` 24 h after `queued_at` and a `forge-check` entry not `delivered` 15
   minutes after, closed when it is confirmed or dropped. R3-1258's export (R-BTN-88) carries the
   git-kind rows. `model/lightQuestion.qnt` renames its writer from a `baton.write` claim to a
   courier lease and `written`/`landed` to `delivered`/`confirmed` (no change to what it checks);
   the `outbox` held position goes into `model/baton.qnt` with a `model:pr` report (R-BTN-68).
7. **The adversarial review's fixes** (2026-10-20):
   - **Bindings for core's handlers** (§4.4, R-BTN-163, adv-2 M12). The sync posts
     `.baton/baton.json`'s `conditions` as the board post's top-level `bindings` (`<type
     prefix>: <course> | {course, replaces}`); the sync is `src/shared/batonSync.mjs`, vendored,
     so that change goes to docs first, then `npm run vendor`. The board validator
     (`src/core/board.ts`) accepts and bounds it beside B-58's `tickets[].conditions`. A new
     migration adds `condition_bindings` `(repository_id, ticket_id, prefix)` (`ticket_id` `''`
     for the repository scope) with `course_id`, `replaces` and `snapshot_sha`, rewritten in each
     accepted snapshot's transaction. Baton reads it only to file handler tickets for its own
     conditions, walking scopes as R-BTN-161 does, and never for a leg's raise.
   - **Never bound** (R-BTN-163, adv-2 M12). A condition on a runner or on the tenant has no
     repository whose bindings and outbox apply: it queues no entry, and Needs you lists it as
     unhandled.

### Do not

- Give Baton or the Worker any git or forge credential, or write git from core: every effect is
  a courier's delivery of a signed entry.
- Let a key deliver that the policy's `outbox` does not admit, or hard-code a rule the policy
  owns (who delivers, trust, release).
- Store a subject, body or any free text in `fields` beyond a `forge-check`'s rendered `summary`.
- Put choice settlements, timer legs, `exhausted` legs or call settlements in the outbox: they
  are overrides written by the leg they queue (R-BTN-171's "Not in the outbox").
- Keep `notices` beside `outbox`, or a `baton.write` leg beside the courier.
