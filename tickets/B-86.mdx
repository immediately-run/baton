**Brief:** the acceptance run (the lessons page): the runner registered, recommended, and
idled in total silence — `parseRunArgs` defaults `--capabilities` to `["agent-venue"]`, the
grant allowed `create-pr,llm-call,agent-venue`, and the gate had no `create-pr` to match the
course's legs. The runbook's `baton run` line omits the flag.
**Spec:** §6.13 (R-BTN-72: the runner), §11 (the heartbeat's capabilities), the runbook
`docs/runbooks/register-baton.mdx` step 5.

### Why

The grant already names the machine's capabilities; asking the operator to restate them on
every runner is one silent mismatch waiting per machine — and the failure mode is idle
silence, the worst kind.

### What to build

1. The heartbeat's capability declaration defaults to the grant's: when `--capabilities` is
   absent, the runner asks once (or the server answers from the grant) and declares what
   the grant allows, the flag overriding for a narrower machine.
2. The runbook's `baton run` line and the runner's `--help` say the flag exists to narrow,
   not to enable.
3. A runner whose declared capabilities match no course's legs logs why it can never claim
   (one line at startup), instead of idling.

### Tests

- Integration (S-BTN-n new or extended): a granted worker with no `--capabilities` claims a
  `create-pr` leg; a narrower `--capabilities llm-call` idles with the startup line.

### Exit criteria

- [ ] a granted runner needs no `--capabilities` to serve its grant
- [ ] a never-eligible runner says so at startup
