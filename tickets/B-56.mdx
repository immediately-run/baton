**Brief:** the owner, 2026-10-20: conditions replace notices for people and Baton's own decisions,
and an unhandled condition reaches the debugger (`spec/BATON_SPEC_HISTORY.md`, "Decisions in
full"). Filed from the ticket plan for the current-state spec (PR #71). With B-55 it supersedes
B-14: severity is now data, and routing and reminders are retired.
**Spec:** `spec/BATON_SPEC.md` §7.3 R-BTN-163 (Needs you, resolving, core conditions, posted
decisions, the floor, reading the list); R-BTN-160's closing table (§7.1), R-BTN-125 (severity),
R-BTN-78 (§6.4, posted decisions), R-BTN-83 (§5.3, a re-route), R-BTN-122 (§7.4, the holds that
raise `baton.retries`), R-BTN-82 (§5.5, `baton.contract`), R-BTN-155 (§11.4: resolving a condition is not a confirmed action),
§11.4's Needs you view, §11.3 (`owner resolve`, `condition.list`), §13.3/§13.6
(`ticket_decisions`, `ticket_overrides.hold_reason`, `pending_owner_state`), §14 (the resolve and
conditions routes); §15 S-BTN-6, -7, -10, -44, -47, -131, G-BTN-149.
**Depends on:** B-55 (the `conditions` row, the route and `condition.list`).

### Requires

None: a checkout and a terminal.

### Why

R3-1257 built `baton:retries` and `baton:stalled` as decisions that Baton writes into
`ticket_decisions` with `source = 'baton'`. Claims, blocks and notices each carry special cases for
them (`src/core/claims.ts` rule 3's exception, `src/core/blocks.ts` `BATON_DECISIONS`, the notices'
joins). The spec now makes them core conditions, so they need one code path. Needs you becomes
the list of what nothing else handles.

### What to build

1. **Core conditions in `src/core/conditions.ts`** (from B-55):
   - `raiseCore(type, {ticket?, ref?, detail?, restarts?})` and `closeCore(…)`, run in the
     caller's transaction.
   - They are unique while open on `(repository_id, type, ticket_id, ref)`.
   - Raising writes a `condition` history row. Closing records `outcome`, `restart` and
     `answered_by`.
2. **`baton.retries`, `baton.contract` and `baton.stalled`** are whole-ticket `suspend`
   conditions with restarts `retry`, `route:<leg>` and `park` and no default. Severity is
   `normal`, except `baton.contract`, which is `high`.
   - Replace `Blocks.raise`/`answer` and `BATON_DECISIONS` (`src/core/blocks.ts`) and the
     `baton:retries` raises in `src/core/claims.ts`.
   - `baton.stalled` keeps today's 72-hour alarm scan until B-61 replaces it with core's default
     timer. It still closes by itself when the leg is posted other than `waiting`.
   - `baton.contract` is raised by B-39's contract-input hold. Whichever of B-39 and B-56 lands
     second wires it.
   - **The restarts**, as R-BTN-163's table:
     - `retry` clears the hold and the backoff (`baton.stalled`: closes and re-arms).
     - `route:<leg>` is B-38's re-route plus clearing the hold. Whichever of B-38 and B-56 lands
       second wires it.
     - `park` closes the condition and keeps the hold, and nothing is raised again while that
       hold stands. For `baton.stalled`, `park` sets an owner hold `hold_reason: parked`.
   - Any owner act that clears the hold under `baton.retries` or `baton.contract` closes the
     condition, including §6's clear-hold.
   - These conditions block nothing. Remove rule 3's `decision`-block exception in
     `src/core/claims.ts`.
3. **Migration** (a new numbered file):
   - Convert each open `ticket_decisions` row with `source = 'baton'` into an open `conditions`
     row (`baton:retries` becomes `baton.retries`, `baton:stalled` becomes `baton.stalled`), and
     delete the `decision` blocks those rows made.
   - Drop `ticket_decisions.source` (posted decisions only remain).
   - If R3-1258 has created `pending_owner_state`, its `kind` becomes
     `override|condition|timer|outbox` as §13.6 says. Otherwise R3-1258 creates it with those
     kinds.
4. **Resolve.**
   - `POST /v1/owner/{repository}/tickets/{ticket}/resolve {restart, condition_id?}` replaces
     `{decision, answer, leg?}` (`src/core/baton.ts`, `Claims.resolveDecision`).
     - `condition_id` is required when the ticket has more than one open condition taking a
       restart.
     - A restart the condition does not offer is `invalid-params`.
   - `baton owner resolve <ticket> <restart>` in `cli/baton.mjs` replaces `resolve <decision>
     retry|park`.
   - The dashboard reaches it with an administrator's session (and an owner's, once B-76 adds
     owner sessions) as `owner.resolve`, in one step: `POST /v1/ui/conditions/{condition}/resolve {restart}`
     answers `{ok: true}` (`invalid-params` for a restart the condition does not offer). It is
     not on R-BTN-155's confirmed list — no `confirm_code`, no `baton admin confirm` (R-BTN-163,
     §11.4, adv-2 B1 and adv-4 B1). The Resolve page `/conditions/<condition_id>` that a core
     handler links to is B-75's.
   - A leg-raised `suspend` closed this way records `{restart, reason: "resolved", answered_by}`
     for B-59's resume.
5. **`hitl.decision`.** In the transaction that stores posted decisions (`src/core/blocks.ts`,
   the `source = 'sync'` write):
   - raise one `hitl.decision` per open posted decision on its ticket (`ref` the decision id,
     severity the decision's own), and close it when the decision closes;
   - no leg waits on it.
6. **The debugger.** `GET /v1/ui/state`'s `needs_you` gains `conditions`:
   - every open condition with no `handler`, plus the floor;
   - ordered by severity, then age;
   - each shown with type, restarts, default, `detail`, `url` and the tickets it would unblock.

   `src/ui/app.js` renders it through `textContent`. The old listings stay beside it until B-57
   converts them.
7. **The floor**, which no binding removes:
   - every open `hitl` and `hitl.*` condition;
   - every open `baton.retries`, `baton.contract` and `baton.stalled` condition;
   - every open posted decision;
   - any `suspend` condition whose handler ticket has had no leg run for 24 hours (none can exist
     before B-58).
8. **Reading the floor.**
   - `GET /v1/repositories/{repository}/conditions?open=1&person=1` returns the floor's
     conditions and posted decisions with their links.
   - `cli/decisions.mjs` (`baton decision list`, `--all`, `--watch` once a minute) reads it in
     place of `GET /v1/courier/decisions`. The courier route stays for B-67 to delete.
9. **The model** (R-BTN-68): Baton-raised decisions become conditions in `model/baton.qnt`, with
   the `npm run model:pr` report in this PR. R3-1254 (in progress) models decisions in its part
   3. If that part has landed, change it here. If it has not, record in R3-1254's `## Status` that
   it should model these as conditions.

### Do not

- Give `baton.retries`, `baton.contract` or `baton.stalled` a default. They never resolve
  themselves.
- Let a handler close a core condition (G-BTN-149). Only its cause or `owner resolve` does.
- Delete notices, couriers or `GET /v1/courier/decisions`; that is B-67, after B-66.
- Convert the other Needs-you listings (B-57) or bind anything (B-58).
- Accept a restart the condition did not offer.
