**Brief:** the Baton project; the owner, 2026-10-16: there can be a race between sync and claim, so Baton operates under the open world assumption.
**Spec:** `docs/content/specs/BATON_SPEC.mdx` §2l R-BTN-104 (the request, unknown, entry legs, the gates at the claim, provisional, settlement, ending), §6.10 as annotated (`unknown-ticket` does not count), §4's 2026-10-16 DDL block (`claims.provisional`, `provisional_json`), §11 (`provisional` refusal, the observation's `provisional`, the board post's `contains`), §13a S-BTN-58 (docs@646eca6, the merge of docs#220)
**Depends on:** R3-1269 (its migration adds the columns; the claim path it reshapes)

### Requires

- none.

### Why

A worker that files a ticket and pushes it cannot claim it until a sync posts it, so it waits
or races. The spec now lets it claim at once, provisionally, and has Baton settle the claim
from the first sync that provably read the ticket's commit. Tier: critical — a new path into the
lease, and server-side claim ends.

### What to build

1. `POST /v1/runner/claim` with `ticket_id`, `course`, `leg`, `sha` for a ticket with no `tickets` and no `closed_tickets` row: refused unless the repository has an accepted snapshot, `leg` is the course's `start_leg` with no required bar, the key holds no other provisional claim, and the id has no `released:unknown-ticket` end in the last 24 hours; then the lease, grant, `min_worker_version` and owner-bar gates.
2. A provisional claim records a sync want; may make one `advance`, into `start_leg`; any later `advance` or end but a release is refused `provisional`.
3. `POST /v1/sync/observation` returns `provisional: [{claim_id, sha}]`; the board post's `contains` lists those `sha`s it contains; only such a post settles a claim, by R-BTN-104's five outcomes.
4. The alarm ends a claim unsettled after 60 minutes `released:unknown-ticket`, actor `alarm`; settlement ends use actor `server`; every end queues a `release` message; none counts in `failedAttempts`.

### Architecture — the decisions, named

- **Settlement is one pure function** `settle(claim, postedTicket | null, closed: boolean, gates) → 'ordinary' | endReason` in `src/core/claims.ts`, called from the snapshot transaction (`Claims.judge`'s neighbourhood) for each provisional claim whose `sha` the post lists. The author gates it re-runs are `gatesFor` in `src/core/gates.ts` with phase `'claim'`, not a copy.
- **"Unknown" is decided in the claim transaction** by reading `tickets` and `closed_tickets` there, so a sync landing between the check and the insert cannot let a known ticket through the open-world path.
- **`countsAsFailed` in `src/core/attempts.ts` returns false for `released:unknown-ticket`**, as it does for `stale-board`; extend its doc comment's list.
- **Do not**: let the worker's `sha` stand in for `contains` (Baton cannot check ancestry); settle on a post that does not list the claim's `sha`; recommend a provisional ticket; count a settlement end as a failed attempt.
