**Brief:** the Baton project; the owner, 2026-10-16: there can be a race between sync and claim, so Baton operates under the open world assumption.
**Spec:** `spec/BATON_SPEC.md` §4.5 R-BTN-104 / R-BTN-148 (the request, unknown, entry legs, the gates at the claim, provisional, settlement, ending), §9.7 (attempts: `unknown-ticket` does not count), §13 (`claims.provisional`, `provisional_json`), §14 (`provisional` refusal, the observation's `provisional`, the board post's `contains`), §15 S-BTN-58
**Depends on:** R3-1269 (its migration adds the columns; the claim path it reshapes)

### Requires

- none.

### Why

A runner that files a ticket and pushes it cannot claim it until a sync posts it, so it waits
or races. The spec now lets it claim at once, provisionally, and has Baton settle the claim
from the first sync that provably read the ticket's commit. Tier: critical — a new path into the
lease, and server-side claim ends.

### What to build

1. `POST /v1/runner/claim` with `ticket_id`, `course`, `leg`, `sha` for a ticket with no `tickets` and no `closed_tickets` row: refused unless the repository has an accepted snapshot, `leg` is the course's `start_leg` with no required bar, the key holds no other provisional claim, and the id has no `released:unknown-ticket` end in the last 24 hours; then the lease, grant, `min_runner_version` (`min_worker_version` in the code until B-68) and owner-bar gates.
2. A provisional claim records a sync want; may make one `advance`, into `start_leg`; any later `advance` or end but a release is refused `provisional`.
3. `POST /v1/sync/observation` returns `provisional: [{claim_id, sha}]`; the board post's `contains` lists those that are its `sha` or an ancestor of it; only the first accepted post containing a claim's `sha` settles it, by R-BTN-104 / R-BTN-148's outcomes as the spec now states them:
   - present at `start` (or past it by a path beginning with the claimed leg), every author gate passing → an ordinary claim — unless it is still at `start` and a posted-state gate fails for the claimed leg, which ends `released:stale-board` with no failed attempt (and taints the branch when the settling post is at the claim's sha);
   - under another course → `released:course-changed`; closed → ended as R-BTN-35 ends a claim on a closed ticket; absent → `released:unknown-ticket`;
   - at another station by another path → `released:stale-board`;
   - an author gate (the `requires` and tier gates over `declared`, judged again over the posted ticket) failing → the first matching row of R-BTN-148's table: `stale-board` (raising `baton.open-world-stale` for a later sha or another machine key) or `misdeclared` (a failed attempt with backoff, a 24-hour bar, `baton.misdeclared`).

   This ticket builds the outcomes that need no `declared`: ordinary, `course-changed`, the closed end, `unknown-ticket` and `stale-board` by position. The `declared` field, the author and posted-state gates, `misdeclared`, the tainting and `released:off-branch` (R-BTN-143) are B-46's (its items on open-world gates and adv-1 M7), built on this ticket's `settle`. Whichever lands second wires the other's half into `settle`.
4. The alarm ends a claim unsettled after 60 minutes `released:unknown-ticket`, actor `alarm`; settlement ends use actor `server`; every end queues a `release` message; only `misdeclared` (B-46's) counts in `failedAttempts`.

### Architecture — the decisions, named

- **Settlement is one pure function** `settle(claim, postedTicket | null, closed: boolean, gates) → 'ordinary' | endReason` in `src/core/claims.ts`, called from the snapshot transaction (`Claims.judge`'s neighbourhood) for each provisional claim whose `sha` the post lists. The author gates it re-runs are `gatesFor` in `src/core/gates.ts` with phase `'claim'`, not a copy.
- **"Unknown" is decided in the claim transaction** by reading `tickets` and `closed_tickets` there, so a sync landing between the check and the insert cannot let a known ticket through the open-world path.
- **`countsAsFailed` in `src/core/attempts.ts` returns false for `released:unknown-ticket` and `released:course-changed`**, as it does for `stale-board`; extend its doc comment's list. `misdeclared` is the one settlement end that counts (B-46).
- **Do not**: let the runner's `sha` stand in for `contains` (Baton cannot check ancestry); settle on a post that does not list the claim's `sha`; recommend a provisional ticket; count a settlement end other than `misdeclared` as a failed attempt.
