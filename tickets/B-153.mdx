**Brief:** the owner, 2026-11-01, on immediately-run/baton#154 (one ticket, one PR, one merge),
asked whether `build-app`'s plan tickets should keep the existing call-leg route under
`protected` (one approval per child) or ride the plan's PR: "Accept, batch later". This ticket is
the batching.

**Spec:** `spec/BATON_SPEC.md` R-BTN-165 (call legs), R-BTN-180 (*proposed, not filed*; the
proposal merge, "one approval covers one proposal"), R-BTN-171 (outbox git kinds).

### Why

Under `tickets.created: proposed`, a call leg's children are proposed one by one, and the proposal
merge accepts one approval per proposal. A `build-app` plan of twenty tickets, or an `epic`'s
children, therefore asks the owner for twenty approvals after they approved the plan itself. The
owner's attention is the factory's constraint.

### What to build

1. **The spec amendment first** (its own PR, two fresh reviewers, the impact list): all children
   one call run proposes are written on one branch, `baton/proposed/<calling ticket>-<run>`, and
   one `std:approval@1` whose subject is the calling ticket at that commit, covering every path
   and blob, lands them by one proposal merge. Decide whether a partial approval exists.
2. **The deliverer** writes one run's entries on one branch, and **the proposal merge** accepts
   the run's single approval.
3. **The call's settlement** counts the children once they land, as today.

### Tests

A scenario row in §15: a call run proposing three children, one approval landing all three; an
approval naming two of the three, refused.

### Exit criteria

- [ ] The spec amendment merged
- [ ] `npm test` green with the new scenario's cases
- [ ] `npm run typecheck` green
