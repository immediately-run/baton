**Brief:** the Baton project; the owner, 2026-10-15: recurring jobs such as the
decision-packet routine as Baton courses, one child ticket per run; legs that time out or are
cancelled administratively (that half is B-60's now).
**Spec:** `spec/BATON_SPEC.md` §6.5 R-BTN-89 (time waits, series), §4.2 R-BTN-74 and §4.4
(`not_before` in the board post), §5.3 R-BTN-83 (the closing marker's `not_before=`), §8.3
R-BTN-72 (the leg result), §6.2 R-BTN-77 (`unblocked`), §6.6 rule 1, §5.1 R-BTN-134 (course
directories), §2.1 (the alarm), §13 (`ticket_next_legs.not_before`, `ticket_next_legs_due`, the
`not-before-invalid` event), §14, §15 (S-BTN-51; G-BTN-79)
**Depends on:** R3-1257 (positions and closing markers, which carry `not_before`), R3-1258
(child tickets, which series use), B-43 (course directories, where `series` lives)

### Requires

- none — every case runs in the integration harness with fixture course files and a fake
  clock.

### Why

A recurring job — the decision-packet routine, a weekly audit, the weekly model check (B-50) —
should run on a course like any other work, with Baton's leases, history and dead-ticket
queue, which needs a leg that waits for a time. Tier: critical — a time wait changes what is
offered and when the alarm wakes. (Leg deadlines, the other half of this ticket as filed, are
B-60's: R-BTN-90 is now an interrupting `start` timer, §7.5.)

### What to build

1. **Migration** (one new numbered file): `ticket_next_legs.not_before` and the
   `ticket_next_legs_due` index (`state = 'waiting' AND waiting_on = 'time'`); the events
   CHECK's `not-before-invalid`.
2. **Time waits** (`src/core/time.ts`, R-BTN-89):
   - validate `not_before` on a ticket (`tickets[].not_before`, used only at `start` with an
     empty path), on a station's queued leg (`stations.<id>.not_before`) and on a suspending or
     releasing `end`, in `src/core/validate.ts`: RFC 3339 with `Z` or an offset, at most
     `NOT_BEFORE_HORIZON_DAYS` (400) after `this.now`, the one constant shared with
     `@immediately-run/baton/leg`. A failing value is logged `not-before-invalid`; a leg posted
     `ready` is stored `waiting` on `not-before-invalid` and raises `baton.not-before-invalid`
     until git is valid (through today's `needsYou()` path if this lands before B-57); a leg
     posted `waiting` on another label keeps it; no snapshot is refused. A value at or before
     `now` is stored NULL;
   - store `waiting` on `time` exactly when posted `ready` and `now < not_before`, re-applied in
     every transaction that writes `ticket_next_legs` (`src/core/stations.ts`) and by the alarm;
     `time` is reserved; a `time` wait raises no condition and is exempt from `waiting_since`;
   - the alarm, set early to the earliest pending instant, readies due legs (`ready_since` =
     `not_before`, `not_before` cleared), drops the repository's cached recommendations and
     queues R-BTN-77's `unblocked` with `cleared: [{kind: "time", ref: <not_before>}]` to the
     idle runners the ticket would pass the gates for, rung once R3-1260 lands; it calls no
     trigger or dispatch;
   - recommendations leave a `time` wait out and rule 1 refuses its claim; `not_before?` in the
     `next` entries of the heartbeat, claim, adopt and advance responses; the dashboard's
     "waiting until …" and the **Courses** view's list of pending waits.
3. **The CLI**:
   - `nextFire(cron, tz, after)` and `writeSuccessor(ctx, {schedule})` in the leg-script module
     `@immediately-run/baton/leg` (B-42's; whichever lands second exports them), with
     `NOT_BEFORE_HORIZON_DAYS`, which both enforce, failing the leg `cannot-finish:spec` past it;
   - `writeSuccessor` commits `<series>-<n+1>` on the default branch at or before the run's
     closing commit, with the same `parent`, `run: <n+1>`, its `not_before` (the next fire
     after the run's own, or after now if that has passed) and `depends_on` naming the current
     run; it writes nothing if that file exists naming this series and `run: n+1`, and fails
     `cannot-finish:spec` on a foreign file or an id the ticket-id rule refuses;
   - `not_before?` in the leg result, validated by the runner (`src/runner/protocol.ts` already
     carries it), written by `close` as the closing marker's `not_before=` and reported on the
     `end`; the default markdown ticket source's `not_before` field and the default `locate`'s
     decoding and posting of the marker's `not_before=`;
   - the runner suspends on a `time` wait more than 30 minutes away and waits under the claim
     otherwise.
4. **The `series` course**, a course directory `src/defaults/courses/series/` (R-BTN-134:
   `course.json`, `sync.mjs`, `legs/retire.mjs`, `legs/retire.md`): stations `active` and
   `retired`, leg `retire`. `retire` does no fan-in: it withdraws the pending run, then retires
   the series. Its children are its runs, ordinary tickets on the job's course.

### Architecture — the decisions, named

- **Time is git data**: Baton reads `not_before` from what the sync posts and never computes a
  schedule; a leg script turns cron into instants.
- **One clock**: every comparison uses `this.now` (R-BTN-32); tests set it with `setClock`.
- **The model**: `not_before` (R-BTN-89) is R3-1254 part 3's; whichever of the two lands second
  keeps the model's traces of a time wait matching core.
- **Do not**: loop a ticket through a timed leg; complete a leg from Baton; raise a condition
  for a `time` wait; refuse a claim for a `time` wait beyond what rule 1 does for any waiting
  leg; build deadlines, `deadline_at`, `cancel_at` or `leg-timeout` here (B-60).

### Tests

- Integration, both hosts: **S-BTN-51** (a series run due at T; idle days; a retried successor
  write; a foreign successor id; invalid `not_before`; a `ci` wait; a withdrawn run).
- Unit: `nextFire` across a DST change and a leap day; `not_before` grammar and the 400-day
  bound; the alarm's due scan at `not_before` ± 1 ms; the runner at `not_before` − 30 minutes
  ± 1 second; `writeSuccessor` with the run's `not_before` past and future, twice, and retried
  after the next fire; `nextFire` and `writeSuccessor` refusing beyond the horizon;
  `writeSuccessor` against a foreign file at its id; the stored state for a posted `ready` and a
  posted `waiting` on `ci`; the `series` course's `retire` withdrawing the pending run.
