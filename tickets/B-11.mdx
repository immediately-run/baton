**Brief:** the owner, 2026-10-16 and 2026-10-17, as specified by B-1 (baton#43); retries became
the default handler of `error` conditions on 2026-10-20 (§7).
**Spec:** `spec/BATON_SPEC.md` §7.4 (R-BTN-122 / R-BTN-23), §7.1 (R-BTN-160: `retryable`,
`LegError(type, …)`, untyped errors as `error.transient`), §7.3 (R-BTN-163: `baton.retries`,
`baton.contract`), §5.3 R-BTN-83 (held positions, `next_source: retry`), §6.1–§6.2 (the `retry`
prerequisite and row), §6.6 rule 10, §9.7 (what counts as a failed attempt), §9.9 (activity
`in-claim-retry`), §13.1's owner `retry` setting, §13.6's `leg_attempts`.
**Depends on:** B-10 (per-leg rows), B-55 (declared `conditions` with `retryable`, typed
`LegError`), B-56 (`baton.retries` and `baton.contract` as `suspend` core conditions), B-38 (held
positions with their `next_source`, for `exhausted`).

### Requires

None: a checkout and a terminal.

### Why

Today a ticket backs off as a whole on a fixed 1 h / 4 h schedule (`src/core/attempts.ts`), so a
failing `review` spends `implement`'s attempts and a loop accumulates failures across passes.
R-BTN-122 makes the retry policy per leg, the handler every `interrupt` condition falls to.

### What to build

1. **State.** A new migration adds `leg_attempts` (repository, ticket, leg, `attempts`,
   `first_attempt_at`, `retry_after`, `last_error_type`) and `claims.error_type`, and the
   `retry` block kind; it moves today's `ticket_overrides.retry_after` onto the queued leg's
   row. The row resets when a run of the leg reaches one of its outcomes (an `advance` or end
   reporting it, or a posted path extended by it) and when a hold is lifted.
2. **Policy.** `validateCourse` (`src/core/course.ts`) accepts a leg's `retry` with `initial_s`,
   `coefficient`, `max_interval_s`, `max_attempts`, `in_claim`, `exhausted` (a leg leaving the
   same station) and `exhausted_on` (declared types); a leg without one takes the repository's
   default. Whether a type is retried is its declaration's `retryable` (B-55) and the owners'
   floor; there is no per-leg `non_retryable`. An untyped exception is `error.transient`.
3. **Backoff.** After failed attempt *n*, `retry_after` = now + `min(initial_s ×
   coefficient^(n−1), max_interval_s)` × a uniform factor in [0.9, 1.1]; meanwhile a `retry` row
   on that leg (`until` = `retry_after`) refuses a claim or `advance` `backing-off` with `until`
   (rule 10, `src/core/gates.ts`), the leg leaves recommendations, and `baton leg why` shows
   `attempt: <n> of <max>`. The alarm clears the row at `retry_after` and `unblocked` follows.
   A backoff on one leg leaves the ticket's other legs alone.
4. **In the claim.** The runner (`src/runner/runner.ts`) re-runs a leg failing with a retryable
   type up to `in_claim` times under the same claim, waiting the schedule's first steps (at most
   5 minutes each); each re-run is an `in-claim-retry` activity, neither a run for `max_runs`
   nor a failed attempt. A provider at capacity is a blocked end (B-21), never an in-claim
   retry.
5. **Exhaustion.** At `max_attempts`, a spent `first_attempt` budget (`budget-spent`, set by
   B-61's timer) or a non-retryable class: Baton holds the ticket (`attempts-exhausted`,
   `budget-spent` or `non-retryable:<class>`), logs the reason and raises `baton.retries`;
   unless the policy names `exhausted`, in which case it queues that leg (`next_source: retry`,
   held via B-38 until the station changes) and raises nothing. A non-retryable type still
   holds unless `exhausted_on` lists it. `contract-input` holds at once, is not an attempt and
   raises `baton.contract`. `lease-lost` counts once per distinct runner.
6. **Owners' bounds.** The owners' `retry` setting (`PUT /v1/owner/{repository}/settings`)
   holds R-BTN-122's table: the default policy; ceilings on `max_attempts`, `max_interval_s`,
   `in_claim`, an interrupting `start` or `first_attempt` timer and `max_refreshes`; the owners'
   own `max_blocked` and `max_wait_s`; floors on `initial_s`, `coefficient` (below 1 refused by
   core and the kit) and the never-retried types `capability`, `spec`, `contract-input`. Baton
   applies field by field the stricter of course and owners, and the course view shows the
   policy in force.
7. **The kit.** `baton course test` warns where the bounds cut a policy back and when a floor
   type is not declared, and refuses a policy naming an undeclared type.

### Do not

- Let a course policy exceed the owners' bounds, or a course set `max_blocked`.
- Build `max_attempts_s` or `max_ready_s`: they are B-61's timers, and the validator refuses them.
- Retry an error naming a prerequisite: that is a blocked end (B-21).
