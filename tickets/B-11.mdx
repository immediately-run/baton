**Brief:** the owner, 2026-10-16 and 2026-10-17, as specified by B-1 (baton#43).
**Spec:** `spec/BATON_SPEC.md` §2m.6a (R-BTN-122), §6.10, R-BTN-23, R-BTN-66 and §7.2 rule 10 as amended.

### Why

§2m is agreed and specified; this ticket builds its part of it.

### What to build

1. A migration adding `leg_attempts`, `claims.error_type` and the `retry` block kind; rule 10 and the gate query read the leg's backoff.
2. The schedule, jittered ±10%, counted per ticket and leg; the alarm clears the row and fires `unblocked`.
3. The runner's `LegError` export and in-claim retries (each an activity, capped at 5 minutes).
4. The owners' `retry` setting: the default, the ceiling on every quota-spending field, `coefficient` ≥ 1, the floor; `baton course test` warns of cut-backs and of an undeclared floor type.

### Do not

- Let a course policy exceed the owners' ceiling.

### Tests

- S-BTN-83 to S-BTN-87; G-BTN-105, G-BTN-106, G-BTN-107.
- `npm test`, `npm run typecheck` and `npm run check:spec` green.
