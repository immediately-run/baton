**Brief:** [[home.mdx]]; the owner, 2026-10-15: a blocked leg leaves its ticket
queued at the station, so the legs between stations are queues, and a course must be able to
bound each — then, the same day: a ticket held outside a full queue would be a second, hidden
queue, so let it enter and issue a warning notice.
**Spec:** `docs/content/specs/BATON_SPEC.mdx` §2g R-BTN-79 (as revised in §2h's PR), R-BTN-75's
**held**, R-BTN-80 (the `queue-over` notice), R-BTN-83 (the queued leg); §2e R-BTN-69
(`max_queue`); R-BTN-52 (depths and bounds in the hook's board summary); §4 (`leg_queues`); §14;
§13a S-BTN-45 (its queue case); §16 G-BTN-71
**Depends on:** R3-1251 (held, the claim transactions), R3-1256 (notices and couriers), R3-1257
(the queued leg and re-routes, R-BTN-83)

### Requires

- none — every case runs in the integration harness with fixture course files and boards and a
  fake clock.

### Why

Without a bound, a fast leg can pile tickets in front of a slow one (reviews waiting on a
single fixer) and nobody hears of it. The bound is a warning, not a gate: git is anyone's,
advice never refuses, a bounded loop could deadlock under a gate, and holding the overflow
outside the queue would hide it. Tier: critical — queue counts move in the claim transactions.

### What to build

1. **Migration** (one new numbered file): `leg_queues` as §4 gives it.
2. **Course file**: accept `max_queue` on a leg, an integer from 1 to 10,000 (R-BTN-69), through
   `src/core/validate.ts`.
3. **`src/core/queues.ts`** — the one writer of `leg_queues`: a ticket is in L's queue while L
   is its queued leg (R-BTN-83; an owner re-route counts) and it is not held. `depth` changes
   in the transactions that accept a snapshot, start, end or advance a claim, confirm an end,
   clear a fence or re-route a ticket.
4. **The warning**: when L's depth rises above `max_queue`, log `queue-over` and queue a
   `queue-over` notice per `owner` courier of the repository through R3-1256's notices
   (`{leg, depth, max_queue, oldest_since}`), updated in place while L stays over; close them
   when the depth falls to the bound or below.
5. **Course intake**: a course is `closed` while any of its legs is over `max_queue` (logged
   `course-closed`/`course-opened`); while closed, recommendations leave out its new
   (at `start`, empty path), unexpedited tickets after the gate query, and a claim of one by id
   records `advice-overridden`.
6. **Visible**: depths, bounds and intake state on the dashboard's Courses view and in the repository's stats;
   each leg's depth and `max_queue` in the hook input's board summary (R-BTN-52), exposed by
   `queues.ts` for R3-1236 to read.

### Architecture — the decisions, named

- **Counted, never scanned**: depth is maintained in the transaction that changes membership;
  no request counts tickets.
- **Do not**: hold, block, refuse or reorder a ticket for a full queue (R-BTN-79's rejected
  designs: a refusing bound, feeder `queue` blocks, admission blocks); count a held ticket.

### Tests

- Integration, both hosts: **S-BTN-45**'s queue case — `fix` with `max_queue: 2` and three
  tickets queued for it: all three enter, the third logs `queue-over` and queues one notice
  per `owner` courier with depth 3 and bound 2; meanwhile the course is `closed` — a new ticket
  at `filed` is not recommended, an expedited one is, and a claim of a new one by id is accepted
  `advice-overridden`; claiming one drops the depth to two, closes the notices and reopens the
  course.
- `test/unit/queues.test.ts`: membership through each transaction that changes it (a claim, an
  end, a confirm, a fence clear, a re-route, a snapshot moving a ticket), a crossing both ways,
  a course-file change of bounds, the notice updated in place while over.
