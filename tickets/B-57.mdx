**Brief:** filed from the ticket plan for the current-state spec (PR #71). The owner's 2026-10-20
decision on conditions (`spec/BATON_SPEC_HISTORY.md`, "Decisions in full") makes Needs you the
debugger, and the rewrite turned every "listed under Needs you" into a `baton.<sub>` raise.
**Spec:** `spec/BATON_SPEC.md` §7.3 R-BTN-163 ("Every other core condition", `detail` under
§12.3, closing when the cause ends); the rules that name each sub: R-BTN-35 (§1, `regressed` at 30
minutes), R-BTN-63 (§5.3, `bars-mismatch`), R-BTN-69 (§5.2, `course-refused`), R-BTN-142 (§5.6,
`course-held`), R-BTN-83 (§5.3, `course-changed`), R-BTN-67 (§5.6, `required-bar`), R-BTN-75
(§6.2, `capability-missing`), R-BTN-76 (§6.3, `resource-unknown`), §9.6 (`unconfirmed`), R-BTN-79
(§5.4, `queue-over`); §11.4's Needs you view; §15 S-BTN-34, -37, -39, -44, -45, -79, -117,
G-BTN-71.
**Depends on:** B-56 (`raiseCore`/`closeCore` and the debugger's list).

### Requires

None: a checkout and a terminal.

### Why

`GET /v1/ui/state` builds Needs you from five `needsYou()` methods (`src/core/claims.ts`,
`blocks.ts`, `notices.ts`, `stations.ts`, and `courses.refusals()` and `keysNotice()` in
`src/core/baton.ts`). Each is computed on read and seen only on the dashboard. As conditions they
are rows a repository can bind (B-58), the outbox can deliver (B-64), and
`GET …/conditions` can list.

### What to build

1. **Raise in the transaction that finds the cause, and close when it ends.** Each is a `notify`
   core condition, using B-56's `raiseCore`/`closeCore`, with a `detail` of identifiers and
   numbers only:

   | Built listing | Condition | Raised | Closed |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | `regressed` (claims) | `baton.regressed`, ref the claim | the alarm, at 30 minutes regressed | the claim stops regressing or ends |
   | `bars_mismatch` (claims) | `baton.bars-mismatch`, ref the claim | the `bars-mismatch` event | the claim ends |
   | `policy` (claims) | `baton.required-bar`, ref `<course>:<leg>` | a leg stored `waiting` on `policy`, or a snapshot lacking the prefix | the wait clears |
   | `courses_refused` | `baton.course-refused`, ref the course | a refused course file or schema | a later file is accepted, or the course leaves |
   | course held for bars, where built | `baton.course-held`, ref the course | a file held (R-BTN-142) | the hold ends |
   | `course_changed` (stations) | `baton.course-changed`, ref `<course>:<leg>` | a queued leg stored `waiting` on `course-changed` | the wait clears |
   | `blocked_capability` (blocks) | `baton.capability-missing`, ref the capability | the alarm, 24 hours blocked | the block clears |
   | `instances_unknown` (blocks) | `baton.resource-unknown`, ref the instance | the alarm, 24 hours `unknown` while a posted leg needs it | the reading returns, or no leg needs it |
   | `unconfirmed` (claims) | `baton.unconfirmed`, ref the claim | the alarm at `ended_at` + 6 hours, or a snapshot undoing its legs | confirmed or released |
   | R3-1255's queue-over (`src/core/queues.ts`) | `baton.queue-over`, detail `{course, leg, depth, max_queue}` | depth rises over `max_queue` | depth back within it |

   - `queue-over`'s notice stays beside the condition until B-67 deletes it.
   - A condition on a repository has `ticket_id` NULL.
2. **Listings that are not conditions.** The spec names no sub for these, so they leave Needs you
   for the views §11.4 gives them:
   - `stale_skills` goes to **Runners**, as "needs a restart", per R-BTN-65 rule 9.
   - `unacknowledged_releases` and `fence_pending` go to **Running**, with the reap deadline.
   - `held_items` goes to the held list. Its retry holds are already on the floor as
     `baton.retries`.
   - Adoptions go to **Running**, with their confirm action (§16.1).
   - `keys_notice`: B-8 retires `BATON_KEYS`, so nothing is raised. If B-8 has not landed, it
     goes to **Runners**.
   - `pool_unclaimed` and `no_owner_channel` are dropped: the floor already lists every open
     posted decision (B-56), and B-67 deletes the pool.
3. **The dashboard.** `needs_you` is the debugger's list alone.
   - Delete every `needsYou()` method and their merge in `src/core/baton.ts`.
   - `src/ui/app.js` drops the old sections.
4. **Backfill.** A migration, or the first alarm after deploy, raises the conditions whose cause
   already stands, so nothing listed today disappears.

### Do not

- Store free text in `detail` (§12.3). It holds ids, counts and instants only.
- Compute a condition on read. It is a row written with its cause (R-BTN-163).
- Invent a sub name the spec does not give. Move the listing to its view instead.
- Remove the queue-over notice (B-67).

### Tests

- On both hosts:
  - `S-BTN-34.progress-not-staleness.test.ts`: `baton.regressed` raised after 30 minutes and
    closed when the path recovers.
  - `S-BTN-37.course-renames.test.ts`: bad files refused, the previous kept, `baton.course-refused`.
  - `S-BTN-39.limits.test.ts`: a leg missing a required bar waits on `policy`, and
    `baton.required-bar` is raised.
  - `S-BTN-44.blocks.test.ts`: `baton.capability-missing` after 24 hours.
  - `S-BTN-45.leg-queues.test.ts`: `baton.queue-over` raised and closed, the course `closed`
    meanwhile (G-BTN-71).
  - `S-BTN-79.schemas-refused.test.ts`: raised for a refused schema.
  - S-BTN-117: bars-held files raise `baton.course-held` once that hold is built.
- Unit: each row of the table raises once and closes when its cause ends; the backfill.
