**Brief:** the owner, 2026-11-20: groups exist so a team "can be alerted in broadcast when
human attention is necessary (or can be organized into escalation chains)"; "Don't store
addresses in Baton … It's the notifier's job to know where to find these"; and, on where the
notifier keeps its place, "Baton may store the typed input and output JSON of legs - could the
cursor not be persisted as such?". The design that came out of the second review round keeps no
cursor at all: the notifier attests each step it worked, a typed statement Baton stores, and
asks for what is due and unattested. This is §5 of [the users and groups proposal](../spec/proposals/users-and-groups.md), Baton's side.

### Decision needed

- **Attestation in place of a cursor.** The proposal's §9 took this without a ruling. The
  owner's question was whether the cursor could be a typed value Baton stores. **(a)** the
  notifier attests `std:notified@1` per step and holds no cursor *(default; recommended)*;
  **(b)** a cursor as a reading on a resource instance, which the review found lapses in the
  outage it is for, is not exported, and is writable by any runner granted on a listed
  repository; **(c)** a cursor in a local file on the notifier's host.

### What to build

1. **Recipients.** For each Needs-you entry: the users with a key matching the leg's
   `answerers` entry (B-305), else the users with a key that is an owner; `unreached` counts
   owner keys attached to no user.
2. **Steps.** The owner setting `escalations`, `{<label> | "*": [{after_s, notify}]}`,
   validated (8 steps, `after_s` 60 seconds to 30 days, `notify` of B-304's forms). Step 0 is
   the recipients, due when the entry opens; step n is due `after_s` later while the entry is
   open. Baton drops from a step everyone who could neither answer the entry nor read the
   repository as an owner. Removing a step or lengthening `after_s` is a loosening
   (`loosensSettings`).
3. **`std:notified@1`**, about an entry, `{step, told, unreachable}`: accepted on the
   attestations route from a writer the policy's `attestations` entry admits for the type,
   `service:` keys only; one per entry, step and writer. It joins the standard library
   (R-BTN-110). B-146 builds the general attestation record this rides on.
4. **The read.** `GET /v1/needs-you` across the caller's repositories and
   `GET /v1/repositories/{repository}/conditions`, one shape, with `steps` and `?due=1` (every
   open entry with a step due that no admitted writer has attested, resolved as the read is
   made). Callers: the conditions list's, and a key matching a label's `answerers` entry for
   that label's entries.
5. **`baton.notifier-silent`:** raised by the alarm where the policy admits a writer for
   `std:notified@1` and a step has been due and unattested for 15 minutes; closed when none
   remains; never an entry of `due=1` itself.
6. **Needs you** on the dashboard and `baton decision list`: each entry's recipients, its
   steps with what was attested, and any user a notifier reported `unreachable`.

### Trust

- `escalations`: an owner's setting (R-BTN-66), with loosening under `approvers.policy`
  (R-BTN-186). It decides who is told, never who may answer, and Baton filters every step so it
  cannot name a stranger to the repository.
- `std:notified@1`: the credential that posted it, counted only when the policy names its key.
  It is the notifier's word, not evidence that anyone read anything: no rule of Baton's may
  treat a told person as having seen the entry, and no leg waits on it (R-BTN-116: a leg waits
  on an effect, never a delivery). A notifier that lies can suppress its own sends and nothing
  else; a key the policy does not name is refused.
- The read: recipients' user ids and channel names go to every key granted on the repository.
  They are identifiers; no address exists to leak.

### Invariants

- BATON_SPEC [R-BTN-163](../spec/BATON_SPEC/07-conditions.md#sec-7-3): core names no channel and sends nothing. The proposal amends "records
  no delivery" to "stores the notifier's word of it"; the entry stays on the floor whatever is
  attested, and closes only as it does today.
- BATON_SPEC [R-BTN-116](../spec/BATON_SPEC/06-prerequisites-blocks-advice.md#sec-6-4): `expires` alone bounds how long a person leg waits. No step, attested
  or not, moves a ticket.
- BATON_SPEC [R-BTN-33](../spec/BATON_SPEC/01-what-baton-is.md#sec-1) and the owner's ruling: no address is stored. `std:notified@1` carries
  user ids and a step number, and its schema refuses any other property.
- BATON_SPEC [R-BTN-50](../spec/BATON_SPEC/01-what-baton-is.md#sec-1): core knows no course. Nothing here reads whether a ticket is on
  `deliver`; a `deliver` runner that should count is a key the policy names.
- BATON_SPEC [R-BTN-58](../spec/BATON_SPEC/11-people.md#sec-11-2): a key granted on A alone reads A's entries alone.
- BATON_SPEC [R-BTN-32](../spec/BATON_SPEC/02-deployments.md#sec-2-1): `due_at` and the 15 minutes read `this.now`.

### Tests

The scenarios *who an entry is for, and when*; *a notifier keeps no place*; *a silent notifier
is seen*, both hosts, with `setClock`.

### Exit criteria

- A person leg queued for a label shows its holders as step 0 and its later steps with their
  instants; a holder removed before a step is due is not in that step.
- A script that reads `due=1`, attests, and reads again gets nothing; a second script with
  another admitted key gets nothing either; a key the policy does not name is refused.
- With no `escalations` and no notifier named, every existing scenario passes and the
  conditions list answers as before, plus `recipients`.
- BATON_SPEC holds U-4 and the amended [R-BTN-163](../spec/BATON_SPEC/07-conditions.md#sec-7-3); the conditions proposal's delivery runner
  reads `needs-you?due=1`.

The change that builds a part moves that part of the proposal into BATON_SPEC, in the section
that owns each rule (`docs/context/spec-writing.mdx`): new rules and scenarios take their ids
