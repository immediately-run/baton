**Brief:** the owner, 2026-10-22, correcting the architecture: "a runner serves one course
is incorrect. A runner serves any course it has the capabilities to serve. Having a runner
process a single leg at a time makes sense (at least for now), but that's the only
limitation."
**Spec:** amends §6.13 (R-BTN-72, line 2038's "serves and declares one course") and
`docs/standard-courses.mdx`'s "A runner serves one course, since a claim does not say which
course its ticket is on"; §6.2's claim answer; rule 9 (§7.2) unchanged — S-BTN-26 already
drives a worker declaring two courses and being offered either.

### What the server already allows, and the one gap

Matching and rule 9 are multi-course-ready: a worker's declaration carries a list, and
S-BTN-26's W2 declares both `docs`' and `ops`' courses and is offered either. The
limitation is the CLI runner's own: `runnerCourse()` picks exactly one course (refusing a
checkout that declares several without `--course`), the heartbeat therefore declares one,
and the claim answer — the documented rationale for all of it — names the course's
*version* but not its *id*, though the core holds `claims.course_id` and joins on it
elsewhere.

### What to build

1. **The claim answer names the course**: `course_id` beside `course_version` in the
   claim's row (§6.2's answer shape; the field the whole correction hangs on).
2. **The runner declares every course its home declares**: `--course`, when given,
   narrows (a machine serving a subset); absent, all of `.baton/baton.json`'s courses go
   in the heartbeat, each with its version (rule 9 per course, as W2 already proves).
   The several-courses-without-`--course` refusal goes.
3. **The runner loads the claim's course**: `loadCourse` by the claim's named `course_id`
   from the claim's snapshot — a claim running `create-spec`'s `converse` and the next
   claim running `create-pr`'s `implement` are one runner's consecutive legs.
4. **One leg at a time stands** (rule 3, one claim per worker) — stated in the amended
   §6.13 as the runner's actual parallelism limit, with the owner's "at least for now"
   beside it.
5. `docs/standard-courses.mdx`'s "one runner per course, each in its own clone" section
   becomes the multi-course checkout's one-runner norm; the second-clone advice retires.

### Tests

- Extend S-BTN-26's runner half (or its CLI twin): a runner on a two-course checkout
  with no `--course` declares both, is offered and serves either's leg; `--course`
  narrows to one; the claim answer's `course_id` is what the runner loads.

### Exit criteria

- [x] one runner, one checkout, two courses, consecutive claims on either
- [x] the spec and the standard-courses page state the owner's rule: any course the
      capabilities serve, one leg at a time
