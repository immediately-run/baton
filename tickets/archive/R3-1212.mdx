### Requires

### Why

The lease is Baton's core: one active claim per item and per worker, claims handed out by
the matching rules, silent workers reaped, live ones fenced before re-issue, completions
confirmed by ancestry. It is built now and exercised by the harness in cut-over mode; in
production the mode stays `shadow` until R3-1220. Tier: critical — the two-holder invariant.

### What to build

1. `POST /v1/worker/claim` (idempotent `request_id`, nonce, both partial unique indexes) and
   `GET /v1/worker/next`.
2. `POST /v1/worker/claims/{id}/end` with the closed §6.5 vocabulary and R-BTN-8.
3. Matching: `isEligible` from `lib/baton.mjs` over the snapshot's `rankReady`, rules 1–9
   (rule 10 arrives with R3-1217).
4. The alarm: reap by silence at each worker's clamped timeout; the unacknowledged-release
   reap at 2 h; `fence_pending` and `fence-cleared` from the heartbeat's `fenced` report
   (R-BTN-19); the 24-hour auto-accept of a held snapshot.
5. `GET /v1/worker/items/{item}/holder`, `POST /v1/ui/claims/{id}/reap`, confirmations
   from the sync (§6.7) and the 6-hour unconfirmed flag.
6. Dashboard: Running, Timeline and Needs you views.

### Architecture — the decisions, named

- **Matching is the §7.2 SQL query** over `work_items`, `work_item_requires` and Baton's
  tables, then `isEligible` over the candidates for rules 2 and 8; the claim response carries
  the item's `path` and `snapshot_sha` (R-BTN-34).
- **GitHub wins (R-BTN-35).** On each accepted snapshot, an active claim whose item git now
  shows as done, archived, superseded, deferred, re-tiered above the holder's ceiling or gone
  is ended `released:stale-board`, and the holder gets a `release`.
- **The lock is the index.** Claim inserts and lets SQLite's unique indexes refuse a
  second holder; never check-then-insert without the index.
- **Mode gate.** Claim and end return `not-cut-over` unless `cutover_at` is set; the test
  harness sets it directly. Shadow behaviour (shadow-report) stays as R3-1211 built it.
- **Clamp** `lease_timeout_s` to [900, 86400] on every heartbeat (R-BTN-9).
- **Do not:** reap on a `last_seen_at` comparison read from `Date.now()` (the injected
  clock only); re-issue an item with an unconfirmed completion or a fence-pending reap; mark
  a `claim-ended` refusal as anything but that claim's (§6.9).
