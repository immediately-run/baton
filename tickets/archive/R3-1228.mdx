### Requires

- none — the review gate's reviewers run on the agent line; nothing here needs a venue or a person, unless a finding would change one of the owner's decisions (below).

### Why

The amendment is a proposal that the owner directed section by section, but no reviewer has
read it yet, and the spec's own history (five rounds on 2026-10-13, each finding blocking or
major) says a design of this size has seams no author sees. Every build item R3-1229–R3-1237
cites sections that exist only on docs#212 until it merges. Tier: judgement — a spec review
whose findings are design calls.

### What to build

1. The `review-gate` skill on docs#212: two fresh-context reviewers in round one, at most
   three rounds, every finding fixed and pushed or answered on its thread.
2. The merged PR, its findings in `content/plans/review-gate/pr-findings.mdx`, and a row
   per round in the spec's Review record.

### Architecture — the decisions, named

- **Review against the spec's own lenses**: internal consistency (sections that still say
  "work item", "workflow" for a course, or a gate that §7.2 now calls advice), seams with the
  unchanged 2026-10-13 text (R-BTN-4, -5, -10, -17 as amended), security (R-BTN-46, -52,
  -53, -54), and executability (can R3-1229–R3-1237 be built from it without a new decision).
- **The owner's decisions are fixed**: git and the PRs win on leg progress; Baton advises and
  gates only safety; hooks run in Baton, probes outside it; the names ticket, course and leg;
  the host `baton.immediately.run`; SSH keys for machine identity; one codebase on two hosts. A finding that would reverse one goes to the owner as a
  decision packet (`prepare-decision-packet`), not into a push.
- **Fixes stay in the spec**: a finding about an item's scope is fixed in that item's file.
- **Do not**: merge with an open finding; renumber R-BTN, S-BTN or G-BTN ids (items cite
  them); restore wording from the 2026-10-13 review record, which is history.
