### Requires

### Why

The owner wants a ticket to rest at a station between legs, suspend there until its next leg
can run, and Baton to advise rather than gatekeep anything but safety (2026-10-14). Today a
claim runs from claim to merge and every rule refuses. Tier: critical — the lease's
confirmation and staleness rules are rewritten around stations.

### What to build

1. Ingest each ticket's station, path, next legs and bars from the board post.
2. `advance {leg, evidence?, bars?, course?}`: finishes the running leg (the claim moves to
   its `to`) and starts `leg` when it leaves that station, its runs — in `start_path` plus
   `claim_legs` — are under `max_runs`, and the holder gates hold (owner bars only if
   `claim+advance`; required bars over posted plus declared `bars`); the response carries the
   latest snapshot's next legs and the course's advice. Every leg, the first included, starts
   with its own `advance`.
3. Ends `done`, `suspended:<station>`, `released:<reason>` with `reached` and `detail`; the
   station an end names must be the running leg's `to` or `from`; a suspension confirmed when
   the posted path **extends the expected path** (`start_path` + the claim's finished legs) by
   edges of the course file — git may be ahead of the claim — or by any later snapshot when it
   ended regressed; `done` by an end station or a recorded closure (R-BTN-10, R-BTN-71); a
   claim ended stale only when the ticket is taken away. A path shorter than git showed before
   (the previous snapshot's or `start_path`) sets `claims.regressed_since`, shows as
   `git.regressed` with the posted path in the heartbeat, is listed under **Needs you** after
   30 minutes, and clears when a later path extends the expected one again or at the end; a ticket is claimable again only after a sync since its last
   end; a resume record for every end; `off-graph` when a path does not extend by edges.
3a. Renames (R-BTN-64): on accepting a course file, rewrite active claims' `station`/`leg`
   through `renamed_from`; resolve every old name met afterwards — an `advance`'s requested
   leg, `start_path`, `claim_legs`, an owner bar's `legs` — and count `max_runs` under the
   resolved name.
3b. Holder-declared `bars` a later snapshot does not post for that leg log `bars-mismatch`
   (**Needs you**).
4. Recommendations: candidates per (repository, course), the default order (expedite,
   `to_end`, rank, age, id), R-BTN-61's merge, a 50-per-group limit; `next` and claim-without-id take the first
   that still passes after the hook; advice never refuses (`advice_overridden`).

5. *(Inherited from R3-1231, 2026-10-15.)* Accept the normative §7.1 board post — `tickets`
   (tier by name, `display`, opaque `course`), `closed`, `stations` — beside the interim shape
   docs posts until R3-1233; keep `closed_tickets` (R-BTN-10); refresh stations from a held force
   post (R-BTN-62).

### Architecture — the decisions, named

- **Gates in one function**, `gatesFor(worker, ticket, leg, phase)` in `src/core/gates.ts`
  (`phase` is `claim` or `advance`, which selects the holder gates); the SQL is its mirror
  and G-BTN-40 checks them against each other.
- **Baton never reads a station's or leg's meaning**: no literal leg or station name in
  `src/`; order is `to_end`, readiness is `state`, the edge check reads the course file.
- **The holder's word**: no leg state is checked at `advance`; only the edge, the run limit
  and the gates.
- **Do not**: confirm an end from an observation not greater than its own; end a claim stale
  because its station lags; let a bound, forecast or exhaustion refuse; keep
  `awaiting-review-capacity` reachable.
