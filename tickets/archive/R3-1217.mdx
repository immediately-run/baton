### Requires

- none — local Worker tests only.

### Why

Cutover makes Baton authoritative, and three server behaviours must exist first: an item
that defeats every claimant is held instead of retried for ever; the next claimant gets the
last one's work as a record; and items already in progress in git when the switch happens are
imported, not orphaned. Tier: critical — the cutover is one-way.

### What to build

1. Failed-attempt counting (§6.10: which reaps and releases count, `lease-lost` once per
   worker, the reset after a hold-lift), backoff (`retry_after` 1 h, 4 h), holds
   (`attempts-exhausted`, `non-retryable:<class>`, `review-capacity`), `held` events, rule 10.
2. Resume records (R-BTN-24) written at every non-completed end, and `previous_attempts` in the
   claim response.
3. `POST /v1/ui/cutover` (one-way; `cutover_at`), `GET /v1/worker/mode` reporting it.
4. Legacy import at cutover (one legacy worker per `in-progress` item, `reap_after` = claim
   note date + 7 days) and `POST /v1/worker/adopt` with owner confirmation on the dashboard.
5. Dashboard: holds and adoptions in Needs you; Lift hold; Cut over (with confirmation).

### Architecture — the decisions, named

- **Counting is a query, not a counter**: failed claims ended after the newest hold-lift
  `override` event, so a reset needs no extra state.
- **The cutover endpoint** refuses unless the newest snapshot is under 15 minutes old, and
  performs the legacy import in the same transaction.
- **Do not:** expose any call that clears `cutover_at`; count owner reaps or
  `provider-suspended` releases; auto-lift a hold.
