### Requires

### Why

R3-1223's round one made `board()`'s force branch reset a `held_snapshots` row that was
accepted or held against another head. Its round two then retired every row held against the
head being replaced on every head move, so every row left is a hold against the current head:
an accepted one makes `acceptedLine(head)` true (the force branch is skipped), an unaccepted
one is already held. The reset — the `UPDATE` branch, the `again: true` event flag, two of the
three clauses of `held` — is unreachable (a `throw` in it leaves the suite green), and the
docstring and the force-branch comment still describe it.

Tier: routine — dead code and two comments.

### What to build

1. In `board()`'s force branch: `held` is `!!known`; drop the `UPDATE` branch and the `again`
   flag; a first post of a sha INSERTs.
2. Rewrite the docstring and the force-branch comment: a hold is one row against the current
   head, deleted when the head moves; a later force post of that sha is a fresh hold.
