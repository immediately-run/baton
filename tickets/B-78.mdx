**Brief:** the coverage audit of the tickets against the spec (2026-10-20) found R-BTN-46 the one
requirement no ticket covers: no board-post `markers` are read, there is no `markers_seen`, and
nothing logs `foreign-write` or raises `baton.foreign-write`. Its scenario S-BTN-25 and gate
G-BTN-50 were uncovered with it. The adversarial review (adv-1 M6) also made the marker caps
truncate instead of refuse, and (m9) put the `branch` course's verdicts under the same rule.
**Spec:** `spec/BATON_SPEC.md` §1 R-BTN-46 (markers carry `claim=<nonce>`, `markers_seen`,
`foreign-write`, the first snapshot seeding); §4.4 (the board post's `markers` and
`markers_truncated`; markers never refused); §2.3 R-BTN-149 (20 markers per ticket, 20,000 in
all); §4.7 R-BTN-85 (the `foreign-write` sub); §3.4 R-BTN-139 (only trusted authors' markers are
posted) and R-BTN-96 (`branch` verdicts posted as markers); §4.3 R-BTN-62 (a held post's markers
taken as a refresh); §5.8 (the marker courses' `.baton/markers.jsonl`); §9.5 R-BTN-19 (a fenced-off
session-mode child noticed as `foreign-write`); §9.1 (the nonce is public); §13.4 `markers_seen`;
§15 S-BTN-25, S-BTN-54, S-BTN-122, G-BTN-50, G-BTN-142.
**Depends on:** B-46 (the fact adapters' `trusted` flag, R-BTN-139, which decides what is posted);
B-57 (`baton.<sub>` conditions); B-77 (`baton.station-jump`, the other half of S-BTN-25, and a held
post's refresh). R3-1265 writes the `branch` course's `Baton-Claim` trailers and posts its
verdicts as markers; this ticket judges them.

### Requires

None: every case runs in the integration harness with constructed PR facts and histories.

### Why

Baton cannot refuse a push or a comment (R-BTN-46), so overlap — a second runner writing on a
ticket it does not hold, a reaped runner's orphaned child still pushing, an agent approving its
own branch — must at least be noticed. Today the sync's station functions return `markers: []`
(`src/defaults/course-pr.ts`, `src/defaults/course-markers.ts`), PR closing markers write the nonce
as prose (`claim <nonce>` in `src/defaults/pr-close.ts`), and core ignores the post's markers. A
per-ticket cap that refused the post (as R-BTN-149 first read) would have frozen a repository's
whole board once one ticket collected 20 markers.

### What to build

1. **Markers carry the nonce** (`src/defaults/pr-close.ts`, `src/defaults/course-markers.ts`):
   every `baton:` marker a standard course writes — verdict, adjudication and conclusion lines,
   closing markers, the `baton:handoff` marker — carries `claim=<nonce>` in one parseable form;
   `.baton/markers.jsonl` lines keep their `claim` field.
2. **The sync posts markers** (`src/defaults/course-pr.ts`, `src/defaults/course-markers.ts`,
   `src/defaults/cli.ts`):
   - The PR-reading station functions return each trusted author's `baton:` marker on a live
     ticket's PRs as `{ticket, leg, ref, claim_nonce}` (`ref` the comment's, review's or PR body's
     forge id; `claim_nonce` null when absent). Untrusted markers are never posted (R-BTN-139);
     the sync log counts them per PR.
   - The marker courses' function returns each `.baton/markers.jsonl` line as a marker, `ref` the
     commit that added it.
   - Past the caps the CLI cuts, never refuses: each ticket's newest 20 by forge creation time
     (commit time for a file marker), then the newest 20,000 in all, naming the cut tickets in
     `markers_truncated`.
3. **The board route** (`src/core/board.ts`): `markers` and `markers_truncated` validated on write
   (§12.3's identifier rules; `claim_nonce` a nonce's form or null); the post is never refused
   for its markers.
4. **Migration** (one new numbered file): `markers_seen (repository_id, ref)` with `ticket_id` and
   `observation`.
5. **Judging** (a new `src/core/markers.ts`, called from the snapshot transaction in
   `src/core/stations.ts`):
   - For each posted marker whose `ref` is not in `markers_seen`: when its `claim_nonce` names a
     claim on that ticket that was active at or after the previous accepted snapshot's
     observation, nothing happens; otherwise Baton logs the discrepancy `foreign-write` (R-BTN-85:
     `{sub, baton: <the ticket's claims in that window>, git: <ref, leg, nonce>, observation}`) and
     raises `baton.foreign-write` on the ticket, naming the ref, leg and nonce. Every judged ref is
     then inserted.
   - A repository's first accepted snapshot only seeds `markers_seen`.
   - A held post's markers are judged as a refresh against the current head (R-BTN-62).
   - Baton reverts nothing, moves no ticket and gates nothing on a marker.
6. **Surfaces:** `src/core/stats.ts` counts `foreign-write` discrepancies (not the mirror's
   `foreign-claim` events) as foreign writes, and the dashboard's discrepancy list shows them by
   sub and age (R-BTN-85).

### Do not

- Refuse, revert or discount anything on a marker, or let a marker gate a leg (G-BTN-50).
- Check a commit, a signature or an author's identity beyond R-BTN-139's `trusted` flag.
- Post an untrusted author's marker.
- Refuse a board post for its marker count.
- Write the `branch` course's trailers here; they are R3-1265's.
- Edit an old migration.

### Tests

- On both hosts, a new `test/integration/S-BTN-25.two-runners.test.ts`: W2 pushes to W1's branch
  and posts markers under its own nonce and with none, logged `foreign-write` and raised, nothing
  refused and no commit checked; W1's next `advance` reports the moved branch; W1 writes a later
  leg's marker under its own nonce without advancing into it and suspends, raising
  `baton.station-jump` on the CI sync's post (B-77) with the suspension unconfirmed; a first
  snapshot over old markers seeding without raising.
- S-BTN-54's unclaimed `branch` verdict logging `foreign-write` (R3-1265's test, which waits for
  this ticket).
- S-BTN-122's marker case: a ticket with 25 markers, the newest 20 posted and the ticket in
  `markers_truncated`, the post accepted.
- Unit: the nonce-window judgement (a claim ended before the previous snapshot, one active
  across it, another ticket's claim, a null nonce); the caps' cut order; marker parsing of each
  `baton:` line form.
