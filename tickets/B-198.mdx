**Brief:** the owner, 2026-11-09 (B-191): "**Goals** `/goals` — the objectives: each goal's open
work in order, done count, and what blocks it." The goals themselves are B-122's (the owner,
2026-10-27): a named objective in a home repository whose open work ranks first, by priority.

**Spec:** [BATON_SPEC §11.4](../spec/BATON_SPEC/11-people.md#sec-11-4) (the Goals screen),
[§6.6](../spec/BATON_SPEC/06-prerequisites-blocks-advice.md#sec-6-6) and
[§3.1](../spec/BATON_SPEC/03-repositories-and-forges.md#sec-3-1) ([R-BTN-61](../spec/BATON_SPEC/03-repositories-and-forges.md#sec-3-1):
the default order and the merge, which B-122 extends for goals), [§6.2](../spec/BATON_SPEC/06-prerequisites-blocks-advice.md#sec-6-2)
([R-BTN-75](../spec/BATON_SPEC/06-prerequisites-blocks-advice.md#sec-6-2): blocks),
[§14](../spec/BATON_SPEC/14-api.md#sec-14) (`GET /v1/repositories/{repository}/goals`, as B-122
adds it). B-122's spec amendment is the home of what a goal is; this ticket cites it.

**Depends on:** B-193 (the shell), B-122 (goals: the files, the sync posting them, the order and
`GET …/goals`).

**Used by:** B-203.

### What to build

1. **The read.** B-122 adds `GET /v1/repositories/{repository}/goals` for its dashboard item;
   this ticket makes sure its callers admit a session (B-192's rule) and that it answers, per
   goal: `id`, `title`, `status` (`active`, `paused`, `met`, `withdrawn`), `priority`,
   `exclusive`, `needs` (ticket ids), `open` (the goal's open work in the order Baton offers it,
   each ticket with its state and position as B-194's `repo.tickets` gives them), `done` (count
   and ids), and `blocked` (the open tickets with their blocks, R-BTN-75, and the condition id
   where the block is an open condition). A goal whose `needs` are all done is `met` in the
   answer whatever its file says (B-122 item 6). If B-122 lands without some field, this ticket
   adds it to the row and the §14 row, in B-122's terms.
2. **`/repositories/{r}/goals`**: one card per goal, active first by priority, then paused, met
   and withdrawn behind a toggle: the title as text, the priority, `exclusive` as a marked
   word, a progress line (done of needed, as a bar with the numbers), then the open work as a
   `<table>` in order (ticket linking to its page, state with its shape mark, position, the
   critical-path length B-122 computes where the read gives it), and the blocked tickets with
   their first block's kind and ref — a block that is an open condition links to the Conditions
   screen's row (`/conditions/{condition}`), a missing capability names it, another ticket
   links to it. The board's expedite lane is not a goal; the card says nothing about it.
3. **The strip.** When a goal is `exclusive`, the repository's overview strip (B-193) carries the
   goal's title after the three counts — the one place the exclusive setting is visible without
   opening this screen.
4. **Phone.** Cards stack; the table collapses to one line per ticket.

### What not to do

- No goal editing: a goal is a file in git (B-122), changed by a commit; the screen draws no
  control.
- No ordering in the app: the order is the read's, as Baton offers it.
- No project references: a goal names tickets (B-122 item 2); the screen shows no project.

### Trust

- **`GET …/goals`** — Baton's rows from the posted goal fields (filter fields only, R-BTN-33):
  `id`, `title`, `status`, `priority`, `needs`, each untrusted text validated on the board post
  (§12.3) and drawn as a text node; the open work and blocks are Baton's own computation.
- Nothing is written.

### Invariants

- **Filter fields only** (R-BTN-33): no goal body is posted or drawn.
- **Positions are Baton's** (R-BTN-39); **blocks are stored** (R-BTN-75).
- **Text, never markup** (§12.3); **an owner's session, its repositories** (R-BTN-155).

### Tests

A scenario numbered at merge, on both hosts, with B-122's fixture (a goal needing T1, T1
depending on T2 and T3, T3 blocked on a person's condition; a second goal met; a third
`exclusive`):

- the read lists the goals with `open` in Baton's order, `done` counts and `blocked` naming the
  condition; the met goal is `met`;
- under `test/ui/`: the cards order active by priority; the blocked row links the condition;
  the strip names the exclusive goal; a hostile goal title is text.
