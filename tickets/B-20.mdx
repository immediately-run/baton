**Brief:** the owner, 2026-10-17: "any leg of a ticket may be blocked by an unmet prerequisite, which may be apparent before a runner starts completing the given leg, but can also become apparent during implementation. For example, because a language model provider's token capacity has been reached. This is not a new station in the course graph. It is simply a colored edge in the graph." Then "Go" on the proposal.
**Spec:** `spec/BATON_SPEC.md` — new §2m.6c, R-BTN-126; dated notes on R-BTN-75, R-BTN-122, §6.12's mid-leg exhaustion, the release reasons and §14.

### Why

R-BTN-120 blocks a leg on a prerequisite known before it starts. A prerequisite that fails while the leg runs has a general ending only for one course's model accounts (`provider-exhausted`). Without one, a VPN, a database or a provider's capacity lost mid-leg can only end as a retryable error, which spends retries on something that is not a failure.

### What to do

1. Add §2m.6c with R-BTN-126:
   - a leg's run ends `released:blocked` with the prerequisite that failed;
   - Baton writes the block row on that leg in the same transaction, and spends no attempt;
   - `provider-exhausted` becomes a case of `released:blocked`;
   - an error naming a prerequisite is a block, not a retry;
   - a blocked leg is drawn as a coloured, labelled edge, never as a station.
2. Add dated notes to every rule it amends, plus scenario S-BTN-91 and gate G-BTN-111, the wire rows, a Decisions entry, the CHANGELOG and the design brief.
3. Review it under the review gate.
