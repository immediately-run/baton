**Brief:** the owner, 2026-10-18 (this session): "a whole ticket should be assigned to a user so that they can carry out all the human-in-the-loop requests and interactive legs because they are working on the particular ticket"; "the default should be anybody with access to the given repository"; "it should be assignable to a person or a group"; and channel-specific courier configuration.
**Spec:** `spec/BATON_SPEC.md` R-BTN-78 (decisions), R-BTN-80 and R-BTN-103 (notices, personal channels, the pool), R-BTN-116 (person legs), R-BTN-86 (children), §7.1 (the ticket's posted fields). This ticket amends them; the amendment is its first deliverable.
**Depends on:** B-7 (person legs, decisions and notices).

### Requires

None: a checkout and a terminal.

### Why

A course's person legs, decisions and B-34's interactive legs are addressed today by a role label (`asks: owner`) and delivered to whichever couriers registered for it. Nothing says which person is carrying a given ticket. In a product build (brief → spec → roadmap → build), the person who defines the product should get every question that ticket raises, and should be the one whose session runs its interactive legs.

### What to build

1. **`assignee` on a ticket** (§7.1's generic fields), set in git by the ticket source. An identity is typed, `<provider>:user:<name>` or `<provider>:group:<name>`, so a person and a team never collide:
   - `github:user:joe`: a forge user;
   - `github:group:wiki-editors`: a forge team, in the organization that owns the ticket's home repository, or `github:group:<org>/<team>` to name another;
   - `baton:group:product`: a group recorded in git (2 below).

   Unset is the default and changes nothing: anyone with access to the repository may answer, and notices go to the couriers registered for the question's `asks` label.
2. **Groups, two kinds, both first-class.**
   - **In git:** `.baton/baton.json` `groups: {"<name>": ["github:user:<login>", …]}`, versioned and reviewed like the repository's other settings. A group's members are users, never other groups in v1. An unknown group or a malformed member fails the sync, naming the ticket.
   - **Forge-native teams:** the sync resolves a `github:group:…` assignee's members through the forge, as it reads PRs (R-BTN-96), and posts them with the ticket, so Baton itself still calls no forge.
     - Reading a team's members needs a token with organization read (a GitHub App's `members: read`, or a fine-grained token). The workflow's default `GITHUB_TOKEN` cannot read it.
     - A team the sync cannot read does not fail the board. The assignee is posted unresolved, its notices go only to channels registered for the group itself, and **Needs you** lists it with the reason.
     - Membership is read on every sync, so a change of team takes effect at the next one.
3. **Routing for an assigned ticket.** Its decisions and person legs notify the assignee's channels: a person's own, or every member's plus any channel registered for the group. The `asks` label still names the kind of question. **Needs you** lists an assigned ticket with an open question that no channel delivers to its assignee.
4. **Interactive legs (B-34)** are a leg capability, `interactive-session:assignee`, which core resolves per ticket to the assignee's identity (a member's, for a group; anyone's when unassigned). B-34 builds the resolution and the grant check. This ticket supplies the assignee and the group membership it resolves against.
5. **Courier routing per channel.** A personal courier is registered for people and groups as well as labels: `baton owner courier add <key> --for github:user:alice | github:group:wiki-editors | baton:group:product`. Each registration carries its own routing settings: a minimum severity (R-BTN-125), immediate or daily digest, its own `remind_after`, and a renderer (R-BTN-112). The channel's own details (a Slack channel, an email address, a push token) stay in the courier's configuration. Baton holds routing, never third-party credentials.
6. **Reassignment is a git edit.** Open notices are queued for the new assignee from the snapshot that posts it. A running leg keeps its claim.
7. **Who answered.** Routing only, in v1: anyone with access may still answer, and the answer's `answered_by` is recorded. Enforcing that only the assignee answers is left for later, as a filter (R-BTN-46), not a boundary.

### Open, to settle in the amendment

- **Children.** A child inherits its parent's assignee unless it sets its own (recommended).
- **Baton's own decisions** (`baton:retries`, `baton:contract`, `baton:stalled`) on an assigned ticket. Recommended: they go to the assignee, with the owner in copy.

### Tests

- A ticket assigned to a person, and one to a group: their decisions' notices reach exactly the assignee's channels (a member's, a group's own), with an unassigned ticket's going to the `asks` couriers as today.
- A forge team's members are resolved by the sync and posted with the ticket. A team the sync's token cannot read is posted unresolved and listed under **Needs you**, and the board still posts. An unknown `baton:` group fails the sync, naming the ticket. Reassignment queues notices for the new assignee.
- An interactive leg on an assigned ticket refuses a runner of anyone else. A group's second interactive leg goes to the member who took the first.
- A courier's routing settings filter by severity and batch a digest.
- A scenario (next free S-BTN number) through the local host, with its G-BTN row.
