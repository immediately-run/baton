**Brief:** the owner, 2026-10-27, on steering ticket selection: "how I can steer the ticket
selection process to ensure that Sandpit completion has precedence to less urgent matters …
the objective I have can only be attained by completing multiple projects, or some projects
completely, others only partially. A language model is clearly capable of determining which
tickets close the gap, which is most pressing, but I do not want Baton to be invoking a
language model during the ticket recommendation process." The owner approved this design the
same day.

**Spec:** §6 the default order and R-BTN-61 (the merge across groups); R-BTN-12 (expedite and
its lane bound); §6.1 (prerequisites, `depends_on`); R-BTN-98 (Baton runs no repository code);
R-BTN-33 (filter fields only); R-BTN-47 (home, qualified ids); §7.1 (the board post).
**Depends on:** none.
**Waits on:** B-113 (a dependency naming another repository's ticket). A goal whose `needs`
stay in its own home works without it.

### Why

Expedite is per ticket and bounded: an owner can expedite every Sand Pit ticket by hand, but
it does not follow the dependency chain, has to be redone as tickets are filed, and competes
with real emergencies (Fieldhouse's ticketing-system tickets already hold the lane). A
project is a label nothing ranks by, and an objective cuts across projects: the first Sand Pit
runner needs part of `sand-pit-hardening` and a ticket from `fieldhouse`. A repository's
`weight` is too coarse, and `rank.mjs` covers one course in one repository.

The judgement of which tickets close the gap is a model's, and belongs where the plan is
written: once, in a pull request the owner reviews. Everything after that is a graph walk.

### What to build

1. **A goal**: a file in a home repository, `<bundle>/goals/<id>.mdx`, carrying filter
   fields only (R-BTN-33): `id`, `title`, `status` (`active` | `paused` | `met` |
   `withdrawn`), `priority` (an integer, lower first among the active goals), `needs` (ticket
   ids, qualified once B-113 lands) and optionally `exclusive` (item 5). The body is prose
   for people; Baton never reads it. The sync posts goals beside tickets.
2. **A goal's open work** is every ticket in `needs` and, transitively, every open ticket
   they `depend_on`, computed by Baton at each accepted board post. `needs` names tickets,
   never projects: a project reference would grow silently as tickets are filed into it.
   No warning when a project a goal draws on gains a ticket the goal does not name: the
   owner, 2026-10-27, "I don't think we see proof that this warning is required yet."
3. **The default order** (§6) and **R-BTN-61's merge** gain one step after expedite: a ticket
   in an active goal's open work ranks by its best goal's `priority`, before every ticket in
   no goal. Within a goal, the ticket with the longest chain of open goal work behind it
   (critical path) ranks first, then the existing steps. A ticket in no goal keeps today's
   order unchanged.
4. **No model, no repository code.** The ordering is computed in core from posted fields; a
   course's `rank.mjs` still reorders within its group on the runner, and receives each
   candidate's goal and critical-path length in its view so it can keep the goal order.
5. **Soft by default.** Goal work ranks first; other work still runs when no goal ticket
   passes the gates (waiting on review, on a person, on a capability no runner holds).
   `exclusive: true` makes it hard: Baton offers no ticket outside the goal's open work in
   that home while the goal is active. Logged as a goal setting, not a hold per ticket.
   (`exclusive` accepted by the owner, 2026-10-27.)
6. **Progress and blocks.** `GET /v1/repositories/{repository}/goals` and the dashboard: per
   goal, its open work and done count, the ready tickets in order, and the blocked ones with
   their blocks (R-BTN-75), so Needs you shows when a goal waits on a person rather than on
   runners. A goal whose `needs` are all done is shown as met; the file's `status: met` is
   git's to write.
7. **The vocabulary page** gains **Goal**: "A named objective in a home repository: the
   tickets it needs, and its priority. Baton ranks a goal's open work first, by priority."

### Tests

- **S-BTN-172, goals steer the order:**
  - a goal needing T1, where T1 depends on T2 and T3: T2 and T3 are offered before an older,
    unrelated ready ticket, and T1 once they are done;
  - two active goals: the lower `priority`'s open work first; a ticket in both ranks by the
    better one;
  - within a goal, the ticket that unblocks more goal work first;
  - expedite still wins over goal work;
  - a goal ticket blocked on a person: unrelated work is offered (soft), nothing outside the
    goal is offered with `exclusive: true`, and the goal view lists the block;
  - a goal set `paused` changes nothing in the order; one whose needs are done reads as met;
  - a goal naming an unknown ticket is reported on the board post, not accepted silently.
- The model: goal ordering as a pure function of the posted board, with `model:pr`'s report.
