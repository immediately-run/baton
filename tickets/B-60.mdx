**Brief:** carved out of R3-1259 (its items 4–6) by the ticket plan for the current-state spec (PR
#71). `max_duration` is now a timer (R-BTN-167). The owner, 2026-10-15: legs that time out or are
cancelled administratively.
**Spec:** `spec/BATON_SPEC.md` §7.5 R-BTN-90 (the `start` deadline, `leg_finished`, the deadline,
the owner's controls) and R-BTN-167 (the timer's shape and range, interrupting timers); R-BTN-122
(§7.4, the owners' 7-day ceiling on an interrupting `start` timer); R-BTN-19 (§9.5, fence before
re-issue) and §9.5's reaping; §9.4's end table (`released:leg-timeout`, actor
`alarm:leg-timeout`); R-BTN-149 (§2.3, once per leg run); §6.8 (Cancel); R-BTN-69 (§5.2, the
validator); `spec/BATON_SPEC_HISTORY.md`, "Migrations the implementation still carries" (R-BTN-170's
window: `max_duration`); §13.5 `claims`; §14 (the heartbeat, the deadline route); §15 S-BTN-52,
G-BTN-80.
**Depends on:** nothing. R3-1257, which provided positions, is done.

### Requires

None: every case runs in the integration harness with fixture course files and a fake clock.

### Why

`src/core/course.ts` accepts and validates `max_duration` but nothing enforces it. A leg that hangs
holds its ticket until the runner's lease lapses, and an owner has no way to stop work at a set
time. R3-1259 scoped this when `max_duration` was a leg field. The spec now makes it a timer, and
the deadline is independent of `not_before`, so it is its own ticket.

### What to build

1. **The timer, in the course file** (`src/core/course.ts`, the kit):
   - A leg's `timers: [{after_s, from: "start", interrupting: true}]` with `after_s` from 60 to
     2,592,000.
   - It is further bounded by the owners' 7-day ceiling. B-11 makes the ceiling a setting; until
     then it is a constant.
   - Any other `from`, a timer with `leg`, or a non-interrupting timer is refused naming the leg
     and the field, until B-61 accepts them.
   - At most one `start` timer per leg.
2. **The window.**
   - A leg's `max_duration` is read as `{after_s: max_duration, from: "start", interrupting:
     true}`.
   - It logs `deprecated-field` once per repository and field per day. Whichever of B-41 and B-60
     lands first adds that event kind.
   - A leg carrying both forms is refused.
   - B-67 refuses `max_duration`.
3. **Migration** (a new numbered file):
   - `claims.deadline_at` and `claims.cancel_at`;
   - `leg-timeout` in the release reasons, the `released:` end reasons
     (`src/core/validate.ts` `RELEASE_END_REASONS`) and the events.
   - R3-1259 keeps only its `not_before` columns.
4. **The deadline** (a new `src/core/deadlines.ts`):
   - An `advance` into a leg with a `start` timer sets `deadline_at` to `this.now + after_s`. The
     next `advance` and the claim's end clear it.
   - The runner sends an immediate heartbeat with `leg_finished: <claim_legs.seq>` when the
     script's `run` returns. It comes before validation, `close`, and any wait under the claim.
   - That heartbeat re-arms `deadline_at` to now + 40 minutes, once per leg run.
   - It is processed before messages are delivered. A pending `leg-timeout` release for that leg
     is withdrawn: the message becomes `expired` with `withdrawn: 'leg-finished'`, and
     `leg-timeout {withdrawn: true}` is logged. A release already delivered stands.
5. **At the deadline** (the alarm in `src/core/baton.ts`):
   - The alarm queues a `release` with reason `leg-timeout` and logs `leg-timeout`.
   - The runner aborts at its next safe point, writes its hand-off and ends
     `released:leg-timeout`. That end is a failed attempt (`src/core/attempts.ts`).
   - A delivered `leg-timeout` release unacknowledged for 15 minutes is reaped by the alarm with a
     fence, actor `alarm:leg-timeout`: `fence_pending` with its `fence_incarnation` and
     `fence_deadline` (the reap instant plus the runner's `lease_timeout_ms`). The ticket is not
     re-issued until the sidecar reports `fenced: {claim_id, terminated: true}`, or until
     `fence_deadline` passes, whatever the runner does meanwhile — a re-key, a new incarnation's
     heartbeats or silence change nothing — and the alarm clears it (R-BTN-19, adv-3 F1). This
     reap counts as the `leg-timeout` end does. This ticket's reap uses the fence path the code
     has today; `fence_deadline`, its alarm clear and `baton.fence-pending` for every live
     runner's reap, this one included, are B-79's, which follows this ticket. Write no clearing on
     the runner's silence here.
6. **The owner's controls:**
   - `POST /v1/owner/{repository}/claims/{id}/deadline` and `baton owner deadline <claim>
     --extend-to <t> | --cancel-at <t|none>`. Each act is logged `override`.
   - `extend_to` must be later than both now and the current deadline, and at most 7 days ahead.
     It is `invalid-params` when the claim has no deadline or once the release is queued.
   - `cancel_at` must be after now and within 7 days, and `null` withdraws it. At that instant it
     runs a Cancel: an owner hold and a `release` with reason `owner-cancel`.
   - If the claim ends first, the schedule lapses, is logged `override {lapsed: true}` and is
     shown on the ticket's row.
   - An ended claim is `claim-ended`.
   - The heartbeat response's claim and the dashboard's claim row carry `deadline_at` and
     `cancel_at`.
7. **The model** (R-BTN-68): add to `model/baton.qnt` the `leg-timeout` release, its fenced reap,
   and `leg_finished` clearing the deadline. Add the properties that every fence clears and that
   no leg finishing inside its budget is timed out. Attach the `npm run model:pr` report.

### Do not

- Compute the deadline from the runner's clock. Use only `this.now` (R-BTN-32).
- Queue a timer's `leg` or arm `arrival`, `ready` or `first_attempt` timers. Those are B-61's.
- Treat a `leg-timeout` release as anything but a failed attempt here. The `leg` exception is
  B-61's.
- Edit an old migration.

### Tests

- On both hosts, `test/integration/S-BTN-52.deadline-cancel.test.ts`:
  - a 1,800-second interrupting `start` timer;
  - runs finishing before, at and after it, with and without delivery;
  - a hung harness reaped with a fence at +15 minutes;
  - `extend_to`, `cancel_at` and the invalid forms of each.
- Unit:
  - `after_s` at 59, 60, 2,592,000 and 2,592,001, and above the 7-day ceiling;
  - `max_duration` read as a timer and logged once a day;
  - `deadline_at` across advances and `leg_finished`;
  - the withdrawal of a pending release;
  - the reap at 15 minutes ± 1 ms;
  - `cancel_at` lapsing with its claim.
- Model: `npm run model` with the new actions and properties.
