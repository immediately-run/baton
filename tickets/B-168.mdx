**Spec:** BATON_SPEC as the restructure's part 4 left it: [R-BTN-76](../spec/BATON_SPEC/06-prerequisites-blocks-advice.md#sec-6-3) (two scopes; every
runner granted on a repository has the same usable instances; an administrator's reading is
one more instrument's; no signature), [R-BTN-77](../spec/BATON_SPEC/06-prerequisites-blocks-advice.md#sec-6-2) and [R-BTN-126](../spec/BATON_SPEC/06-prerequisites-blocks-advice.md#sec-6-2) (the block is the same for every
runner), [R-BTN-181](../spec/BATON_SPEC/12-security.md#sec-12-8) and [R-BTN-188](../spec/BATON_SPEC/12-security.md#sec-12-8) (`readings: {writers}`, no `signed`), [R-BTN-66](../spec/BATON_SPEC/01-what-baton-is.md#sec-1) (the settings),
[R-BTN-106](../spec/BATON_SPEC/08-runners.md#sec-8-3) (no `required_independence`), [R-BTN-94](../spec/BATON_SPEC/03-repositories-and-forges.md#sec-3-3) (no `trigger_workflows`: the forge dispatches
only a workflow that declares a manual dispatch), [R-BTN-99](../spec/BATON_SPEC/06-prerequisites-blocks-advice.md#sec-6-7) (reports kept 6 hours, no
`report_horizon_h`), [§13](../spec/BATON_SPEC/13-data-model.md#sec-13) and [§14](../spec/BATON_SPEC/14-api.md#sec-14). The owner's ruling, as B-165 quotes it: "Take review's
default". History: the changelog entry "the restructure, part 4".

### What to build

1. **Resources.** `baton admin resource add` takes `--scope account|service` only; a new
   migration drops `runner_instances` (the built `worker_instances`), `external_resources.atoms_from`,
   every `runner`-scope (built: `worker`) and `host`-scope registration and the readings of their
   implicit instances (`<resource>@worker:<id>`), logging each dropped registration as an
   `admin` event; usable instances are the repository's (`src/core/resources.ts`);
   `resource_fleet` becomes `usable_instances`, `open_instances`, `live_runner` (§13.7).
   The heartbeat's `uses` and the claim's `bills` are ignored and logged `deprecated-field`
   while the history's Migrations entry "part 4's retired fields" lasts, then refused;
   `refused_uses` and the claim answer's `refused` go.
2. **The administrator's reading.** `POST /v1/admin/readings` stores one more instrument's
   reading under R-BTN-76's limits; no precedence, no 7-day window, no `overridden` answer.
3. **Readings' signature.** `POST /v1/runner/readings` and `/v1/probe/readings` ignore
   `nonce` and `signature` (logged `deprecated-field`, as above); a migration drops
   `instance_readings.signature` and `nonce`; an administrator's reading has writer class
   `admin`; the policy's `readings` entries lose `signed` (export and import
   tolerate an old `signed` and drop it); the trusted-leg check of R-BTN-181 admits writers only.
4. **Settings.** Drop `required_independence` (rule `chain` reads `no_chain_from` alone),
   `trigger_workflows` (the unblocked trigger names a workflow on the default branch) and
   `report_horizon_h` (6 hours) from the settings route, the course entry (ignored and logged
   while the Migrations entry lasts), the dashboard's limits and the export, each by a new
   migration; this repository's `.baton/baton.json` drops `report_horizon_h`. Setting or
   changing the unblocked trigger is a loosening under `approvers.policy` (R-BTN-94,
   R-BTN-186); Baton reads no workflow file — a forge's refusal of the dispatch is logged with
   its reason.
5. **Tests.** Update `S-BTN-45.block-edges` (an administrator's reading after a probe's: the
   later sets the state), `S-BTN-63.no-chain` (no owner setting), and the unit tests naming the
   removed fields.
