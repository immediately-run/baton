**Brief:** [[projects/baton.mdx]]; the owner, 2026-10-15: as instruments push readings in, a
service outside Baton pulls human-in-the-loop requests out — claiming them, then marking them
sent; the simplest courier is a CLI that shows them.
**Spec:** `docs/content/specs/BATON_SPEC.mdx` §2g R-BTN-80 (and R-BTN-78, the decisions it
carries); R-BTN-13 and R-BTN-57 (credential kind `courier`, one role per key); §4 (`couriers`,
`notices`, `notices_due`, `notices_alarm`, `credentials.kind` `courier`,
`repositories.remind_after_s`); §10.6 (owners register couriers and set `remind_after`); §11
(`POST /v1/courier/notices/claim`, `POST /v1/courier/notices/{id}`, `GET
/v1/courier/decisions`, `POST /v1/owner/{repository}/couriers`, the `closed` error code); §13a
S-BTN-46; §16 G-BTN-72 (docs@630a51c4, docs#213 as merged)
**Depends on:** R3-1251 (`ticket_decisions` and the decision blocks, the `unblocked` event; it
depends on R3-1240 for owner keys and the `baton owner` CLI)

### Requires

- none — every case runs in the integration harness with fixture keys and a fake clock.

### Why

Filed from R3-1251. A decision block (R-BTN-78) stops a ticket until a person answers, but
nothing tells the person; today the owner finds packets by running a skill. Couriers let any
outside service — a laptop CLI, a phone-push relay — pull requests out of Baton, while Baton
itself sends no mail and calls no service. Tier: judgement — it shortens the owner's loop but
gates nothing.

### What to build

1. **Migration** (one new numbered file): `couriers`, `notices` with `notices_due` and
   `notices_alarm`, the `courier` credential kind, `repositories.remind_after_s` (default
   86,400, from 3,600 to 604,800).
2. **Registration**: `POST /v1/owner/{repository}/couriers` `{public_key, asks}` or
   `{fingerprint, revoke: true}` and `baton owner courier add <key> --asks owner`; a key already
   registered as a worker, probe, owner or administrator key is refused `conflict`; another
   repository's owners may add an existing courier key to theirs; revoking closes that
   repository's notices for the courier, and the credential goes when no repository registers
   it or an administrator revokes it. A courier added, or given a new `asks`, is queued a notice
   for each decision already open there that it now delivers. `remind_after` joins the owner
   settings (`PUT /v1/owner/{repository}/settings`).
3. **Queueing**: in the transaction that first stores a `ticket_decisions` row, one notice per
   courier of the ticket's repository registered for its `asks` — `{notice_id, kind: 'decision',
   repository, ticket, title, decision: {id, asks, url?}, unblocks, created_at}`, all text under
   §10.4.
4. **Lifecycle** (`src/core/notices.ts`, the one writer of `notices`): `queued` → `claimed` →
   `sent`; `POST /v1/courier/notices/claim` takes up to 50 queued notices of the courier's own,
   each leased 10 minutes; `POST /v1/courier/notices/{id}` `{state: 'sent'|'failed', channel?}`
   — `failed` re-queues after 1, 5, 30 minutes, then hourly; a lapsed lease re-queues; a mark on
   a closed notice is answered `closed`, a mark from a lapsed lease is accepted, another
   courier's notice is `forbidden`. A sent notice still open after `remind_after` is queued
   again. The alarm drives leases, backoffs, reminders and deletion through `notices_alarm`.
5. **Closing**: the snapshot that stops posting a decision closes its notices in that
   transaction, whatever their state; a closed notice is deleted after 7 days.
6. **`GET /v1/courier/decisions`**: every open decision the courier delivers over its
   repositories and `asks`, with each notice's state — the courier's own read of
   `blocked?on=decision:*`.
7. **`baton inbox`**: claims the user's key's notices, prints them as text (title, question,
   link), marks each `sent`; `--watch` asks once a minute; `--all` lists `GET
   /v1/courier/decisions`.

### Architecture — the decisions, named

- **Baton sends nothing**: no mail, no push, no outbound call; delivery is the courier's.
- **One notice per courier**, not one shared, so a CLI and a push service each get theirs; at
  least once, deduped by `notice_id`.
- **The answer stays in git**: a notice carries the link; nothing in Baton records an answer.
- **Text, never markup**: titles, decision ids and URLs are passed as text and printed by
  `baton inbox` as text.
- **Do not**: let a courier see another repository's notices; let a key hold two roles; store a
  packet's body or question text beyond §4's columns (R-BTN-33); close a notice on anything but
  the snapshot that drops its decision or a revocation.
