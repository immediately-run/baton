### What to build

The spec is the authority; where this list and a rule differ, the rule wins.

1. **The knob (R-BTN-188).** `repos.unlisted` (`by-name` | `none`) in the policy document, its
   validation, `policy show | diff`, and the presets: `cooperative` and `plain-git` write
   `by-name`, `protected` and `protected-hardware` write `none`. A stored version that does not
   name it reads `by-name`; no guarantee reads the knob. The dry run names, in
   `stops.unreached`, the live tickets whose `repos:` entries a change to `repos.unlisted`
   leaves without reach.
2. **`repos.unlisted` (R-BTN-140).** `by-name` is the code's present reading for a home that
   lists no `code_repos` (`src/core/codeRepos.ts`, `src/defaults/cli.ts`), with two things the
   rule adds: Baton judges it from the posted path (host and owner equal to the home's), and a
   registered repository is reached by name only while its own policy says `by-name`. Under
   `none` the reading ends: the entry has no reach and its ticket raises
   `baton.repo-not-listed`. The observation hands the sync the knob as `repos_unlisted` (§4.3,
   §14). R-BTN-92, R-BTN-96, R-BTN-139, R-BTN-141 and R-BTN-26 read "with reach".
3. ~~**`executor.builtin` (R-BTN-107, §6.6).** Rule `executor` at the claim only, refusal
   `executor-refused`, for a runner whose last heartbeat reported `builtin` or no `executor` (a
   sidecar), on every repository the ticket reaches whose policy says `refused`.~~ Removed: the
   simplification of 2026-11-19 took the knob, rule `executor` and `executor-refused` out of the
   spec on the owner's ruling, and nothing replaces them.
4. **`last_written_by.human` (R-BTN-194).** Decided in the transaction that records
   `last_written_by`, against the policy then in force, stored in its JSON and never recomputed;
   rows recorded before this ticket read `false`. Served wherever `last_written_by` is; printed
   at the claim and by `baton ticket show`.
5. **The `out-of-course` finding (§12.9, R-BTN-73).** Everything its bullet says: the paths
   judged by the floor's outright globs with R-BTN-184's match grammar; accounting only by a
   record naming the delivery's head, for a ticket whose scope covers the repository, in
   either order — a trusted leg's `ended_at_sha` from a runner granted `trusted-runner` on the
   pushed repository, or a `pr-merged` arrival from the fact reader that repository's policy
   names; one finding per head, open or closed; the truncated delivery; what the row stores
   (sixteen paths and a count, the commit ids); `resolved_by` `baton`; an owner's resolve
   refused `not-found` until listed; the 15 minutes from `raised_at` by `this.now` at the
   read; the line and its `unrecorded paths`. The merge leg's end names the merge result as
   `ended_at_sha` (R-BTN-183): check the shipped `merge` leg does.
6. **The schema.** A new numbered migration (its number from `baton tickets next migration`)
   widens `scope_findings.kind`, whose CHECK (`0034_scope_audit.sql`) names two kinds today,
   and `events.kind` if its CHECK enumerates kinds.
7. **R-BTN-184's floor** as amended: `.github/**`, `CODEOWNERS` and `docs/CODEOWNERS`,
   `.codex/**`, wherever the floor is computed (the merge runner's refusal, the protection
   print).

### Exit criteria

- S-BTN-239 and S-BTN-240 pass as their rows read; S-BTN-220 passes with `human`.
- A `protected` policy stored before the knob holds every guarantee it held (R-BTN-189).

### Trust

- **The policy knob**: written only by an `approvers.policy` approver (R-BTN-188), validated
  on write, never read from git.
- ~~**A runner's `executor.source`**: the runner's own statement on its heartbeat (a machine key).
  Rule `executor` trusts it only to stop a misconfigured host; a lying runner passes, and the
  spec says so (R-BTN-107).~~
- **Push records**: Baton's own record of the forge's deliveries, verified in the Worker
  (`X-Hub-Signature-256`, R-BTN-73). The finding reads `sender`, `after` and the commits' paths
  from them and nothing a board post or a runner says (reach 7). A pusher controls the paths
  its own push names only by changing them, which is what the finding reports.
- **`pr-merged` arrivals and a trusted leg's reported end**: written by the fact reader's
  `service:` key the owner named (R-BTN-192) and by a trusted runner's claim (R-BTN-182); a
  `sync`-written arrival accounts for nothing. Only a runner granted `trusted-runner` on the pushed repository, and the fact reader its
  own policy names, account for its pushes: another home's trusted runner does not. A
  compromised fact reader or merge runner of the repository can account a finding away; that
  is inside §12.8's
  "a compromised host of trusted runners".
- The finding is information: it grants nothing, so a forged delivery could at worst list a
  false line under Needs you.

### Invariants

- R-BTN-73: a notification never computes or carries state beyond its named products; the
  finding is one of them, from the delivery's own fields, with no repository content read.
- R-BTN-32: the 15 minutes are compared against `this.now` at the read; no alarm, no
  `Date.now()`.
- Migrations are append-only: one new file widens the CHECK; 0034 is not edited.
- R-BTN-33: the row stores identifiers only (pusher, commit, paths), no file content.
- R-BTN-189: the knob is no guarantee's condition; adding it breaks no stored floor
  (test: a `protected` policy stored before the knob keeps every guarantee it held).
- R-BTN-66: under `paths.control: any` nothing is checked and nothing is raised.
- R-BTN-31: S-BTN-239, S-BTN-240.
