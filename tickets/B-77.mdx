**Brief:** the coverage audit of the tickets against the spec (2026-10-20) found that the
adversarial review's fixes to heads, held posts and owner state (spec/BATON_SPEC_HISTORY.md,
"Adversarial review (2026-10-20)", adv-1 M1–M3, M5, m1, m2; adv-3 F4, F8; adv-4 B2) are built
only in their older form and ticketed nowhere. This ticket builds them.
**Spec:** `spec/BATON_SPEC.md` §4.3 R-BTN-11 (held rows, acceptance, `contains`, `posted_by`),
R-BTN-62 / R-BTN-150 (numbers issued per credential, release with `behind`, lapses, a held post's
refresh) and R-BTN-147 (founded heads, the lagging fetch, the known sha, the tighten-only list,
`station-jump`, `ticket-dropped`, `machine-loosened`); §4.4 (`contains`, a returning ticket's kept
state); §5.3 R-BTN-71 and §9.6 R-BTN-10 (arrivals beyond the expected path); §6.8 R-BTN-12 (owner
state outlives the board); §1 R-BTN-66 (the residual risk owners see); §4.8 R-BTN-88
(`closed_tickets` exported with `kept_json`); §2.3 R-BTN-149 (4 held rows per credential); §13.4
`board_head.behind_since`, `held_snapshots`, `closed_tickets`; §14 the observation and board
routes; §15 S-BTN-13, S-BTN-39, S-BTN-121, S-BTN-122, G-BTN-7, G-BTN-59, G-BTN-141, G-BTN-142.
**Depends on:** B-46 (its part 3 builds the pre-review form of R-BTN-147 and R-BTN-150: the
founded-head check, `head-replaced`, renumbering, observation renewal and voiding, the sync bar
and `observation_lapses`; this ticket changes them as below, and B-46 does not build these
changes); B-11 (`leg_attempts`, which a drop keeps); B-57 (`baton.<sub>` conditions).

### Requires

None: every case runs in the integration harness with a fake clock and constructed git
histories.

### Why

The adversarial review found that a fetch from a lagging replica could move the head backwards,
that the 24-hour auto-accept had no board to adopt and could rewind the head by clock, that a
held post replaced stations wholesale and so could drop bars, decisions and tickets, that a holder
could run a leg it never advanced through and have its suspension confirmed, and that withdrawing
a ticket in git and reopening it cleared an owner's hold and its attempts. The code still behaves
the old way: `src/core/baton.ts` replaces a held post's stations and closures, the alarm in
`src/core/claims.ts` accepts every held row at `auto_accept_at`, and `src/core/stations.ts`
deletes a returning ticket's `closed_tickets` row with nothing kept.

### What to build

1. **Migration** (one new numbered file in `src/migrations/`): `board_head.behind_since`;
   `held_snapshots.posted_by`; `closed_tickets.kept_json` and `dropped` in `closed_as`.
2. **The lagging fetch** (`src/shared/batonSync.mjs` through docs and `npm run vendor`, and
   `src/core/observations.ts`):
   - The sync's founded check follows R-BTN-147: the head is founded when it is reachable from
     `new` or `new` from it (after one fetch of the head's sha by name); only an absent or
     diverged head posts `force: true, head_unfounded: true`.
   - When `new` is a strict ancestor of the head the sync posts nothing and releases its number
     with `POST /v1/sync/observation {release, behind: new}`.
   - Baton sets `board_head.behind_since` at the first such release since the head last moved,
     and clears it when the head moves. After 30 minutes the observation response carries
     `behind_confirmed: true`, and the sync then posts `new` as `force: true` without
     `head_unfounded`, which is held.
3. **Numbers** (R-BTN-62 / R-BTN-150, `src/core/observations.ts`):
   - A post is `stale-observation` unless its number was issued to the posting credential, as
     well as greater than the last accepted one's.
   - `{release: <number>, behind?: <sha>}` voids the holder's own number; every no-post exit of
     `baton repo sync` (nothing to post, a failed trust read, a failing ticket source, any error)
     releases.
   - A number **lapses** only when it is voided for idleness or age, never posted or released;
     an immediate void by the sync key or CI identity is not a lapse. Only lapses count toward
     B-46's sync bar.
4. **Held rows** (R-BTN-11, the board route in `src/core/baton.ts`, the alarm in
   `src/core/claims.ts`):
   - A force post is held whoever posted the head when its `sha` is an accepted snapshot's
     (`board_snapshots`), and a machine key's `head_unfounded` post is held; only a sync key's or
     CI token's `head_unfounded` post over a machine key's head is accepted at once (B-46's
     `head-replaced`).
   - Each held row records `posted_by`; a fifth held row of one credential on a repository drops
     that credential's own oldest (`admin` event `held-dropped`), never another's.
   - A new held row raises `baton.held-history` on the repository (G-BTN-7).
   - **Acceptance adopts nothing by itself.** `POST /v1/ui/board/accept` and the alarm at
     `auto_accept_at` set `accepted_at` only on the repository's newest held row by observation,
     and only while it is still held; the repository's other held rows are dropped
     (`held-dropped`). The alarm never moves the head. The next force post of that sha whose
     observation is greater than the last accepted one's is accepted as a new-head post with its
     own board.
   - The observation response's `held` lists held rows' shas; an accepted post naming one in
     `contains` drops that row (`held-dropped`).
5. **A held post only tightens** (R-BTN-62, R-BTN-147, `src/core/baton.ts`,
   `src/core/stations.ts`):
   - Its positions (station, path, next leg) and markers are taken as a refresh against the
     current head when its observation is newer.
   - Its gates are applied to current rows only where they tighten: from `tickets[]` a raised
     tier, added `requires` or `depends_at`; from `stations` tags added to `bars`, added
     `decisions` and a next leg turned from `ready` to `waiting`; from `registry` a raised
     `min_runner_version`, an added `stream: false`, a lengthened `no_chain_from` or `requires`.
     Nothing is removed or loosened.
   - Its `closed` list is ignored and a ticket it omits is not dropped.
6. **Arrivals no claim ran** (R-BTN-10, R-BTN-71, R-BTN-147; `src/core/claims.ts`,
   `src/core/stations.ts`):
   - On every accepted or held post, whoever posted it, an arrival on a ticket that has had a
     claim with no `claim_legs` row of that ticket for it — other than a person's leg (R-BTN-116)
     or a Baton settlement — raises `baton.station-jump` naming the ticket, the leg and the
     posting key.
   - A suspension is confirmed only when every arrival beyond its expected path is such a person's
     leg or settlement; otherwise it stays unconfirmed (and so goes to `baton.unconfirmed` at 6
     hours).
7. **Drops and returns** (R-BTN-12, R-BTN-147, `src/core/stations.ts`, `src/core/claims.ts`):
   - An accepted post that drops, without listing it in `closed`, a ticket Baton holds state for
     raises `baton.ticket-dropped`, ends an active claim on it `released:stale-board` (G-BTN-59)
     and writes its `closed_tickets` row with `closed_as: dropped`.
   - On every closing or drop, the ticket's `ticket_overrides` row and its `leg_attempts` move
     into `closed_tickets.kept_json`. When a later post returns the same id, they are restored
     before the row is deleted: the hold stands, no attempt is refunded, and a backoff not yet over
     blocks again. The traversal record stays with the claims.
   - `kept_json` survives the 90-day purge (`src/core/history.ts`) and is exported and imported
     with `closed_tickets` (R-BTN-88).
   - The dashboard's **Courses** view (`src/ui/app.js`, its view route in `src/core/baton.ts`)
     lists every closed or dropped ticket that keeps a hold, with its reason, beside the new
     ticket (R-BTN-12, R-BTN-66's residual risk).
8. **Machine-key loosening** (schema review 13): a machine-key post that drops a course's
   `stream: false` raises `baton.machine-loosened` with the other loosenings B-46 builds.
9. **The model** (R-BTN-68): in `model/baton.qnt`, held rows accepted only at a later numbered
   post, `behind` releases, numbers bound to their credential, and suspensions not confirmed
   past a station jump; the property that the head never moves by the alarm. Attach the
   `npm run model:pr` report.

### Do not

- Let the alarm, or an administrator's acceptance, move the head; only a later-numbered post
  does (G-BTN-141).
- Store a held post's board; the next force post brings it.
- Let a held post close, drop, clear or loosen anything.
- Raise `baton.station-jump` only on machine-key posts; any poster's post counts.
- Refund attempts or lift a hold when a ticket returns; carry a hold to a ticket re-filed under a
  new id (only R-BTN-147's renumbering at the same path does).
- Build B-46's open-world settlement (the author and posted-state gates) here.
- Edit an old migration.
