**Status:** `active` — author-set.

## Why this exists

- The factory coordinates through git: `claim.sh` uses a push as its lock, a dead claim
  blocks an item for up to 7 days, eligibility is self-judged, and nobody can see which
  runners are up or what they are doing (BATON_SPEC §1).
- The owner asked for a Temporal-like view of runners, running work and queues, the power to
  switch off a provider before its credits run out, and integration tests for timeouts,
  retries, poisoned items, reaps and disabled providers.
