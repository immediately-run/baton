## What a person gets

1. **Signing in.** An owner names them an operator, once. `baton operator login` gives them a key;
   `baton operator open` or Cloudflare Access gives them the dashboard; `baton mcp operator` gives
   their agent the same key.
2. **The work.** One read lists the operator legs they could take now: unblocked except for
   needing a person. The CLI, MCP and the dashboard all show it.
3. **In their own session.** `operator_take` (MCP) or `baton operator take` (CLI) claims the
   ticket by name and prints the leg's brief. They work the leg with their agent and finish with
   `leg_done`; the runner checks the move. The next, non-operator leg goes back to agents.
4. **In a sandbox.** **Start a sandboxed session** (or `baton operator order`) places an order. The
   reply links at once to the order's page, which shows the opencode session's link as soon as a
   launcher's runner starts it. They attach and work the leg there.
