### Requires

A checkout and a terminal for most of it. Two exit criteria need forge access:
- a public GitHub repository, to exercise fork PRs and permission reads with a real token;
- a GitLab project, to read real `id_tokens` claims.

### Why

The owner, 2026-10-18, asked that the single-tenant findings of the security review be
addressed, and settled five choices: a multi-repository key opts in, exit-status verdicts,
MCP add-only holds, a loopback local host with `--bind`, and Developer as GitLab's "can
push".

### What to build

Split into reviewed PRs along four parts. Each is independent.

1. **People without push** (R-BTN-139 to R-BTN-142).
   - The fact adapters' `trusted` flag and fork detection, by ids; `trusted_identities`; the
     default station functions read trusted facts only.
   - `code_repos`.
   - The branch-pattern grammar, and branch-holder scoping.
   - Literal bars and `renamed_from` collisions, with `baton owner bars follow`.
2. **Runners** (R-BTN-143 to R-BTN-145). Server side: `default_branch`, `rewrite_held` and
   `tainted_branches` in the claim response; the `off-branch` end, the `baton.off-branch`
   condition it raises (R-BTN-163) and its sync want. In `src/runner/`:
   - the ancestry check and the verified-sha cache, fetching the registered branch, not
     `main` (`home.ts`, `main.ts`);
   - `close` and `handoff` in a child;
   - a scrubbed leg environment, with `credential:` scoping;
   - the masking set and patterns; pointer `commit` and `sha256`; `baton leg log`
     verification;
   - the `IR_BATON_LEG` tag sweep, with a systemd scope where available;
   - the replay allowlist and `BATON_RESUME`.
3. **Keys, posts and claims** (R-BTN-146 to R-BTN-152).
   - Span, the grant's `sync` flag (its only flag), `GET …/grants` and `--confirm-span`.
   - `head_unfounded`, held posts that only tighten, the `baton.<sub>` conditions those rules
     raise (`baton.span-changed`, `baton.head-replaced`, `baton.renumbered` and the others they
     name), renumbering, and dropped claims.
   - Open-world `declared` gates, `misdeclared`, and tainted branches.
   - The per-repository caps, observation takeover, and readings and bills bounds.
   - `rank` output validation.
4. **Surfaces** (R-BTN-153 to R-BTN-158).
   - Check heads as core fields, claim-bound shas, confirmed ends, `exec` verdicts.
   - MCP add-only holds and `ticket.unhold`.
   - The sign-in code flow, logout and revoke.
   - CI-token `event_name`, `workflow_ref`, `ref_protected` and `pipeline_source`; the
     GitLab protected-variable check in `repo init`; notification binding.
   - Token limits, role expansion from the snapshot, and links.
   - The App manifest checks; backticks and the export fence.
   - The local host's `--bind` and file modes.
   - Import re-validation and the pre-seal refusal.
   - The signature profile, the headers, `trigger_workflows`, foreign claims after cutover,
     and the fence per incarnation.

5. **The migration.** One new numbered migration with §13's columns and tables for these rules.
   - Columns: the columns listed there, and `worker_grants.accepted_at`, backfilled to each
     grant's creation time so existing grants stay active.
   - Tables: `tainted_branches`, `open_world_bars`, `login_codes`, `write_budgets`,
     `observation_lapses` and `action_confirmations`.
   - Migrations are append-only, so `login_links` stays, unwritten.
   - The release notes name the cost: keys already active on two or more repositories lose
     machine sync until owners set each grant's `sync` flag. `baton owner grants list` names
     them beforehand.
6. **Rounds 2 and 3's additions** (each with its scenario):
   - pending grants, with `runner.grant.accept` and `runner.grant.decline` and their routes
     and commands;
   - the board post's `source: {kind, cli}`;
   - observation renewal, the 30-minute age and the sync key's or CI's void;
   - the observation response's `trusted_identities`, effective `code_repos` and
     `tainted_branches`;
   - confirmation codes for the dashboard's authority-changing actions, with `baton admin
     confirm` (and its `dry_run`);
   - the sign-in eviction order;
   - bearer refusal by peer on an `http` listener, `--trusted-proxy`, and the Cloudflare
     host's refusal on plain `http`;
   - the tainted-branch `pre-push` hook;
   - round 4's (the hardening rules' fourth look): the settlement outcome table; `check_head_ref` in the claim
     response and a sync want on a moved head; `pending_grants` in the heartbeat and the
     runner's hint; API-key and migrated grants active at once; decline returning an active
     grant to pending, logged; a hostname binding at first acceptance; the confirmation set
     without Stop or setting a hold; clients refusing to send bearers over non-loopback `http`
     and the server revoking any that arrive.

### Architecture

- **Forge reads stay in the CLI**, never core (§1, R-BTN-50). Core learns only the `trusted` result
  through the posted facts, and the new owner settings.
- **Fail closed** wherever a forge read is refused: no trusted author, no counted PR, no
  verdict.
- **No signatures and no per-runner identities** (R-BTN-46).

### Tests

S-BTN-115 to S-BTN-125 in `test/integration/`, on both hosts, with unit tests for each
adapter's trust and fork mapping from real payload shapes.

### Exit criteria

- `npm test` and `npm run typecheck` green, with S-BTN-115 to S-BTN-125 and G-BTN-135 to
  G-BTN-145 passing.
- On a scratch public GitHub repository, an outsider's fork PR and approve comment move
  nothing.
- A real GitLab `id_tokens` JWT from a merge-request pipeline is refused.
