**Brief:** continues R3-1257 (split 2026-10-19). The owner, 2026-10-15: a leg's completion always names a (station, next leg) pair, and Baton keeps it until git confirms it.
**Spec:** `spec/BATON_SPEC.md` §5.3 R-BTN-83 (the pair, git first, the provisional queued leg, held positions and their kinds, the owner's re-route), §4.7 R-BTN-85 (`position-replaced`, `route-overridden`), §7.3 R-BTN-163 (`baton owner resolve <ticket> route:<leg>` on `baton.retries`), §8.3 R-BTN-72 (the runner suspends at the leg's `from` on a bad pair), §13.6 (`ticket_overrides.route_leg`, `route_station`, `override_kind`); §15 S-BTN-47's positions and re-route half, G-BTN-73, G-BTN-75.
**Depends on:** R3-1254. Its status lists "the holder's reported `next` (R-BTN-83)" as model work still to do. A provisional queued leg and an owner's re-route both change which leg a claim is offered, and the model's `claim` offers `st.queued` alone. Built before the model covers them, every scenario trace that ends a leg or re-routes would be rejected by `npm run traces`, and R-BTN-68 asks the code and the model to change together.

### Requires

- none — every case runs in the integration harness, plus the model change R3-1254 makes.

### What R3-1257 already delivered

- Advances and ends carry `reached`/`next`, checked against the course file's edges (`off-graph`, `not-queued`); `next_source` `course`, `marker`, `inferred` and `holder` as posted (R3-1232).
- The ticket history (R-BTN-81), discrepancy logging for the triggers that exist (R-BTN-85), the `course-changed` wait, and Baton-raised `baton:retries` and `baton:stalled` decisions with `resolve retry|park` (R3-1257). B-56 turns those decisions into the `suspend` core conditions `baton.retries` and `baton.stalled` (R-BTN-163).

### What to build

1. **The provisional queued leg.** An end or a suspension carrying `(reached, next)` stores `next` as the ticket's queued leg with `next_source: holder`, provisional. The next snapshot's posted pair confirms it (a `position-confirmed` history row) or replaces it (a `position-replaced` discrepancy with both values, then git's).
2. **The owner's re-route.** `POST /v1/owner/{repository}/tickets/{ticket}/route {leg}` and `baton owner route`: a leg leaving the ticket's station, stored in `ticket_overrides.route_leg` and `route_station` (item 3's migration). It wins over the posted pair until the station changes. The snapshot or advance that moves the ticket clears it and logs `route-overridden`. History row `re-routed`.
3. **Held positions by kind (R-BTN-83).** The new migration that adds `route_leg` and `route_station` also adds `ticket_overrides.override_kind` (`route`, `choice`, `timer`, `retry`, `cancel` or `outbox`, §13.6) and widens `ticket_stations.next_source` past `0013_stations.sql`'s four values; the queued leg an override holds carries its kind as `next_source`. This ticket writes only `route`. One write-and-clear path in `src/core/` serves every kind, so B-11 (`retry`), B-63 (`choice`), B-61 (`timer`), B-65 (`cancel`) and B-64 (`outbox`) add a kind, not a mechanism. Every held position is in the ticket's history and the owner-state export (R-BTN-88).
4. **The `route:<leg>` restart of `baton.retries`** (R-BTN-163): re-routes to a leg leaving the station and clears the hold. Whichever of this ticket and B-56 lands second wires it: before B-56, `route` with a `leg` on R3-1257's `resolve` of a `baton:retries` decision; after it, `baton owner resolve <ticket> route:<leg>`.
5. **The runner:** a leg result whose pair is not an edge suspends at the leg's `from` before Baton is called.
6. **The model:** the same in `model/baton.qnt`, with `@impl`/`@model` links.
