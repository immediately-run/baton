**Brief:** the owner, 2026-11-09 (B-191): "the per-runner page carries the 24h timeline." The
mockup's Workers page (`workers.png`: cards with ● Working, ◆ Silent 18 min, ○ Idle, ✕ Dead; the
claim block; Open harness session ↗, Stream stdout / stderr, Status, Interrupt leg…) is the list;
its Overview Timeline (one lane per runner, claims as bars, activities inside, a legend of
released, silent, reaped, activity) is the runner page's lane. "Interrupt leg…" is Stop
(R-BTN-119); "Stream stdout / stderr" is not built (R-BTN-128); the model line is not drawn
(R-BTN-3) — all in B-191's drop list.

**Spec:** [BATON_SPEC §11.4](../spec/BATON_SPEC/11-people.md#sec-11-4) (the Runners screens,
carrying the retired Runners and Timeline views; stalled is 2 hours; Stop one click),
[§6.9](../spec/BATON_SPEC/06-prerequisites-blocks-advice.md#sec-6-9) (messages: status request,
wind-down), [§8.4](../spec/BATON_SPEC/08-runners.md#sec-8-4) ([R-BTN-119](../spec/BATON_SPEC/08-runners.md#sec-8-4):
Stop), [§8.3](../spec/BATON_SPEC/08-runners.md#sec-8-3) ([R-BTN-113](../spec/BATON_SPEC/08-runners.md#sec-8-3)),
[§8.5](../spec/BATON_SPEC/08-runners.md#sec-8-5) ([R-BTN-128](../spec/BATON_SPEC/08-runners.md#sec-8-5)),
[§5.9](../spec/BATON_SPEC/05-courses.md#sec-5-9) ([R-BTN-174](../spec/BATON_SPEC/05-courses.md#sec-5-9)),
[§14](../spec/BATON_SPEC/14-api.md#sec-14) (`POST /v1/ui/messages`: `status_request`,
`wind-down`, `release`). Context: the brief — Temporal's UI as the reference for the timeline;
an interactive SVG group carries its own accessible name.

**Depends on:** B-196 (the reads), B-207 (controls and dialogs).

**Used by:** B-203.

### What to build

1. **`/repositories/{r}/runners`** (`src/dashboard/screens/runners/`): one card per row of
   `repo.runners`, grouped by hostname as R-BTN-3 says (Decision 9a; the mockup's grouping by
   state is not followed), each host's cards in state order — working, silent (alive and
   `last_activity_at` over 2 hours old), idle, dead — with the state as each card's mark: the
   name line (hostname and session name, the state mark and word, harness and kind, tier
   ceiling, "last report n ago"), capability
   chips (granted ones marked), then the claim block from `claim` — ticket linking its page,
   leg, running for, the lease bar from `claimed_at` to `lease_ends_at` with now marked and
   minutes left, attempt n of `max_runs` with `next_backoff_until`, `log_dir` as text with the
   `baton leg log` line, the current activity's kind and label — or "idle since", or for a dead
   runner "lost its heartbeat at …; its claim on … was reaped" from `reaped_at` and the
   history, as text; `busy_elsewhere` as "working on another repository"; the newest
   `last_report` payload as text with its age under the card (the reply a Status request
   produces), and `transcript_url` as a link where set. Controls by `ui.view` on
   `repository/runners/{runner}` through B-207 with context `{repository, to_runner_id,
   ticket_id}`: **Status** (`runner.status-request`), **Wind down** (`runner.wind-down`), **Stop**
   on the claim (`leg.stop`, one click, Decision 3a), and **Open session** as a plain link only
   where `session_url` is set (host shown).
2. **`/repositories/{r}/runners/{runner}`**: the card; `profile` through `View` where its
   schema has a view, else pretty-printed; the earlier `attempts`; then the **timeline** from
   `runner.show.window`: inline SVG, one lane, time left to right over `since` to now with hour
   ticks, each claim a bar coloured and labelled by `end` (running, released, silent, reaped,
   done, suspended — label first, colour second; the mockup's legend), activities nested under
   their claim by kind with failed outcomes marked by shape; each bar and segment a focusable
   `<g>` with `role="button"` and an accessible name, opening a panel with its label, detail
   (text), session link under R-BTN-113 and `log_dir` as text; `?since=` as URL query (24 h
   default, 7 d max). Under the drawing, the same claims and activities as a `<table>` for
   screen readers and phones. A pure layout helper in `src/dashboard/lib/timeline.ts`
   (time to x, nesting) is unit-tested.
3. **Phone.** Cards stack at 375 px with the controls under the claim; the timeline is the table.

### What not to do

- No log streaming; no runner channel; no message kind the registry does not list.
- No dialog on Stop (Decision 3a); no code dialog here (nothing on this screen is confirmed).
- No capability meaning; no model.

### Trust

- **Every value** is the reads' text through `Text` (hostnames, labels, detail), `profile`
  through `View`; `session_url` only as passed.
- **Status, Wind down, Stop** — registry actions through `authorize`; Stop on a ticket the
  runner does not hold is `forbidden`, drawn as text.

### Invariants

- **Capabilities are the runner's statement** (R-BTN-174); **log directories as text**
  (R-BTN-128); **session links only from `session_hosts`** (R-BTN-113).
- **Stop is one click** (R-BTN-155, Decision 3a); **text, never markup** (§12.3).
- **An owner's session, its repositories** (R-BTN-155).

### Tests

Under `test/ui/` with fixture reads: the cards group by hostname, order by state within a host
and draw the lease bar; `last_report` is drawn as text under the card; Stop sends `release` with
no dialog and draws a `forbidden` as text; an unlisted `session_url` is
text; the timeline's bars and nested segments match the fixture and the table lists the same
rows; a segment's `<g>` has an accessible name; a hostile label is text; `?since=` round-trips.

### Exit criteria

- [ ] `/runners` and `/runners/{runner}` built and usable on a local Baton at desktop and 375 px.
- [ ] The tests pass; `npm test`, `npm run typecheck`, `npm run check:wiki` and
      `npm run check:tickets` are green.
