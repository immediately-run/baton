**Brief:** the owner, 2026-11-09 (B-191): "`/attestations` — … people's signed statements."

**Spec:** [BATON_SPEC §12.8](../spec/BATON_SPEC/12-security.md#sec-12-8) ([R-BTN-181](../spec/BATON_SPEC/12-security.md#sec-12-8):
attestations, their writers, lapses, `attestation.add` and `attestation.revoke`; [R-BTN-185](../spec/BATON_SPEC/12-security.md#sec-12-8):
a session is `owner`, never `human`; [R-BTN-180](../spec/BATON_SPEC/12-security.md#sec-12-8):
domain and run label), [§11.4](../spec/BATON_SPEC/11-people.md#sec-11-4) (the `allowed` rule as
B-191 amends it; R-BTN-155: an attestation is not a confirmed action), [§5.5](../spec/BATON_SPEC/05-courses.md#sec-5-5)
([R-BTN-112](../spec/BATON_SPEC/05-courses.md#sec-5-5): a value with a view is drawn by the `ui`
renderer), [§12.3](../spec/BATON_SPEC/12-security.md#sec-12-3), [§14](../spec/BATON_SPEC/14-api.md#sec-14)
(`POST`, `DELETE …/attestations`).

**Depends on:** B-207 (controls), B-192 (sessions on the repository reads), B-205 (the cursor
convention).

**Used by:** B-203.

### What to build

The §14 row first, then the code.

1. **`GET /v1/repositories/{repository}/attestations`** (`attestation.list`, read; the repository
   reads' principals and a session — every attestation of a repository is readable by its
   readers, there is no narrower class): `attestations`, each `id`, `type`, `subject`, `value`,
   `writer` (`credential`, and `class`: owner, human, service, runner or sync), `domain` and
   `run_label` where the grant carries them, `written_at`, `lapses_at`, `revoked_at`; then
   `next_cursor` and `writable_types` (a list of types); `?subject=`
   (a ticket id or commit), `?type=`, `?live=1` (not lapsed, not revoked), `?cursor=`, 100 a
   page, newest first. `writable_types` is the list of `type`s the caller may write as `owner`
   under the policy in force, computed by `authorize` with the caller as principal (B-191's
   `allowed` rule); empty for a key or session the policy admits for none.
2. **`/repositories/{r}/attestations`** (`src/dashboard/screens/governance/Attestations.tsx`):
   the `<table>` — type, subject (a ticket id linking its page, a commit as text), writer and
   class, domain and run label, written at, lapses at — with the filters as URL query, load-more
   by cursor, lapsed and revoked rows marked by word; each row expands to its `value` through
   `View` where the type's schema has a view (R-BTN-112), else pretty-printed text. One control
   by `ui.view` on `repository/attestations` through B-207: **Add attestation**
   (`attestation.add`, writer `owner`, not confirmed), drawn only when `writable_types` is not
   empty, its form from the entry's `input` with `type` a select of `writable_types`, `subject`
   text, `value` a JSON text field validated on the server, `lapses_at` optional. Revocation is
   `baton attestation revoke <repository> <id>` as text (destructive and a credential action; a
   session does not revoke).
3. **Phone.** Cards at 375 px; the value expands in place.

### What not to do

- No policy read in the client: `writable_types` is the server's word; the control is drawn on
  it and the server decides again on the call.
- No `human` attestation from a session (R-BTN-185); no revoke from a session.
- No value rendered as markup: `View` or pretty-printed text only.

### Trust

- **`attestation.list`** — Baton's rows: `value` and `subject` are validated JSON and identifiers
  (§12.3), `writer` a fingerprint or `mcp:<id>`; drawn through `View` and `Text`.
- **`writable_types`** — computed by `authorize`; a drawing hint, not authority.
- **`attestation.add` from a session** — writer `owner` (R-BTN-181), bounded by the policy on the
  server; `invalid-params` and `forbidden` drawn as text.

### Invariants

- **A session writes as `owner`, never `human`** (R-BTN-185); **an attestation is not confirmed**
  (R-BTN-155).
- **Text, never markup** (§12.3); **an owner's session, its repositories** (R-BTN-155); **both
  hosts** (R-BTN-55).

### Tests

A scenario numbered at merge, on both hosts, with three attestations (one lapsed, one revoked,
one `human`) under two policies: `attestation.list` filters by subject, type and live, pages,
and answers `writable_types` non-empty under the admitting policy and empty under the other;
an owner's session reads it. Under `test/ui/`: Add attestation is drawn only with
`writable_types`, its type select lists them; a `human` row shows its class; a hostile subject is
text; a value with a view goes through `View`.
