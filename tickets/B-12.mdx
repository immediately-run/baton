### What to build

1. Session adapters for `claude-code` and `opencode`.
2. `baton leg yield` and `refresh` in `baton leg status`.
3. `session` mode: replace the child session; `process` mode: abort `run` with reason `context`, call it again with `ctx.refresh`; `attached`: log once.
4. Neither a run nor an attempt; at most `max_refreshes` (under the owners' ceiling), then suspend at `from`.
