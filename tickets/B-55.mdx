**Brief:** the owner, 2026-10-20: conditions bound dynamically to courses, not delivery policy in
core (`spec/BATON_SPEC_HISTORY.md`, "Decisions in full"). Filed from the ticket plan for the
current-state spec (PR #71).
**Spec:** `spec/BATON_SPEC.md` §7's limits, §7.1 R-BTN-160 and R-BTN-125; R-BTN-69 (§5.2, the
course file), R-BTN-111 (§5.5, the signature), R-BTN-33 (§1, filter fields only), R-BTN-81 (§4.7,
the history row), §2.1's write budget, §12.3 (`message` is untrusted text); §13.6 `conditions`;
§14 `POST /v1/runner/claims/{id}/conditions` and `GET /v1/repositories/{repository}/conditions`;
§15 S-BTN-130, S-BTN-134, G-BTN-146. After the adversarial review (adv-2 m1,
`spec/BATON_SPEC_HISTORY.md`): R-BTN-160's core-only types, §14's raise row (`invalid-params`),
G-BTN-146's last clause, S-BTN-131.
**Depends on:** nothing. It is better built after B-42 (the leg module
`@immediately-run/baton/leg` and its `LegError`) and B-52 (course signatures). Whichever of these
tickets lands second adds `LegError`'s type argument and the signature's conditions.

### Requires

None: a checkout and a terminal.

### Why

Every later §7 ticket raises or reads a condition: the debugger (B-56), the Needs-you conversion
(B-57), bindings (B-58), suspend (B-59), retries as the handler of `error` (B-11) and timers
(B-61). This ticket lays what they share: the declaration, the runner's check, the row and the
route. It adds no handler. Until B-58 lands, every condition is unbound.

### What to build

1. **One module, `src/core/conditions.ts`**, imported by core and by the runner
   (`src/runner/protocol.ts` already imports `src/core/graph`):
   - the type grammar `^[a-z][a-z0-9-]*(\.[a-z][a-z0-9-]*)*$`, at most 64 characters;
   - the dot hierarchy (`hitl.spec-contradiction` is a `hitl`);
   - the standard types table (`hitl` suspend, `warning` notify, `error` interrupt with core's
     five classes, `baton` core only);
   - the raise check, described in item 3.
2. **The course file** (`src/core/course.ts`'s `TOP_KEYS` and `LEG_KEYS`, and the kit in
   `src/local/testCourse.ts`):
   - Top-level `conditions: {<type>: {disposition, restarts?, default?, wait_s?, retryable?,
     description?}}`, where only `disposition` is required.
   - Restart ids use R-BTN-78's decision-id pattern, and `default` must be one of them.
   - A declared subtype of a standard type inherits its disposition unless it states one.
   - A course may not declare `baton`, `baton.*` or `hitl.decision`, which are core's alone
     (adv-2 m1); the validator names the type.
   - A leg's `conditions: [<type>…]` narrows what it may raise; `[]` allows nothing.
   - A file carrying `error_types` is refused, naming the field. It was never built, so it has no
     dual read. `max_duration` is B-60's window and B-67's refusal.
3. **Raising, in the runner** (`src/runner/runner.ts`, `src/runner/legProcess.mjs`, the leg
   module):
   - `ctx.raise(type, {subject, body?, restarts?, default?, wait_s?, severity?})`.
   - `LegError(type, message)` raises an `interrupt` type. A `LegError` naming a `prerequisite`
     stays B-21's blocked end.
   - An exception with no type is `error.transient`. A course type's class is `transient` when
     `retryable`, else `spec`.
   - `baton leg raise <type> --subject … [--body <file>] [--restarts <json>] [--default <id>]
     [--severity …]` goes on B-47's local channel, wired by whichever of the two lands second.
     `--wait` is B-59's.
   - **The check**, made before anything is reported:
     - the type is standard or declared, and the leg's narrowing allows it;
     - the type is not `baton`, `baton.*` or `hitl.decision`, which no leg may raise (adv-2 m1);
     - `restarts` are the declared ones or a subset of them (a standard type with none declared
       takes the raise's own);
     - `default` is one of them;
     - `severity` is valid.

     A raise that fails is answered on the channel with the reason and never reaches Baton.
4. **The route.** `POST /v1/runner/claims/{id}/conditions` for the holder only, in
   `src/core/workerApi.ts`, its body checked in `src/core/validate.ts` before any SQL:
   - The body is `{type, disposition, handler?, restarts, default?, severity?, url?}`.
   - A `subject`, `body` or any other field is `invalid-params` (R-BTN-33).
   - The answer is `{condition_id}`.
   - A `type` of `baton`, `baton.*` or `hitl.decision` is `invalid-params`, so a runner that
     skips its own check still cannot raise core's types (adv-2 m1).
   - The limits are 20 open conditions per claim and 200 raises per ticket per day. Past either,
     the request is refused with `quota`.
   - One transaction writes the `conditions` row, an activity of kind `condition` on the leg run
     and a `condition` history row.
5. **Migration** (a new numbered file in `src/migrations/`; never edit an old one):
   - the `conditions` table exactly as §13.6, with the open-by-repository and by-handler indexes
     and the unique open core condition on `(repository_id, type, ticket_id, ref)`;
   - `condition` added to the activity kinds and to the `ticket_history.kind` CHECK.
6. **Unbound dispositions:**
   - `interrupt`: the runner ends `released:cannot-finish:<class>` with `error: {type, message}`
     (`message` is free text under §12.3). Today's attempts path (`src/core/attempts.ts`) routes
     it, and B-11 makes the retry policy read the type.
   - `notify`: recorded and closed at once with outcome `recorded`.
   - `suspend`: refused on the channel with `suspend-unavailable` until B-59 builds the wait.
     B-59 removes this refusal.
7. **Closing**, as R-BTN-160's table says for what this ticket raises: an unbound `interrupt` and
   an unbound `notify` close when raised. The other rows are built by B-56, B-58 and B-59.
8. **Severity** (R-BTN-125) is `critical`, `high` or `normal` (default `normal`). It is stored and
   returned. It routes nothing and gates nothing.
9. **The signature** (R-BTN-111): `baton course test` prints the conditions each course's legs may
   raise. These are the course's own types plus the standard ones, unless a leg narrows them.
10. **Reading.** `GET /v1/repositories/{repository}/conditions` accepts `open`, a `type` prefix
    and `ticket`, and returns 100 a page with a cursor.
    - It may be read with a key granted on the repository, by an owner or by an administrator.
    - Its registry action is `condition.list` (`src/core/registry.ts`).
    - `person=1` is B-56's.
11. **The model** (R-BTN-68): if `model/baton.qnt` cites an end reason or a `LegError` form this
    ticket changes, change it in the same PR and attach the `npm run model:pr` report.

### Do not

- Accept or store a raise's subject or body anywhere in Baton (R-BTN-33, G-BTN-146).
- Resolve bindings, file handler tickets (B-58) or wait in the claim (B-59).
- Translate `error_types`. Nothing ever posted it.
- Put the module in `src/shared/`, which is vendored from docs (CLAUDE.md).
- Read the clock outside `this.now` (R-BTN-32).
