**Brief:** the owner asked for the runner to be stopped at once while B-150's implement leg
ran in sandbox `i0cti9vch5i0tmplzbqts`. The runner logged `stopping at once …` and, ten
seconds later, `SIGINT — the leg did not stop; its group is killed and this process exits`. The
board still showed claim 123 held by worker 32, and the sandbox was running; both were cleaned up
by hand.

**Spec:** [BATON_SPEC §8.3](../spec/BATON_SPEC/08-runners.md) (the immediate stop, B-109),
[R-BTN-107](../spec/BATON_SPEC/08-runners.md) (sessions; `stop`).

**Depends on:** none.

**Used by:** none.

### What to build

1. **The stubs** (`src/defaults/cli.ts`, the agent spawn): on `ctx.signal`'s abort, TERM the
   agent, KILL it after three seconds, and reject `the agent was stopped: <reason>` when it exits
   (not when its pipes close: a child of its own may hold them). `legProcess` reports that as
   `aborted`, and the runner writes the hand-off and ends the claim released.
2. **The hard stop** (`Runner.hardStop`, `main.ts`): kill the call, then stop the session,
   waited on for at most another ten seconds before the process exits.
3. **The spec**: §8.3's sentence says both.

A repository on a sourced course takes the stub change when its course source moves to a CLI
with it (`baton repo init`).

### Trust

No new input: the abort line already exists (R-BTN-133); the stub now acts on it.

### Invariants

- **R-BTN-24, a lost lease kills with no hand-off.** Unchanged: the lease-loss path does not
  go through the release.
- **B-109, the immediate stop is a safe point.** Strengthened: the release now reaches one.
