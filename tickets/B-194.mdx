**Brief:** the owner, 2026-11-09 (B-191): "**Tickets** `/tickets` → `/tickets/{t}` — the work
itself." The mockup's Tickets table (`tickets.txt`) — one state per row with a reason band
(Held by …, Retries exhausted, Decision open, No capable worker, Waiting on), the course
position with holder and duration, requires as chips, the tabs All, Ready, Running, Blocked,
Unconfirmed, Merged today with counts — is what these reads serve.

**Spec:** [BATON_SPEC §11.4](../spec/BATON_SPEC/11-people.md#sec-11-4) (the ticket state words
and precedence, the tab mapping, as B-191 amends them), [§5.3](../spec/BATON_SPEC/05-courses.md#sec-5-3)
([R-BTN-83](../spec/BATON_SPEC/05-courses.md#sec-5-3): a position), [§6.8](../spec/BATON_SPEC/06-prerequisites-blocks-advice.md#sec-6-8)

### What to build

The two §14 rows first, then the code, in `src/core/tickets.ts` (new; pure where it can be,
reading the rows `readyRows`, `claims.running`, `blocks.view`, `claims.needsYou`, `attempts.ts`
and `stations.ts` already produce).

1. **`GET /v1/repositories/{repository}/tickets`** (`repo.tickets`, read; the repository reads'
   principals and a session). Each live ticket: `ticket_id`, `title`, `course`, `tier`,
   `requires`, `tags`, `order`, `parent`, `position: {station, path_len, next_leg}` (R-BTN-83),
   `state` and `reasons` (§11.4: `unconfirmed` > `running` > `waiting-person` > `held` >
   `blocked` > `backing-off` > `suspended` > `ready` > `closed`; `state` is the first that
   applies, `reasons` the rest), `detail` for the state — `running: {runner, leg, since,
   lease_ends_at}`, `held: {set_by, reason, since}`, `blocked: {kind, ref, since}` (the first
   block), `backing-off: {attempt, max_runs, until}`, `waiting-person: {leg, question_id}`,
   `unconfirmed: {reported_at, runner}`, `suspended: {station, waits_for}`, `closed: {at,
   end}` — and `eligible_runners` (alive runners whose grant passes the gates, by id) for a
   `ready` ticket, the Board's "nobody can take it" being an empty list with `failed_rule`.
   Closed tickets appear only with `closed_at` in the last 24 hours. Query: `?state=` (one word
   or a tab name: `blocked` as a tab means `blocked`, `waiting-person`, `backing-off`,
   `suspended`; `closed-today`), `?q=` (a case-insensitive substring of id or title, matched
   never interpreted), `?tier=` (a ceiling: `fitsTier` as `ready_by_tier` uses it), `?cursor=`;
   order: Baton's matching order for ready tickets, then the others by state precedence and id.
   `counts: {<state>: n, total}` over the whole repository, unaffected by the filters.
2. **`GET /v1/repositories/{repository}/tickets/{ticket}`** (`ticket.show`, read; same callers):
   the ticket's row as above plus `claim` (null, or `{claim_id, runner_id, hostname,
   session_name, leg, run_id, claimed_at, lease_ends_at, deadline_at, attempt, max_runs,
   next_backoff_until, session_url, log_dir}`), `attempts` (earlier attempts on the current leg:
   `{run, runner, started_at, ended_at, end_reason, detail}`), `overrides: {expedite, hold:
   {set_by, reason, since} | null}`, `conditions` (open condition ids and types), `scope`
   (open scope findings, §12.9: `{id, kind, target, ref, at}`), `parent`, `children` (ids),
   `resume` (as `history` returns it, R-BTN-24: branches and PRs of ended claims), `web_url`
   (the ticket's page on its forge from `web_base`, R-BTN-92). `not-found` for a ticket not in
   the repository, the same as for none.

### What not to do

- No body (R-BTN-33): the title is the one text; `web_url` points at the rest.
- No state computed in a client: `state`, `reasons` and `detail` are the read's.
- No order parameter: the order is Baton's (R-BTN-61); a client never re-sorts.
- No screen here: B-209 and B-210.

### Trust

- **Both reads** — Baton's rows, answered to the repository reads' principals and a session of
  the repository; titles, hostnames, reasons, hold reasons, condition types, URIs and detail
  are untrusted text validated on write (§12.3), returned as text; `session_url` only where
  R-BTN-113 passed it; `web_url` built from `web_base` only.
- **Query parameters** — validated (`invalid-params`); `q` is matched, never interpreted; a
  cursor by B-205's rule.

### Invariants

- **Positions are Baton's** (R-BTN-39); **blocks are stored** (R-BTN-75); **filter fields only**
  (R-BTN-33).
- **One state word by one precedence** (§11.4), the same words as the vocabulary page.
- **An owner's session, its repositories** (R-BTN-155); **both hosts** (R-BTN-55).

### Tests

Scenarios numbered at merge, on both hosts, with a fixture repository holding a ready, a
running, a held-and-blocked, a backing-off, an unconfirmed, a suspended, a person-waiting and a
closed-today ticket:

- `repo.tickets` gives each its state by precedence (the held-and-blocked one is `held` with
  `reasons: ["blocked"]`) and its detail; `?state=blocked` returns the tab's four states;
  `?q=` and `?tier=` filter; `counts` ignore the filters; pages chain; the ready ticket names
  its eligible runners and a ticket nobody can take names the failed rule;
- `ticket.show` carries the claim, attempts, overrides, conditions, scope findings, children
  and resume; a foreign id is `not-found`.
