**Spec:** BATON_SPEC §10.7 — R-BTN-136 to R-BTN-155 (new) and the rules they amend;
S-BTN-110 to S-BTN-120; G-BTN-130 to G-BTN-140.
**Depends on:** B-34 for the origin rules R-BTN-155 cites. Check courses (B-5), streams and
Stop (B-9) and MCP (R-BTN-130's ticket) take their parts of §10.7 wherever they are built
first; this item builds the rest and their tests.

### Requires

A checkout and a terminal for most of it. Two exit criteria need forge access:
- a public GitHub repository, to exercise fork PRs and permission reads with a real token;
- a GitLab project, to read real `id_tokens` claims.

### Why

The owner, 2026-10-18, asked that the single-tenant findings of the security review be
addressed, and settled five choices: a multi-repository key opts in, exit-status verdicts,
MCP add-only holds, a loopback local host with `--bind`, and Developer as GitLab's "can
push".

### What to build

Split into reviewed PRs along §10.7's four parts. Each is independent.

1. **People without push** (R-BTN-136 to R-BTN-139).
   - The fact adapters' `trusted` flag and fork detection, by ids; `trusted_identities`; the
     default station functions read trusted facts only.
   - `code_repos`.
   - The branch-pattern grammar, and branch-holder scoping.
   - Literal bars and `renamed_from` collisions, with `baton owner bars follow`.
2. **Runners** (R-BTN-140 to R-BTN-142). In `src/runner/`:
   - the ancestry check and the verified-sha cache, fetching the registered branch, not
     `main` (`home.ts`, `main.ts`);
   - `close` and `handoff` in a child;
   - a scrubbed leg environment, with `credential:` scoping;
   - the masking set and patterns; pointer `commit` and `sha256`; `baton leg log`
     verification;
   - the `IR_BATON_LEG` tag sweep, with a systemd scope where available;
   - the replay allowlist and `BATON_RESUME`.
3. **Keys, posts and claims** (R-BTN-143 to R-BTN-149).
   - Span, the grant flags, `GET …/grants` and `--confirm-span`.
   - `head_absent`, held posts that only tighten, the new Needs-you items, renumbering, and
     dropped claims.
   - Open-world `declared` gates, `misdeclared`, and tainted branches.
   - The per-repository caps, observation takeover, and readings and bills bounds.
   - `rank` output validation.
4. **Surfaces** (R-BTN-150 to R-BTN-155 and §10.7.5).
   - Check heads as core fields, claim-bound shas, confirmed ends, `exec` verdicts.
   - MCP add-only holds and `ticket.unhold`.
   - The sign-in code flow, logout and revoke.
   - CI-token `event_name`, `workflow_ref`, `ref_protected` and `pipeline_source`; the
     GitLab protected-variable check in `repo init`; notification binding.
   - Token limits, role expansion from the snapshot, and links.
   - The App manifest checks; backticks and the export fence.
   - The local host's `--bind` and file modes.
   - Import re-validation and the pre-seal refusal.
   - The signature profile, the headers, `trigger_workflows`, foreign claims after cutover,
     the fence per incarnation, and courier `asks`.

### Architecture

- **Forge reads stay in the CLI**, never core (§2b). Core learns only the `trusted` result
  through the posted facts, and the new owner settings.
- **Fail closed** wherever a forge read is refused: no trusted author, no counted PR, no
  verdict.
- **No signatures and no per-worker identities** (R-BTN-46).

### Tests

S-BTN-110 to S-BTN-120 in `test/integration/`, on both hosts, with unit tests for each
adapter's trust and fork mapping from real payload shapes.

### Exit criteria

- `npm test` and `npm run typecheck` green, with S-BTN-110 to S-BTN-120 and G-BTN-130 to
  G-BTN-140 passing.
- On a scratch public GitHub repository, an outsider's fork PR and approve comment move
  nothing.
- A real GitLab `id_tokens` JWT from a merge-request pipeline is refused.
