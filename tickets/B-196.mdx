**Brief:** the owner, 2026-11-09 (B-191): "**Runners** `/runners` → `/runners/{name}` — the fleet
granted here: alive/dead, claim held, current activity; the per-runner page carries the 24h
timeline." B-191 keys the URL by the runner's id, since a host runs several sessions and
`/v1/ui/messages` addresses `to_runner_id`.

**Spec:** [BATON_SPEC §8.1](../spec/BATON_SPEC/08-runners.md#sec-8-1) ([R-BTN-3](../spec/BATON_SPEC/08-runners.md#sec-8-1):
a runner's fields — no model among them; [R-BTN-9](../spec/BATON_SPEC/08-runners.md#sec-8-1): the
lease timeout), [§8.2](../spec/BATON_SPEC/08-runners.md#sec-8-2) ([R-BTN-146](../spec/BATON_SPEC/08-runners.md#sec-8-2):
accepted grants), [§5.9](../spec/BATON_SPEC/05-courses.md#sec-5-9) ([R-BTN-174](../spec/BATON_SPEC/05-courses.md#sec-5-9):
declared and granted capabilities), [§6.7](../spec/BATON_SPEC/06-prerequisites-blocks-advice.md#sec-6-7)
([R-BTN-99](../spec/BATON_SPEC/06-prerequisites-blocks-advice.md#sec-6-7): the fleet view a machine
key reads), [§9.9](../spec/BATON_SPEC/09-claims.md#sec-9-9) (activities and transcripts),
[§8.3](../spec/BATON_SPEC/08-runners.md#sec-8-3) ([R-BTN-113](../spec/BATON_SPEC/08-runners.md#sec-8-3)),
[§8.5](../spec/BATON_SPEC/08-runners.md#sec-8-5) ([R-BTN-128](../spec/BATON_SPEC/08-runners.md#sec-8-5)),

**Depends on:** B-192 (sessions read the repository reads), B-205 (the response convention; the

**Used by:** B-211.

### What to build

The two §14 rows first, then the code, reusing `workerApi.ts`'s `workersView` and
`claims.ts`'s `running` and `timeline` queries.

1. **`GET /v1/repositories/{repository}/runners`** (`repo.runners`, read; the repository reads'
   principals and a session): every runner row Baton holds whose grant on the repository is
   accepted (R-BTN-146) — Baton prunes no runner row, so a dead one stays until its key is
   revoked — each `{runner_id, hostname, session_name, alive, last_seen_at, last_activity_at,
   reaped_at, harness, kind, isolation, tier_ceilings, capabilities, granted, tags, courses,
   lease_timeout_s, credential: {kind, token_id?, role, domain?}, claim, busy_elsewhere}`:
   `alive` is `reaped_at` null and `last_seen_at` within `lease_timeout` (as `workersView`
   computes it); `granted` the repository's grant capabilities (R-BTN-174); `courses` the
   runner's course versions for this repository; `claim` null or this repository's claim it
   holds — `{claim_id, ticket_id, station, leg, run_id, claimed_at, lease_ends_at,
   deadline_at, attempt, max_runs, next_backoff_until, session_url, log_dir, activity: {kind,
   label, started_at} | null}`; `busy_elsewhere` true when it holds a claim on another
   repository, with no detail. Ordered alive first, then by `last_seen_at` descending. The
   `profile` blob is not in the list (it is on `runner.show`).
2. **`GET /v1/repositories/{repository}/runners/{runner}`** (`runner.show`, read; same callers):
   the row above plus `profile` (the opaque blob, §12.3), `attempts` (earlier attempts on its
   live ticket's current leg: run, started, ended, end reason, detail), and `window: {since,
   claims: [{claim_id, ticket_id, leg, started_at, ended_at, end: released | silent | reaped |
   done | suspended | running, session_url, log_dir, activities: [{id, parent_id, kind, label,
   source, started_at, ended_at, outcome, detail}]}]}` for `?since=` (default 24 hours, at most
   7 days, else `invalid-params`), from `claims.timeline`. `{runner}` is the integer `runner_id`;
   a runner with no accepted grant on the repository is `not-found`, the same as none.

### What not to do

- No tenant-wide fleet: an owner's session sees the runners granted on its repository; a claim
  elsewhere is `busy_elsewhere` and nothing more (R-BTN-155).
- No model: R-BTN-3 names none; the mockup's "claude-opus-4.1" is not a field (B-191's drop list).
- No capability meaning: names, matched by name (R-BTN-174).
- No screen here: B-211.

### Trust

- **Both reads** — Baton's rows; hostnames, session names, labels, detail, harness and
  capability names are a runner's own statements (R-BTN-3, §12.3), validated on the heartbeat,
  returned as text; `profile` an opaque blob; `session_url` only where R-BTN-113 passed it.
- **`?since=`** — validated (`invalid-params` over 7 days); the integer `runner_id` validated.

### Invariants

- **Capabilities are the runner's statement** (R-BTN-174).
- **An owner's session, its repositories** (R-BTN-155); **readable with a key** (R-BTN-179).
- **Filter fields only** (R-BTN-33); **both hosts** (R-BTN-55).

### Tests

Scenarios numbered at merge, on both hosts, with two granted runners (one holding a claim with
two activities, one dead after a reap), one granted elsewhere and one busy elsewhere:

- `repo.runners` lists the two granted with `alive`, `granted`, the claim's lease and activity,
  the dead one's `reaped_at`; the third is absent; the busy one is `busy_elsewhere` with no
  claim;
- `runner.show` returns the window's claims with nested activities, `profile` and `attempts`;
  `?since=` over 7 days is `invalid-params`; a runner not granted here is `not-found`.
