**Brief:** the owner, 2026-11-01, on the whole-spec review they commissioned ("assess it for …
can any vocabulary, core concepts or actors be simplified … is the user experience intuitive …
are the security guarantees … truly guaranteed and … do they matter"), asked for its findings
to be filed as tickets. Source: `docs/review/spec-simplification-review-2026-11-01.mdx`.
The review's claims are a reviewer's, checked against the spec where they cite it; this ticket
verifies each against the text before changing anything.

### Why

Review F2, F5, F6 and the guarantee table (section 3.1).

### Decided (the owner)

**Rulings of 2026-11-01** (the owner, answering each question in the session that continued
immediately-run/baton#150; the restructure, B-166, writes them into the spec):

- **Human gates:** ~~"Only protected-signed": `protected` stops claiming `human-gates`.~~ Reversed by the owner on
  2026-11-24 and 2026-11-26: `human-gates` keeps a variant without hardware keys, which `protected` claims;
  `human-gates-hardware` is the hardware one (R-BTN-189).
- **Signing:** "Remove until designed": `signed-authorship` and `authorship: signed` leave the
  spec until a host-side signer is designed; continuity ranges stay.

### What to build

1. **A spec PR first**, under the babysit skill's *Spec PRs*: two fresh reviewers, the impact list,
   every rule restating the same fact changed with it.
2. ~~`human-gates` held only by `protected-signed`, or `human_key: hardware` part of
   `protected`.~~ Done otherwise by the 2026-11-26 amendment: `protected` holds `human-gates`, and
   `human-gates-hardware` needs `human_key: hardware` and a verified key.
3. `signed-authorship` either specified with a host-side signer or removed until one is
   designed; continuity ranges kept.
4. `independent-review`'s machinery cut to what it needs: a review count ~~and a domain label~~ (each review its own `reads_only` run of the exact head).
