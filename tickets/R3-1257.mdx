**Brief:** the Baton project; the owner, 2026-10-15: legs connect stations, a leg's
completion always names a (station, next leg) pair, Baton keeps a running record and pointers
to each leg's inputs and outputs, schemas are optional and checked by the worker, and any leg
can be replayed.
**Spec:** `docs/content/specs/BATON_SPEC.mdx` §2h (R-BTN-81, -82, -83, -84), R-BTN-33 as
amended, R-BTN-78 (Baton-raised decisions), R-BTN-69 (`to` lists, `start_leg`, contracts),
R-BTN-63, -70, -71 (positions in advances, paths and ends), §6.10 (`cannot-finish:contract-input` and `-output`),
§6.13 (the runner's result, `ctx.inputs`, a leg's `close`), §7.1 (the board post), §7.2 rule 1, §4,
§10.4 (URIs and markers), §11, §14; §13a S-BTN-47, -48; §16 G-BTN-73, -74, -75 (docs#214)
**Depends on:** R3-1251 (blocks and decisions, which Baton-raised decisions extend)

### Requires

- none — every case runs in the integration harness with fixture course files, boards and
  stub leg scripts, and a local git server for `git:` inputs.

### Why

A ticket's next queue must not depend on which course file is in force when someone looks:
the leg that finished decides it, in git. Legs that read each other's outputs need those
outputs named, typed and found again; failures should name the producer; a past run should be
re-runnable. Tier: critical — positions and the queued leg feed rule 1 and every claim.

### What to build

1. **Migration**: `ticket_stations.next_leg`, `next_source`; `claim_legs.to_station` nullable,
   `next_leg` and `head_sha`; `ticket_history.run`, `snapshot_sha`, `head_sha`;
   `closed_tickets.purged_at` and `closed_unpurged`; `run_inputs_by_ticket`; the events CHECK's
   new kinds; `hold_reason` `parked`; `ticket_next_legs.waiting_since`; `ticket_overrides.route_leg`,
   `route_station`; `ticket_decisions.source`; `repositories.artifact_buckets`; `leg_outputs`,
   `leg_run_inputs`, `ticket_history` and its index; `notices.payload_json` — as §4 gives them.
2. **Course file**: `to` as a station or a list; `start_leg` (required); leg `version`,
   `inputs`, `outputs`, `replay_safe`; the acceptance checks of R-BTN-69, through
   `src/core/validate.ts`.
3. **Positions** (R-BTN-83): `advance {reached, leg, outputs?}`, ends with `reached`/`next`;
   the provisional queued leg, confirmed or replaced by the snapshot's posted pair; `inferred`
   and `course` sources; `course-changed` stored by Baton on course-file and snapshot
   acceptance; `not-queued` on a first advance into another leg; owner `route` and its clearing.
4. **Rule 1 and its SQL** on the queued leg; claim and recommendation `legs` of one.
5. **History** (R-BTN-81): the one writer `recordHistory(tx, …)`, called from every transaction
   that changes what it records; `GET …/history`; the alarm's 90-day deletion; hook input's
   last 10 rows.
6. **Contracts** (R-BTN-82): URI validation (§10.4, including the repository allowlist and the
   marker encoding); input resolution at the advance; `input-missing`/`input-version` stored as
   `waiting` before a leg is offered; foreign-marker outputs never resolved.
7. **Baton-raised decisions** (R-BTN-78): `baton:retries`, `baton:contract`, `baton:stalled`,
   the reserved prefix, `POST …/resolve`.
8. **Discrepancies** (R-BTN-85): a `discrepancy` event and history row, with both values, in
   every transaction where a snapshot overrides Baton's value; the dashboard's Discrepancies list.
9. **CLI** (baton repository): `baton runner start` validates each result's position and schemas, calls the
   leg's `close` to write the closing marker from it (failing the leg
   `cannot-finish:contract-input|-output` instead), provides `ctx.inputs`;
   `baton leg replay [--against]` with credentials withheld; `baton owner route|resolve`;
   `baton course test`'s dataflow and version checks.

### Architecture — the decisions, named

- **Git first**: no position or pointer is reported before the leg's `close` writes it to its
  closing marker; Baton's copy is provisional until `locate` confirms it.
- **Pointers, never content**: Baton validates and stores URIs and digests and never fetches.
- **Do not**: infer `next` from a station's one leg out; let a foreign marker's outputs reach a
  leg; refuse anything for a Baton-raised decision that advice would not.
