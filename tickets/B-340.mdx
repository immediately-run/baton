**Brief:** the owner, 2026-11-23: "I think a human using the dashboard should be considered a
human." Asked which sessions, the owner chose every person's session however it was opened, and,
under `human_key: hardware`, "only with a per-act key touch".

**Spec:** [BATON_SPEC §12.8](../spec/BATON_SPEC/12-security.md#sec-12-8) ([R-BTN-185](../spec/BATON_SPEC/12-security.md#sec-12-8),
[R-BTN-181](../spec/BATON_SPEC/12-security.md#sec-12-8): the `human` writer,
[R-BTN-188](../spec/BATON_SPEC/12-security.md#sec-12-8): the `human` approver entry),
[§11.4](../spec/BATON_SPEC/11-people.md#sec-11-4) ([R-BTN-155](../spec/BATON_SPEC/11-people.md#sec-11-4)),
[§12.1](../spec/BATON_SPEC/12-security.md#sec-12-1), [§13](../spec/BATON_SPEC/13-data-model.md#sec-13).

**Depends on:** B-76 (owners' and members' sessions), B-195 (a session's path onto `POST
…/attestations`).

### What to build

1. **Writers.** A session's attestation carries writer classes `human` and its key's role; the
   policy check admits it when its entry names either (`src/core/` attestation path). `GET
   …/attestations` shows both.
2. **Approvers.** R-BTN-188's `human` entry matches a session whose role may take the action;
   the approver check is the one `authorize` uses for keys.
3. **The touch under `hardware`** (R-BTN-185's *Under `hardware`*).
   - **Registration.** `baton human-key webauthn add --for <fingerprint>` sends `POST
     /v1/human-keys {webauthn: {for_key, credential_id, public_key, attestation}}` signed by a
     human-only key that owns the repository (or a break-glass line) and meets its `human_key`;
     the attestation must meet what `hardware` requires (B-136). Stored in
     `webauthn_credentials` (§13.2); suspended and revoked with its signing key.
   - **Challenge.** The page asks `POST /v1/ui/human-challenge {action, params}` and gets a
     challenge bound to the session, the action and the canonical parameters' hash, used once,
     two minutes (`human_challenges`).
   - **Assertion.** The act carries `webauthn: {credential_id, authenticator_data,
     client_data_json, signature}`; Baton checks the challenge is the one issued for this
     session, act and parameters and unused, the RP ID hash against the tenant's host, the
     origin against `BATON_ORIGIN`, the type, user presence, the signature with WebCrypto on
     both hosts, and that the credential is registered for the session's key. Anything else:
     `forbidden`.
   - **Untouched writes.** A session's attestation records `touched`; an untouched one counts as
     `human` only while the repository's `human_key` is `any`.
4. **The conditions read** (`answerWord` in `src/core/reads/conditions.ts`) says a session may
   answer where the policy admits `human`, and names the touch where `hardware` asks for it.

### Trust

- **A WebAuthn credential** — registered only with a human-only key's signature over it; stored
  as its public key and id; validated in `src/core/validate.ts`.
- **An assertion** — verified against a registered credential, its challenge bound to the
  action and parameters, user presence required; never stored beyond its event.
- **A session's `human`** — the session cookie and `Baton-Session` under R-BTN-16's checks; no
  new input.

### Invariants

- **R-BTN-185**: a key outside a session is `human` only when `human_only`; a session gains no
  action its role lacks by being `human`.
- **~~`human-gates`~~ `human-gates-hardware`** (R-BTN-189): under `hardware` every human approval is made on a hardware key. `human-gates` rests on custody and holds under `any` (2026-11-26).
- **R-BTN-186**: the session-only touch is the dashboard's way to carry the human; the CLI keeps
  the human-only key's signature.
- **Both hosts** (R-BTN-55); migrations append-only.
