### What is decided, and what is not

Decided by the owner (the proposals, *The owner's decisions*):

- **A person is for accountability and notification, never authority.** Roles stay on keys. Baton keeps no groups.
- **Who may answer** a person leg is the keys holding its labels (R-BTN-204's members), or an owner. A label is the nearest thing to "group Z": it says who may answer, not who is told.
- **Who is told** is the ticket's assignee, resolved to a person through verified linked identities. "An entry with no one to notify is shown, not sent." A team is told by pulling Needs you, or by one notification to a channel the team already shares.
- **Preferences** are a person's own: which events go to which channels, and quiet hours.

Not decided, and to be put to the owner as one question in this ticket's PR: **how a person leg waiting on a label reaches anyone, when its ticket has no assignee**. The proposals' answer today is "nobody is told; it is shown on Needs you". Two alternatives the PR should lay out:

- (a) keep that answer;
- (b) an arena maps a label to one shared channel (a team's Slack channel or ntfy topic), notified once per entry. This is not a group: no membership is kept, and it confers no authority.

### What to do

This is a spec ticket: its PR changes the spec and files tickets, and builds nothing.

1. Move people §5 and the personal-secrets proposal into BATON_SPEC: each rule into the section file that owns it, following each proposal's *Amends* list, with `npm run spec:index`. Their hardening items stay on `spec/proposals/hardening.md`.
2. Put the open question above to the owner as a decision packet in the PR, and write the chosen answer into the spec.
3. File the build tickets the moved rules need, at least: people and linked identities with forge sign-in; the roster (`std:roster@1`); channels, preferences and verification; the notifier and `std:notified@1`; the CLI `baton notify …` commands. Each names the rules it builds and its scenarios.
