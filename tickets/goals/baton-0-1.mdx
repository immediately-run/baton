and its tickets are steered by their `order` (below)._

without interventions for 3 days such that: 1. It is working on the correct issues (most
important first) 2. It follow the course as expected 3. It operates at full capacity. That is in
order of importance, most important first." The plan is
[Baton 0.1](../../docs/plans/baton-0-1.mdx).
