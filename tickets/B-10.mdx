### Why

§2m is agreed and specified; this ticket builds its part of it.

### What to build

1. Per-leg `ticket` and `capability` rows with `@at`, `@reached` and `@done` refs, rewritten in the transaction that changes a referenced ticket; `closed_tickets.final_station`, `final_path` and `parent` read for closed tickets.
2. `depends_at` on the posted ticket; cycles listed `prerequisite-cycle`, blocks kept.
3. `GET /v1/repositories/{repository}/tickets/{ticket}/prerequisites` and `baton why` from the blocks index alone.
4. Capability types and roles validated with the course file; runners re-run checks while idle; `ROLES` leaves the vendored library through the transition window.

### Do not

- Read past the blocks index to answer `baton why`.
