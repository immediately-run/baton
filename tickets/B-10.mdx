**Brief:** the owner, 2026-10-16 and 2026-10-17, as specified by B-1 (baton#43).
**Spec:** `spec/BATON_SPEC.md` §6.1 (R-BTN-120), §5.6 (R-BTN-121), §6.2 (R-BTN-75's `ticket` and
`capability` rows), §6.6 rules 1 and 4, §13.6's `ticket_blocks`; history, Migrations: the `ROLES`
window.

### Requires

None: a checkout and a terminal.

### Why

R-BTN-120 gives each kind of prerequisite one way in and answers "why not" from one index;
R-BTN-121 moves capability types out of the vendored vocabulary into the course file. This
ticket builds both.

### What to build

1. **Ticket entries.** `validateCourse` (`src/core/course.ts`) accepts a leg's
   `requires.tickets` with `select` `parent` (optional `course`) or `posted`, and refuses any
   other `select` (no `children`, no `run`: children are a call's range, B-62). Each entry has
   exactly one of `at`, `reached`, `done: true`, `withdrawn: true` or `closed: true`, plus
   optional `skip_withdrawn` and `allow_empty`. `baton course test` checks each named station
   exists in the selectable course.
2. **Rows.** Per-leg `ticket` and `capability` rows in `ticket_blocks` (`src/core/blocks.ts`)
   with refs `@at:`, `@reached:`, `@done`, `@withdrawn` and `@closed`, rewritten in the
   transaction that changes a referenced ticket (index `ticket_dependents`);
   `closed_tickets.final_station`, `final_path` and `parent` read for closed tickets. A
   withdrawn ticket meets only `withdrawn` and `closed`.
3. **`depends_at`** on the posted ticket (`src/core/board.ts`), with `leg` binding one leg and
   no `leg` the whole ticket.
4. **Fail closed.** A `parent` entry with no parent, an unknown `posted` id, or an entry a
   withdrawn ticket leaves unmet keeps its block and raises `baton.prerequisite-unmet`; a cycle
   in an accepted snapshot keeps every block and raises `baton.prerequisite-cycle` naming the
   tickets (through today's listing if B-57 has not landed).
5. **Why not.** `GET /v1/repositories/{repository}/tickets/{ticket}/prerequisites[?leg=][&runner=me]`
   and `baton leg why` from the blocks index alone, every prerequisite met or not, with R-BTN-120's
   `why` table. The `condition` and `call` rows are B-59's and B-62's, and show here when they
   land.
6. **Capability types and roles** (`capability_types`, `capability_roles`) validated with the
   course file; a course may not declare a type named `runner` or `human-operator`; grants may
   name `<type>:*`, never a role; runners re-run `check` commands while idle. Through the `ROLES`
   window a course without `capability_types` reads through the vendored `ROLES`; `ROLES` is
   removed once no registered course reads through it.

### Do not

- Read past the blocks index to answer `baton leg why`.
- Accept a `children` or `run` selector, or select a call's children from `requires.tickets`.
