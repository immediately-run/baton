Reviewed [Baton #311](https://github.com/immediately-run/baton/pull/311) at `ba1655cd`
([review](https://github.com/immediately-run/baton/pull/311#pullrequestreview-6004812930)). The
verdict was changes: the reviewer ran typecheck, the model links and 15 focused tests on both
hosts, and found that sync producers did not share the persisted dispatch window, several wants
were never recorded, and polling, import and local secrets broke their stated invariants.

The PR was merged on 2026-11-26. Every finding of this round was fixed in `e4a8b9d5`.
