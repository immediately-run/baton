## Rollout hold

The landed Fieldhouse R-FH-3, R-FH-11 and R-FH-90 prescribe the limited user's token in stage 1
and a host-only forge gate for stage 2 (FLDH-60 and FLDH-61). Fieldhouse #49 proposes a different
stage-1 credential. Do not deploy the broker on the strength of this fix. The open owner decision
is whether to approve that interim exception or continue only with the specified gate. No live
credentials, host configuration or deployment were used or changed in this leg.
