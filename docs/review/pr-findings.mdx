defect found on `main` that the gate should have caught.</Lede>

review-gate log and moved here with Baton's tickets; new rows are appended here.

The columns:

- **date**, **repo** and **PR**: when, and which pull request (`baton` is this repository).
- **rubric**: the implementation standard the finding cites (`R1`, `R2`, …), or the
  review round's finding id.
- **severity**: `blocking`, `should-fix` or `nit`.
- **finding**: what was wrong. `(A)`, `(B)` or `(AB)` names the reviewer or reviewers who
  raised it.
- **disposition**: `fixed` (with where), `argued` (answered and left), `escalated` (the ticket
  went back to in-progress) or `escaped` (found on `main` after merge).
- **origin**, **missed-by** and **introduced**: where the defect came from (`impl`, the
  implementation; `spec-silent` or `spec-wrong`, the spec said nothing or the wrong thing;
  `item-silent` or `item-wrong`, likewise the ticket), which check should have caught it, and
  the change that introduced it.
