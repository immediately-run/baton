---
title: Adding a delivery channel
description: Deliver Needs you entries from a repository-owned process without extending Baton's legacy notice queue.
nav: Delivery channels
tags: [runbook]
---

Use **Needs you** as the source. For a new channel such as email or chat, run a
delivery process outside Baton, under the repository's control:

1. Poll `GET /v1/repositories/{repository}/needs-you` with a credential that
   already has read access to that repository. Each response includes open
   conditions in `conditions` and queued person legs in `questions`, with no
   pagination. See the [API contract](../../spec/BATON_SPEC/14-api.md) and
   [S-BTN-276](../../spec/BATON_SPEC/15-verification.md#scenarios).
2. Pick the entries the channel should deliver. Post a short summary and a link
   back to Baton, the ticket or the joinable session, as appropriate. Keep the
   channel's credentials, retry state and deduplication outside Baton. Use the
   condition ID or the queued question's identity to avoid resending on every poll.
3. Leave answers and resolutions on their existing paths. Sending a message does
   not close a condition or answer a person's leg.

Do not add notice kinds, courier registrations or heartbeat courier declarations
for new channels. The legacy notice and courier paths stay in place for existing
callers until [B-67](../../tickets/B-67.mdx) finishes the migration. Handler courses
and the outbox are [proposed, not built](../../spec/proposals/conditions.md).
